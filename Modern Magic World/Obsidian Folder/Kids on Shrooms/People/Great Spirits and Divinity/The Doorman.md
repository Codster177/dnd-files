## Backstory:
The Doorman is a high level and very dangerous spirit who is controlled by the [[Scholars of Avira]]. His job is exactly this: finding people using [[Magic]] outside of the Scholars' territory.

He travels in an extradimensional space within [[The Kamihara]], and enters anywhere in the physical world through doors. Any door can be his door, so long as his taboo is satisfied: he can only come through a door that he has been invited through.

He has powers that make the invitations come easily. The Doorman can fully morph his face and voice, but not his body, into anyone he knows of, and he gets ideas of who the last people to touch a given door were.

It was [[Ian Long (Aaron)]] accessing the internet the way he did that put the party on the Doorman's route; the Scholars' network flags any magic user interfacing with the internet who is not one of their own. The night of [[Session 5]], the Doorman knocked on [[Caspian Forrest (Kai)|Caspian]]'s door, guised his voice as Caspian's mom, and was invited through into Caspian's room.
