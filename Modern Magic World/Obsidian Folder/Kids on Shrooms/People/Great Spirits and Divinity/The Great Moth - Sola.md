## Themes:
Sola is a child of [[The Goddess]], and together with her sibling [[The Space Spirit - Aurorlin]] she dedicates her existence to helping her mother defend [[Avira]] from [[The Great Horrors]] of the universe, akin to how Helios pulled the sun and moon.

The horrors are celestial, eldritch beings that feed off of life and [[Magic]], and witnessing one would cause you to go mad. So the energy and effort the nearest horror constantly spends attacking [[Avira]] gets repurposed by Sola into something that actively helps the life on the world: the sun itself.
