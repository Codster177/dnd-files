## Character Breakdown:
How old is your character? 
The Great Whale is as old as the world, since the Goddess touched and created [[The Kamihara]].

What is your appearance like? 
A whale that is half of the size of a city. It is similar to a blue whale, but much longer and with many strange features. It has many small fins along it's body, two sets of large fins, 7 blowholes that are about 8 feet in diameter, and one large blowhole about 25 feet in diameter. It also has two large "horns" that are shaped like the rams of a ship.

## Backstory:
The Great Whale is a godlike spirit, one of the few powerful enough to ignore the blockades around [[The Kamihara]] entirely.

The Whale is a spirit of good, and its latest act of faith is a warlock. It selected [[Marlowe Melville (Rylee)]] after looking into her soul and psyche, and chose her with the hope and intention of Marlowe being the Great Whale's change in the world.
