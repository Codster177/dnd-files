Sadie Marsh (24) and Erin Kowalski (27) are the young couple who lived in the apartment next to the Melvilles', one wall over from [[Marlowe Melville (Rylee)|Marlowe]]'s bedroom. They got engaged last Second Autumn and were saving for the wedding.

On the night of the [[Astaria#The HUMI|HUMI]] raid in [[Session 6]], the strike team's sweep of the block found an enchanted Neridoran prayer lamp hidden above the ceiling panel of their patio storage closet. The lamp was planted there by [[Vincenzo (Vince) Melville]] as a decoy years ago; the couple never knew it existed. The HUMI confiscated the lamp, logged it as the source of the incident, and took Sadie and Erin into custody. They have not come home.

## Character Breakdown:
How old is your character? 
Sadie is 24. Erin is 27.

What is your appearance like? 
Sadie is small with dark curly hair she keeps tied back, usually in scrubs from the animal clinic, always with a pen shoved behind one ear. Erin is a head taller, freckled, sunburnt from working outside, and lives in her Parks Department polo even on weekends.

What is your personality like?
Erin does the talking. She is loud, warm, adopts every new tenant within a week, and ran the building's summer cookout two years straight. Sadie is the quiet one with the dry humor, the kind of person who says four words an hour and two of them are the funniest thing anyone said all day. She remembers the name of every pet in the building and none of the owners.

What kind of cliques or clichés would your character be a part of? 
The nice couple in the next apartment that everyone in the building actually likes.

What are your character's goals? 
Before the raid: pay for a wedding by next Second Summer, and get Sadie through her certification so the clinic will finally pay her what she is worth. Now: figure out what a lamp is, and go home.

What are your character's fears?
Erin used to fear rent hikes. Sadie used to fear the dogs she can't save. Both of those have been replaced by a windowless room and polite people in white who keep asking the same questions.

## Backstory:
Erin grew up in [[Lake Prospect]] and works for the city Parks Department; the wilted, blackened lawn around her own block is, by cruel coincidence, exactly the kind of thing her crew would be sent to dig up and re-sod. Sadie moved to town three years ago for a vet tech job at the animal clinic. They met when Erin brought in a one-eyed stray she refused to leave in a drainage ditch, and Sadie named it after her before she ever asked her out.

They were good to the Melvilles. Erin brought over chili whenever she cooked too much, which was always, and Sadie fed the building's strays with day-old treats from the clinic. When Erin's pickup died, [[Vincenzo (Vince) Melville|Vince]] fixed it for free, and later her water heater too. That is when he planted the lamp. He chose them precisely because they were clean, kind, and certain to read innocent under any interrogation; their decency was the load-bearing part of his plan.

The night of the raid they were taken quietly, without arrest paperwork, sirens, or anything the neighbors could point to. Their mail is piling up, Erin's pickup sits in its usual spot, and their apartment windows have been dark since Wednesday.

In custody they are exactly what they appear to be: two frightened, mundane young women who have never seen the object they are being questioned about. The likely ending is a memory modification and a quiet release with a cover story of an impulsive coast trip, because the HUMI has no use for them once the interrogators get bored. But the process is not gentle, nobody is in a hurry, and the case file with their names on it now exists forever.

Nobody who matters knows they are innocent except Vince, and Vince is not telling.
