## Backstory:
Lexi has been friends with [[Rachel De Narris]] since middle school, one third of the old trio with [[Milani Toll]]. She was also decent friends with [[Benito Garcia (Mela)|Benito]], who ran in the same circle through Rachel in those years.

The group knows her as the stupid one, and that is a costume. Rachel wants Lexi to act stupid, so she does, and underneath it Lexi is smart, a little cold and calculated; her schedule of college classes is the tell nobody bothers to read. She stays in the group because Rachel is a great connection to have, and she plays whatever part keeps the connection warm.

After the prom-night sleepover at the end of Second Summer 1620, Lexi pulled back from the group a little, and she cannot name why. Milani was erased from the world that night, and like everyone close to her Lexi keeps only the smallest fragments, so the retraction is an instinct with its reason scrubbed out. She stayed close anyway. The connection was worth it.
