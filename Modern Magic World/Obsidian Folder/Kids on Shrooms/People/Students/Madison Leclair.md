Junior

What is your personality like?
Madison is the biggest hype man for Rachel. Beyond that, she will be dismissive, and usually takes any opportunity she gets to put someone down.

Madison is secretly in love with [[Rachel De Narris]] and would love to date them. Furthermore, they have a toxic obsession about it, and will do as much as she can to keep other people away from her.

## Backstory:
Madison entered [[Rachel De Narris]]'s circle at the end of freshman year (Second Autumn, 1619), and started off as just a part of it. The inner seats were taken: [[Milani Toll]] was the best friend, [[Lexi Conner]] the other third of the old trio.

Prom night changed that. At the end of Second Summer 1620 the girls slept over at the De Narris manor, and sometime in the night Madison caught [[Damien De Narris]] with his wand out, using [[Magic]] to carry an unconscious Rachel back to her room. Rather than being dealt with, she made a deal: she says nothing, and she keeps an eye on Rachel. Damien has granted her powers as part of the arrangement (the specifics of the deal and of her powers are still open; see [[Madison and Damien Dynamic]]).

Madison caught the magic, not the murder. She does not know Milani was killed that night, and the erasure scrubbed Milani from her memory like everyone else close to her, leaving only the smallest fragments. Even so, a friend vanishing without any answers does not sit well with her, whatever she gained from the deal. Madison is evil in the mean girl sense, not the murder sense.

With Milani gone from the world, the best friend seat was open, and Madison took it around the end of sophomore year (Second Winter, 1620), after Rachel was cursed and began to change.

After Rachel and [[Benito Garcia (Mela)|Benito]] broke up at the beginning of First Summer 1621, Madison started the rumor that Benito cheated on Rachel.
