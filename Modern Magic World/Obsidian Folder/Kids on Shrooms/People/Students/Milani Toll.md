Milani Toll was [[Rachel De Narris]]'s best friend, one third of the middle school trio she formed with Rachel and [[Lexi Conner]]. She was a [[Sorcerers|sorcerer]] who had not yet come into her powers. On the night of prom, at the end of Second Summer 1620, [[Howard De Narris]] captured her, killed her, and had her rewritten out of the world's memory. Almost nobody remembers she existed.

## Backstory:
Milani had been friends with [[Rachel De Narris]] since 1615, and was her best friend for a long while. Together with [[Lexi Conner]] they were a trio from middle school on. When Rachel started dating [[Benito Garcia (Mela)|Benito]] at her 16th birthday party (First Autumn, 1620), Milani became pretty good friends with him too.

At the end of Second Summer 1620, Rachel, Milani, Lexi, and [[Madison Leclair]] went to prom together, and afterward the girls went back to the De Narris manor for a sleepover. Sometime before that day, or during it, [[Howard De Narris]] had realized what Milani was: a sorcerer whose powers had not yet arrived.

While the girls slept, Howard captured her. In a secret room somewhere in the mansion, he dissected and experimented on her with [[Damien De Narris]], as part of Damien's teaching. Damien did not seal the entrance to the room correctly, and Rachel witnessed what was happening inside. Because it was not Rachel's first encounter with that room and with [[Magic]], Howard placed the advanced permanent curse on her rather than a simple mind erase (see [[Modify Memory]] and [[Rachel De Narris]]).

Then Milani was erased. The [[Scholars of Avira]] hold a captured demi-god level spirit of erasure and oblivion, and people who need to be completely removed from the world's memory are fed to it; the spirit rewrites them out of the world (see [[The Spirit of Erasure and Oblivion]] and [[Modify Memory]]). Most people forgot Milani entirely. The spell scrubs hardest where the bond was closest: Benito, Lexi, and even Madison retain only the smallest fragments, a name or a photo that feels vaguely familiar, though with time the memories could return in full. Strangers keep incidental glimpses precisely because there was no relationship to erase; a shop owner might remember Rachel once walking in with some girl.

When Damien was carrying Rachel back to her room with magic, Madison caught him with his wand out and made a deal with him rather than report what she saw. Madison caught the magic, not the murder; she does not know Milani was killed.

## Rumors:
There are no rumors about Milani Toll. That is the point of the spell: there is no missing girl, no cold case, no story at all. She was rewritten out.

## Relationships:
### School:
- [[Rachel De Narris]]
	- Best friend
	- Friends since 1615, best friends for most of it. Rachel witnessed her murder through the unsealed entrance, and the memory is sealed under her father's curse. Of everyone who loved Milani, Rachel is the one scrubbed deepest.
- [[Lexi Conner]]
	- Best friend
	- The third of the middle school trio. Lexi keeps only fragments of her now, and pulled back from the group after that summer without being able to name why.
- [[Benito Garcia (Mela)]]
	- Friend
	- Became pretty good friends with him while he dated Rachel. Like everyone close to her, he keeps only fragments; her name or a picture might feel familiar, and through time he could recover all of it.
- [[Madison Leclair]]
	- Friend, and witness to the night
	- Madison caught [[Damien De Narris]] using magic to carry Rachel back to her room and made a deal: silence, and keeping an eye on Rachel. She does not know Milani was killed, and even with only fragments left of her, a friend vanishing without answers does not sit well with her.

### The De Narris Family:
- [[Howard De Narris]]
	- Identified her as a pre-power sorcerer, captured her from the sleepover, and killed her through dissection and experimentation.
- [[Damien De Narris]]
	- Participated in the dissection and experimentation as part of his teaching. His failure to seal the room correctly is why Rachel saw.
