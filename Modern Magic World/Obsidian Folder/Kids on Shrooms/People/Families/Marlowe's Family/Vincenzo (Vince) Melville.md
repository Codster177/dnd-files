## Backstory:
Vince is secretly an independent [[Wizards|Wizard]] out of [[Orochi]]. Nobody in [[Astaria]] knows, his daughter included.

Back in [[Neridora]] the family lived in [[Safilari]], where Vince worked as an artificer. He is not a very strong wizard, and never pretended to be; his living was a small shop selling enchanted items and gear, practical work for practical customers.

The shop is what doomed them. Vince got into business with the criminal guild that rules the [[City of Zimino]], and once that family has its hooks in a supplier, there is no polite way out. The Melvilles needed to disappear to a better life, and the developed world was the one place the guild could not follow.

He was sent ahead first and alone to set up that life, about a year before [[Marlowe Melville (Rylee)|Marlowe]] crossed. Him being the wizard of the family is part of why: he was the one who could survive the trip and beat the process waiting on the other side.

Before he was led into the [[Astaria#The HUMI|HUMI]]'s rune room, Vince expended a protection scroll on himself. The Massive memory modification ritual of [[Modify Memory]] washed over him and took nothing. He walked out with his identification, his Neridoran memories, and full knowledge of the concealment Astaria performs on its immigrants, including what would later be done to his daughter.

He does not think it is the most perfect way to live. He also thinks this life is better than the one in Neridora, and he has made his peace with that trade. A world without magic is the price of a world without the guild.

So he performs the wiped immigrant, flawlessly, every day. When Marlowe probed the immigration story over spaghetti dinner, he gave her the sanctioned version with nostalgia in all the right places ([[Session 4]]). When her singing set [[Caspian Forrest (Kai)|Caspian]]'s car on fire, he blamed mean kids and bad friends, because a father who never considers magic is a father nobody investigates ([[Session 3]]).

His deepest precaution was the decoy. From his old shop stock he kept one enchanted piece, a Neridoran prayer lamp that burns without oil, and hid it wrapped in oilcloth above the ceiling panel of the patio storage closet next door, in the apartment of [[Sadie Marsh and Erin Kowalski]]. The logic was cold and simple: if magical activity ever brought investigators to this block, they would find a source, and it would not be in his home.

In [[Session 6]], it worked. The HUMI's sweep of the block found the lamp, confiscated it, and took the couple into custody as the source of the incident. The strike team closed its case and left, and the Melville apartment stayed just another address inside the circle.

Vince came home late that night to a repaired door, a hundred feet of dead lawn, and dark windows next door. He knows exactly what all three mean: magic happened at or near his home, the decoy fired, and his lightning rod is spent. What he does not know yet is whose magic it was.
