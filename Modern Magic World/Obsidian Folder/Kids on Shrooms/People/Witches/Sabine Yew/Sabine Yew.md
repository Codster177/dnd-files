## Backstory:
Sabine wanted to be a witch, and for a time the path looked open: blood inheritance of stations is typically popular, and she was partially trained by her mother, [[Hazel Eyes]], the Witch of the Eternal Woods.

Then the relationship between mother and daughter went bad, and the apprentice became a rival. When Hazel Eyes died, the Eternal Woods went not to Sabine but to a stranger her mother had been quietly watching for years: [[Benito Garcia (Mela)]].
