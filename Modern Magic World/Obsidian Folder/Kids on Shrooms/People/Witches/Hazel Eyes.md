## Backstory:
Hazel Eyes was the Witch of the Eternal Woods before [[Benito Garcia (Mela)]], and a very, very powerful one. Her station let her see the world through every living plant, and she held abilities of divination on top of it.

She was a member of [[The Celestial Coven]] and fought against the [[Scholars of Avira]] in [[Era 5 - The Severing War]]. Losing that war left her weary of the Scholars for the rest of her life, and it shaped her final decision as a witch: she worried that training a successor would attract dangerous attention to herself, and more importantly to the successor.

Through the plants she had been drawn to Benito as a soul fitting the next Witch of the Eternal Woods, and she watched him for a good while before his time. Against the couple thousand years she had lived, he was still a bit of a last minute pick. Like many witches, she did something foolish and assumed she had more time left; many stations are accidentally destroyed out of exactly that pride. When the end came, throwing Benito into the role untrained was likely one of the only things she could have done.

Her own family was not very interested in the station. Her youngest daughter [[Sabine Yew]] was, and was partially trained by Hazel Eyes, but became a rival when the relationship between mother and daughter went bad.
