A demi-god level spirit of erasure and oblivion (godlike spirits being the strongest class), held captive by the [[Scholars of Avira]]. Its true name is not yet chosen.

The Scholars use it as their answer to a problem too heavy for wizardry: completely erasing a person from the world's memory is theoretically possible as a casting of [[Modify Memory]], but it would take an incredibly powerful wizard. It is much easier to feed people to the spirit. When a person who needs to be removed from society is fed to it, the spirit performs the erasure, rewriting them out of the world.

The erasure scrubs hardest where the bond was closest. Family and close friends are left with only the smallest fragments, a name or a photo that feels vaguely familiar, though with enough time the memories can return in full. Strangers keep incidental glimpses precisely because there was no relationship to erase: a shop owner may remember the target walking in once with a friend, only because she never knew her.
