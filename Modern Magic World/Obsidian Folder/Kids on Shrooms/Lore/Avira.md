The world that our Kids on Shrooms campaign is set in.

A year in Avira is 400 days across eight 50-day months and two full season cycles; see [[The Aviran Calendar]]. Human lifespans are slightly spread out to fit those years, with puberty and maturing scaled accordingly, so a 36 year old Aviran biologically resembles a 36 year old from Earth.

Some things about Avira are simply not like Earth. Sexism, homophobia, and racism do not exist here; nobody in this world holds those prejudices, not even the [[Scholars of Avira]], who are evil in entirely different ways. Male pregnancy exists through magical intervention. The conflicts that drive this world run on nationalism and religion instead.
