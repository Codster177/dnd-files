All [[Magic]] in the world of [[Avira]] originates from [[The Kamihara]]. It reaches mortal hands through five categories of spellcaster: [[Sorcerers]], [[Witches]], [[Warlocks]], [[Wizards]], and [[Spirits]], the fifth category added to the spellcasters, being magical beings in their own right rather than humans who acquired power.

## Holding More Than One Source

A person can hold two sources of magic, but it is taboo. Spirits may not like a warlock gaining magic from other sources, and the [[Wizards]] within the [[Scholars of Avira]] are purists: they are directly against drawing on any other source, and would rather figure out how to harness those magics for their own advantage using wizardry. Still, it happens. A witch may learn a few wizard spells, and a sorcerer may hold a station as a witch.

Two rules do lie in the fundamentals of magic itself:

- Spirits cannot cast wizard magic.
- Warlocks are the only humans that can cast spirit magic, and only the magic they receive from the spirit they are in a pact with.

## What Wizardry Cannot Do

Healing magic is very, very rarely able to be utilized by wizards in the same capacity that [[Witches]], [[Spirits]], and [[Warlocks]] can wield it. It is the one province of magic that has never fully reduced to wizard-math.

## Magic and Technology

Magic is the underlying physics for how electricity works. Modern technology grew out of it, and the [[Scholars of Avira]] have kept that origin buried; see [[Wizard Magic]] for how the science community ended up holding half the picture.
