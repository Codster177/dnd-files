Modify Memory is the wizard spell for rewriting what a person remembers, and it is the quiet backbone of the world's masquerade. There are three commonplace castings of it within the standards of magic that the [[Astaria#The HUMI|HUMI]] uses (there are likely more, but these are the ones the HUMI focuses on):

- **One time minor modification.** The lowest level of modify memory, similar to the Modify Memory spell in D&D 5e. It erases a recent event or string of events and/or replaces it with something that fills the gap. This is the tool of the HUMI's response teams when dealing with people exposed to magical events.
	- Cast as an instantaneous spell that performs a one time effect.
	- Can be cast simply from a focus, or from a ritual / mass ritual.
- **Massive memory modification.** A medium level of modify memory, typically used in a more controlled environment; casting it instantaneously takes much more skill. It can replace, alter, add, and erase information throughout the target's entire life. Its most common application is the immigration process, and the reinstatement of previous magic users into the world as nonmagical people (although in modern days it is more common to fully investigate, and likely kill, a magical person through experimentation).
	- Casting time ranges from instantaneous up to an hour for less skilled spellcasters. Effects typically linger long term as a charm or curse.
	- Typically cast as a focused one person ritual, but skilled wizards can apply it as an instantaneous spell or even a mass ritual.
- **Continuous memory modification.** The highest level of modify memory, possible only for highly trained experts and still an experimental spell. It attaches to the target as a strong charm or curse, rewriting desired memories of any kind and conditioning the target to process new memories in a designated way: a target can be set not only to forget any memories relating to magic, but to rewrite and reprocess every future exposure to it. This spell typically has severe side effects on the target's personality.
	- Casting usually takes a few hours to days of experimentation. The effect is a permanent and hard to remove curse.
	- Typically cast through an experimental ritual; it would take an insanely powerful spellcaster to apply it instantaneously.
	- The spell is an ongoing area of study for the [[Scholars of Avira]], since the ability to mass apply it would mean a much more easily controlled world.

Known castings in play: [[Marlowe Melville (Rylee)]] had the Massive ritual cast on her during immigration, and her own magic cracked it. [[Rachel De Narris]] carries a Continuous curse cast by her father, [[Howard De Narris]].
