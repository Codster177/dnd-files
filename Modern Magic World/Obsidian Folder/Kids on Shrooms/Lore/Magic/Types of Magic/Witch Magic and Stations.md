A witch's power flows from their station: the aspect of the natural world they hold and protect. How a station is first granted or created is covered in the [[Witches]] note; this note covers how a station lives, changes hands, and dies.

## Losing a Station

If the thing under a witch's care is destroyed or diminishes, the witch slowly loses power as their station becomes less prominent. A station small enough to be destroyed outright is usually a sign of the witch ignoring it.

A witch dedicated to their station will typically go down with it. The witch of a woods will lay their life down before the last tree in their forest is cut.

A witch who does not, who abandons their station and lives on powerless, typically puts a target on their own head: if not from whoever destroyed the station, then from other tradition oriented witches, most prominently [[Mori, the Witch of Life and Death]]. An ex-witch can lose their power, become a nonmagical person, and live, but only if their station was never powerful enough to attract Mori, if they had a good reason that convinced her, or if they somehow escaped her. Escaping Mori is akin to escaping death: very rare, but it happens.

Note that scale matters here. [[Benito Garcia (Mela)]]'s station covers all woods and greenery, so destroying it would require every tree to be logged and paved. Stations that broad do not die by chainsaw.

## Changing Stations

A witch whose station is being overtaken may slowly progress into a different witch, adopting things they resonate with as the old station fades. The same transition also happens without any threat at all; it simply describes a witch moving from one station and passion to another.

## Stripping a Station

A negligent witch usually needs no one to punish them: an ignored, unmaintained station grows less prominent and less powerful, and the witch's power fades with it.

A witch blatantly disrespecting their station, or actively working against it, is another matter. Historically [[The Celestial Coven]] might have stepped in, but after losing [[Era 5 - The Severing War]] the Coven is far too weak to be doing anything of the sort. Mori still steps in personally for outrageous violations.

## Passing a Station

The witch holding a station does the matching of who takes it up next. Blood inheritance is actually typically popular, though nothing in the magic requires it; [[Hazel Eyes]] passed the Eternal Woods over her own daughters to a stranger she had watched for years through her plants.

## Witches and Time

Witches are not bound to a human lifespan. [[Hazel Eyes]] died old, but old meant a couple thousand years, alive since the end of [[Era 2 - The Great Awakening]]. Mori herself is a psychopomp who has ascended to a being beyond death, guiding people and creatures to the afterlife.
