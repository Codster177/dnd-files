Sorcery is [[The Kamihara]]-granted power carried through bloodlines. Where a wizard studies and a witch is chosen, a sorcerer simply is one, born into a line that once earned its magic and has passed it down in the blood ever since.

The inheritance can rarely skip generations. A sorcerer's child may show nothing at all while the power waits for a grandchild, and a parent watching for signs may find none. This is partially why [[Georgia Forrest]] had been so disappointed in [[Caspian Forrest (Kai)|Caspian]]: not that the boy was never into sports, but that he never showed signs of inheriting his sorcery power.

Sorcery does not manifest the same way twice. Some sorcerers gain a broad access to all kinds of [[Magic]]. Others are specialized: fire, ice, nature, and rarely something niche like gravity, and those narrow, strange specializations tend to produce very strong spellcasters.

A sorcerer is not locked to their blood alone. A sorcerer may also take up a station and hold it as a witch, learn wizard spells, and engage with pact magic, though mixing sources is taboo; see [[Magic]].
