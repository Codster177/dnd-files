Wizard magic is magic derived from the natural processes of the world, and has been refined to be dictated by various math's and sciences combined with magical logicizes to produce spells, enchantments, curses, and effects.

## The Great Gap

Not everything reduces to wizard-math. Healing magic is very rarely able to be utilized by wizards in the same capacity that [[Witches]], [[Spirits]], and [[Warlocks]] can wield it.

## Wizardry and Electricity

Magic is the underlying physics for how electricity works. Modern technology grew out of wizardry, and the mundane sciences have been living on half the picture ever since [[The Nullcasting]].

Most physicists do not have a magically attuned wand, and most physicists do not have the idea that magic is even remotely possible. Some physicists have definitely discovered properties of electricity that are magic related, and created entire new areas of physics around them (the electromagnetic spectrum, for one). The math works and the technology runs; they are simply describing magic without the vocabulary for it. [[Caspian Forrest (Kai)]] deduced in a single evening what a century of physicists could not, because he was holding the missing instrument: an attuned wand.
