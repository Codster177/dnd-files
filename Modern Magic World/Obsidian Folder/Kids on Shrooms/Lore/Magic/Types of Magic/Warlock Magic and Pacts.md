A warlock's [[Magic]] is borrowed power, granted by a spirit through a pact. Warlocks are the only humans who can cast any type of spirit magic; the pact is the door that power comes through.

A pact can be very loose or very strict, and the spirit sets the terms.

## Loose Pacts

A loose pact asks for alignment rather than payment. [[The Great Whale]]'s pact with [[Marlowe Melville (Rylee)]] is the model: the Whale is always able to gauge her values and passions, and as long as Marlowe fits with them, the power is granted, gaining strength as her values and passions align stronger with the Whale's.

What does the spirit get out of an arrangement like that? Hopefully, a better world. The Great Whale selected Marlowe after looking into her soul and psyche, and chose her with the hope and intention of Marlowe being the Whale's change in the world. The Whale is a spirit of good, and it has faith in its warlock.

## Strict Pacts

A more strict spirit may take more from a pact: a creature's soul, a promise, a first born. Stricter spirits also bind the pact with conditions, and if a condition is broken, the spellcaster becomes less powerful or loses the power completely.

## Boundaries

A warlock gaining magic from other sources is taboo, and spirits in particular may not like it; see [[Magic]] for how the five sources fit together.
