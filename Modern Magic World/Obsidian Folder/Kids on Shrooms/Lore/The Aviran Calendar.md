A year in [[Avira]] runs 400 days: eight months of exactly 50 days each, following two full cycles of seasons. Winter always comes twice.

## The Months

In order, with the rhythm of the school year alongside:

1. **First Spring** — school is on vacation.
2. **First Summer** — school is on vacation.
3. **First Autumn** — school starts the first Monday of the month.
4. **First Winter** — the schools' Winter Formal season typically takes place.
5. **Second Spring** — midterm season, followed by a "Second Spring Break."
6. **Second Summer** — the schools' prom season typically takes place here.
7. **Second Autumn** — the last month of school, with finals about halfway through.
8. **Second Winter** — school is on vacation; this month holds the Christmas-like holidays.

Dates are written month/day/year: "8/36/1603" is the 36th of Second Winter, 1603.

## The Week

When school is in session, school runs Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with alternating schedules between Monday/Wednesday and Tuesday/Thursday.

## BA and AV

AV counts the years since the founding of [[The Grand Aviran Empire]]; BA counts the years before it. [[The Church of Abeus]] teaches the same year zero differently: to the faithful it marks the descension of [[Abeus Algori]] down to Avira to guide the world to a better society.

## Years and Aging

Human lifespans in Avira are slightly spread out to fit the 400-day year, with puberty and maturing scaled accordingly. Someone who is 36 years old in Avira biologically resembles a 36 year old from Earth, so ages in these notes read the way you would expect.
