The Great Horrors are celestial, eldritch beings that feed off of life and [[Magic]]. They press against [[Avira]] constantly from the dark beyond its sky, pouring energy and effort into attacking the world without end.

Witnessing a Great Horror causes you to go mad.

The world survives them because of [[The Goddess]] and her children. [[The Great Moth - Sola]] and [[The Space Spirit - Aurorlin]] dedicate their existence to helping their mother defend Avira, akin to how Helios pulled the sun and moon. Rather than merely holding the horrors back, the two spirits repurpose the energy the horrors constantly spend attacking the world into something that actively helps the life on it: Sola processes the nearest horror into the sun, and Aurorlin processes the rest into the stars of the night sky.
