Also known as The Second Dark Age, the Modern Age starts after [[The Nullcasting]]. 

The history taught in this era is a curated one. Modern history does remember that the world went through medieval eras, wars, kingdoms, and empires, but everything is written to avoid [[Magic]]. The deep past survives in outline; the truth of it does not.
