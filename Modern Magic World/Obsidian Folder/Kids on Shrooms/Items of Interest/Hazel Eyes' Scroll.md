The scroll is a twine-bound letter penned by [[Hazel Eyes]], the late Witch of the Eternal Woods, addressed to her successor [[Benito Garcia (Mela)]]. She left it in the keeping of [[Cedrela, the Queen Bee Spirit]], who retrieved it from her hive in [[Sweet Lake Forest]] and handed it to Benito in [[Session 4]], alongside the diamond-shaped honeycomb that summons her.

Benito read the scroll privately. Neither [[Li JiaShen (Nick)|Shen]] nor Cedrela knows what it says. [[Apricot (Alvin)]] stared at it, declared the scribbles nonsensical, and admitted he cannot read.

## Contents:

Dearest Benito, 

I am looking forward to meet you! I can only imagine how you might be feeling; I remember very little about my youth. I know I do not have much time left, and I am leaving you with a lot of my problems, but try not to worry, time will make everything easier. 

You are now the witch that protects this woods, and all woods beyond. Hopefully, you will have the seed of my tree, as planting this will sprout a new one! If you ever need a home, this is one you can always rely on. 

My first advice to all new witches is to trust in what you represent. For us, the forest will always have our backs. 

But, I know that most early witches begin to find their way once they find a familiar, so try to do this. Although, in my experience, familiars usually have a way of finding you. 

Be strong, trust in the woods, trust in yourself, and be careful with trusting more. Trusting my sisters too much have led to many regrets in my life. 

Thank you. By being who you are, you are fighting for what I have fought for my entire life. 

Hazel Eyes

## Notes:

- The seed she mentions is the apricot seed from her tree, the one Benito kept in the secret compartment of his dresser. [[Sabine Yew]] broke through his window to take it, and it came back to him by way of [[Apricot (Alvin)|the chipmunk]] in [[Session 3]]. Planting it sprouts a new tree of hers, a home Benito can always rely on.
- The warning about her sisters references her coven, and how she was perceived to betray them during the Severing War.
- "What I have fought for my entire life" points at her side in [[Era 5 - The Severing War]], where she fought the [[Scholars of Avira]] as a member of [[The Celestial Coven]].
- Her advice on familiars had already come true before Benito read it: Apricot found him and bonded as his familiar (see [[Witch Magic and Stations]]).
- "I am looking forward to meet you!" is written in the present tense by a woman who knew she was dying, and she watched Benito through the plants for years before choosing him. Whether the line is warmth, divination, or a promise tied to the new tree is an open question.
