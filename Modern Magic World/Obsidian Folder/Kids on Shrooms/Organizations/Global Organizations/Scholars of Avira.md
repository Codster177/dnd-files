The secret wizard society that rules the world.

Their headquarters is the hollow mountain beneath [[Aviani Palace]] in [[Aviani]], which holds one of the biggest cities in the world. [[Port Moonbeam]] serves as their minor front port.

The Scholars also maintain the two blockades around the island of [[The Kamihara]]: a naval one crewed mostly by [[Astaria]]'s military, and a magical one of their own making.

## The Perfect World

The Scholars wanted a world that feels like it has agency, and they built one. The point is to keep people's problems exactly that: their problems. If a country goes to war with its neighbor, hits an economic crisis, or tears itself apart over social issues, the Scholars do not care about any of it. They know that if they did, they could end it in a moment's notice, because they control an incredible level of [[Magic]] and are deeply rooted in almost every government. Giving people the freedom to have their own troubles keeps them distracted from looking at the place where the most power actually is, and from ever attempting a revolt.

There is no finish line. The end state is to keep refining the new world into something everyone in the organization agrees upon, and to bring [[Orochi]] under their control as well.

## Conflict Within the Scholars

The only conflicts the Scholars treat as real problems are conflicts between their own wizards. When one wizard's goal crosses another's, that is what registers, and it is what actually brings geopolitical trouble to the regions each of them controls. The wizards rarely duel. They get passive aggressive, and they spend the pawns and massive resources they hold but do not much care about to get back at each other, causing bloody wars and ruining thousands of lives along the way. It is all about the bureaucracy and the power.

## Spirits and Experimentation

The Scholars believe they are the one true power in this world, so they are actively against anything that challenges that notion. They encourage and promote the capture, research, and experimentation of spirits and magical sources, witches, sorcerers, and other magical humans included. Some Scholars are not the biggest fans of magical experimentation on humans (though they will likely do it anyway), but nobody bats an eye when it comes to spirits.

## Magic in Industry

Every major company heavily invested in technology of any kind will eventually become shadowed by Scholar wizards, because a startup can only go so far without knowing the true nature of its own technology. But the magic is buried quite deep, and there are many profitable, successful companies that are not tech, industrial, or power giants, run without any knowledge of magic and with engineers doing actual work. Magic is the underlying physics for how electricity works, and even though there is a whole lot more to electricity than nonmagical people realize, electricity is still plenty useful on its own; see [[Wizard Magic]].

The giants that do know are successful because they can rely on shortcuts. They fundamentally incorporate spells into products that would otherwise take more expensive and complex processes to operate. They work magic into advertisement, hypnotizing people into favoring their company. Food and beverage companies use magic to make their products more addictive and enticing.

Why hand the magic-blind population magic-powered tools at all? Because innovation comes naturally, and the hierarchy within the Scholars is always in tension. The Scholars are a small percentage worldwide, but within [[Aviani]] there are a great many wizards, likely thousands to hundreds of thousands, and very few strong ones among them. For an ambitious wizard, becoming tied into an Astarian corporation grants more direct power in the nonmagical world: organizing a project for other people to build beats spending months or years trying to do it yourself discreetly with magic. And the Scholars like to save the interesting work for themselves, discovering new areas of magic and exploring the mystical. If they want a building built, they are going to hire a hundred or a thousand men. Who cares if the men have magic power tools?

## The Internet

What the Scholars gain from a global internet is a bigger and stronger network. It grants them easier access to information, and it doubles as a security and detection system: if a magic user interfaces with the internet and it is not one of them, they know.

The internet is an extradimensional space that wizards can occupy, travel through, and sense through. There is no harm in giving it to people who cannot use it that way; it just spreads the Scholars' power. Some high ranking wizards gunned especially hard for it, and one wizard is able to crawl through monitors, magically teleporting through the internet.

The Scholars also use the internet for travel, and have essentially built a gate system through it. The gates that physically transport matter and wizards through the internet do not translate to ordinary computer screens; they are specially designed magical machines hidden away from the population in secret locations and hideouts, usually top secret government facilities.
