The Developed International Concord (DIC) is the international body formed in 1511, at the end of [[Era 5 - The Severing War]]. Its founding ruling assigned the blame for the war to [[Orochi]], and that blame gave the Concord the flexibility to pass its defining act: the embargo.

## The Orochi Embargo

The embargo prohibits trade, travel, and immigration into [[Orochi]]. The [[Scholars of Avira]] pushed for it because [[The Nullcasting]] never reached that continent, and its people still know definitively of spirits and [[Magic]].

Emigration out of Orochi is a grey area, and deliberately so: there is no system for it. The DIC's member nations welcome refugees and immigrants from Orochi with no threat of deportation, granting papers and, after enough years in one place, citizenship. But every one of them must go through the immigration process, and will likely be forced to.

Since most of Orochi runs on medieval technology and many of its nations forbid travel to the outside world, an emigration typically plays out the same way:

- A group looking to leave finds each other and somehow gets ahold of a ship.
- The ship leaves Orochi, assuming its passengers were not caught by whatever nation they fled, if that nation forbids emigration.
- The Scholars' barricade spots the ship. It is boarded, and the refugees are taken aboard an Astarian or other naval vessel, depending on which country made the catch.
- The refugees are wrangled, and the ship's specialized crew screens them for spirits, independent [[Wizards]], [[Sorcerers]], anyone with magic. Anyone or anything found may be killed or captured.
- The survivors are brought to whichever nation the intercepting ship belonged to, and each person goes through that nation's immigration process. The processes differ country to country, but all of them are required to wipe memories of magic and spirits.
