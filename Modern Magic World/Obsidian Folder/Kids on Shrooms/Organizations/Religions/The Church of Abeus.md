[[Aviani Palace]] is one of the church's holy sites, said to be where the God-Emperor [[Abeus Algori]] once lived.

The Church teaches its own meaning for the calendar's year zero: where the world counts AV from the founding of [[The Grand Aviran Empire]], the faithful accrue it to the descension of [[Abeus Algori]] down to [[Avira]] to guide the world to a better society.
