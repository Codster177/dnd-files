- The current intro notes on her character tie the magic to a butterfly spirit, "perhaps generational," with the spirit having "gave the last of their generation powers."
- Open question from the warlock work (see [[Warlock Magic and Pacts]]): does the generational butterfly-spirit idea make Shen a warlock, and if so, what kind of pact is it (loose like [[The Great Whale]]'s with Marlowe, strict, inherited)?

# Notes:
Here is what I am ruling as the answer to this backstory:
- Shen's mom ([[Li SiSheng]]) was born in [[Dōngyè]], the northernmost town in [[Jiā]]. She was not attractive growing up, and she was bullied for it. In a town that small, her family was known as the family with the daughter who would never make it anywhere.
- The family eventually left Dōngyè and moved to [[Diéyuán]], the Butterfly Garden town on the western coastal hillside.
- In Diéyuán, SiSheng met Shen's father ([[Li ShiWang]]), and she met him as a spirit: the butterfly spirit. Over time the two formed a close relationship.
- SiSheng became his warlock, using his spirit power to make herself beautiful.
- Her dancing, though, was hers. She was a talented dancer from the start and never needed magic for it. The magic helped, but the talent is what the spirit liked about her, and it played a real part in their relationship.
- She became a famous dancer and moved to [[Méilí]], the city whose name means "beautiful."
- Somewhere along the way they married. ShiWang wore his human form (his beauty) for most of their life together.
- Her father ([[Li LewMingzhi]]) noticed her sudden beauty after she fell in love, and that was his first sign that something was going on. I think he knows more than that, probably that ShiWang is a spirit, but I'll work that out later.
- The pregnancy had complications (a child of a human and a spirit), so they decided to move to [[Astaria]] where the birth would be safer.
- During the move to Astaria, ShiWang was found out as a spirit.
	- TODO: found out by who, and what happened to him after. This is the gap where he leaves Shen's life.
- SiSheng's death: the complications that started with the pregnancy manifested into an illness, and years later that illness is what killed her.
- The butterfly spirit in Shen's backstory only appears after ShiWang dies. Spirits don't truly die: they don't age to death, and when one is killed its soul returns to [[The Kamihara]] and rejoins the reincarnation cycle (already canon in the Kamihara note).
	- This answers half the TODO above: whatever happened when he was found out, it killed him.
- Related lore: a lot of what the [[Scholars of Avira]] do is capture spirits and keep them for future use. Worth holding next to the TODO about who found ShiWang out.
- When ShiWang died, he returned to the Kamihara and eventually came back as the new butterfly spirit. Reincarnation keeps no memories, but it keeps the passions and the feelings.
- So the new butterfly spirit still has a connection to Shen and can aid her, but it doesn't know her the way it should. It doesn't remember SiSheng or Shen, yet they feel familiar to it, and it carries a warmth toward them it can't explain.
- That's the same wound from both sides: her father died and wasn't there for her through no fault of his own, so they have no relationship, and the reincarnation can't have one with her either. Not yet.
- I want this new butterfly spirit to play a bigger part in the story.



### Claude Brainstorming:

**The redirect: sorcerer, not warlock.** Shen is a [[Sorcerers|sorcerer]] with a spirit bloodline. [[Spirit Magic]] already carves out the exception ("only Sorcerers that have spirit-based bloodlines and Warlocks can cast any type of spirit magic"), so no new rules are needed. The old intro line "spirit gave the last of their generation powers" gets reread: not a pact and not a gift from outside, but an inheritance. The butterfly spirit is *in* the family tree, and its power dwells in one member of the line per generation. Nobody signed anything. There are no terms to break, which is exactly why the spirits circling her are so uneasy: a pact can be revoked, blood can't. Loose end to fix when this finalizes: [[Magic]] still says warlocks are the *only* humans who can cast spirit magic; it should be corrected to match [[Spirit Magic]].

Everything at the table already points to sorcery. Her magic modifiers sit on Constitution and Charisma, the body and the presence, not on borrowed willpower. The power came out of grief with no spirit anywhere in the room, no bargain, no voice. That's a bloodline waking up, and it woke the night the previous holder died.

**Option A: the father is the spirit.** Her character sheet contradicts itself, and the contradiction is a gift. The intro says her father passed away before she was born; her backstory says he *left* before she could remember him, the reason "deliberately hidden," and the few things Shen heard were suspiciously positive. Both are true if [[Li ShiWang]] was a butterfly spirit wearing his beauty (human form). The man is gone and never coming back, which a grieving widow would round to "passed away," but there was no body and no funeral photos on the shelf, because he folded his human shape and returned to whatever garden or court he came from. Maybe spirits can't stay in a beauty through a child's whole upbringing without fading. Maybe his kin found him and dragged him home. Either way [[Li SiSheng]] knew exactly what she married, hid it to her grave, and the "pretty positive" scraps Shen heard were a widow protecting a good memory, not a shameful one. This makes Shen half-spirit outright, the strongest version of the bloodline, and gives the campaign a living NPC to eventually meet: a father who has been watching his daughter through every reflective surface she passes.

**Option B: the bloodline runs through the mother.** The spirit is further back, a grandmother or great-grandmother on [[Li SiSheng]]'s side, and the power passes down the line one holder at a time. SiSheng was the previous holder. The dancing Shen thought was the most beautiful thing she'd ever seen wasn't just skill, it was the glamour working through her, the same magic Shen now wears as a face. Her illness reads two ways, pick one: the power began migrating to Shen early and its departure hollowed SiSheng out, or the bloodline itself has a price (see the cost idea below) and the bill came due. Under this option the mirror scene is literal inheritance: the magic left the mother's body as she died down the hall and found the next daughter of the line sobbing in front of a cracked mirror. Shen's magic is, in the most direct sense possible, her mother. That is a brutal, excellent thing for her to learn mid-campaign.

**Option C: distant ancestry in Jiā.** The Li family reads as Jiā stock, so put the spirit generations back in the old country: a butterfly spirit who loved a dancer, or a calligrapher, or a silk merchant's daughter, long before the family ever crossed to [[Astaria]]. The Zhuangzi resonance is free money here. Jiā philosophy could hold a famous parable about a scholar who dreamt he was a butterfly and woke unsure which one was real, and the family could even own a worn copy without knowing it's about *them*. This option fits Shen's identity crisis best: a girl who transformed into a dreamt-of self and no longer knows which face is the real one is living her ancestor's parable. It also lets [[Li LewMingzhi]] carry old-country stories he half-believes, and makes the bloodline something the family *forgot* rather than something they hid.

**Making the powers cohere as a butterfly bloodline.** Her whole kit maps cleanly once you commit to the theme. Metamorphosis: the new look, and the full disguise as Rachel, are literally what a butterfly does, one body traded for another. Camouflage: her invisibility is wings folded, the underside of the wing that looks like bark. True-sight through reflections: butterfly wings wear eyespots, false eyes that see nothing, and Shen inverts that, real sight hidden in false surfaces. She saw Ian's dragon form in a bus window and her prettier self in a cracked mirror; glass and mirrors could be formally *hers*, the surface where her magic tells the truth. And she sees through glamour because she is made of glamour. A useful line for the eventual lore note: the bloodline doesn't grant spells so much as it grants the butterfly's whole life cycle, and Shen has only reached the second stage.

**A possible cost worth seeding.** Butterflies are brief. If the bloodline's beauty burns bright and dies young, then SiSheng's illness wasn't bad luck, it was the family curse doing what it always does, and every holder before her died the same way: radiant through their twenties and thirties, gone before fifty. LewMingzhi tucked his daughter in knowing the pattern. This hangs a quiet clock over Shen's new face (the thing she wanted most is the thing that kills the women of her line) and gives her a long-game quest: break the cycle, outlive the wings. It also sharpens the mirror scene in hindsight, since the power jumped to her *while she was wishing to look like her mother*. Careful what you inherit.

**The spirit itself.** Two directions worth weighing:

- Kin of [[The Great Moth - Sola]]. Sola is a Great Spirit, a child of [[The Goddess]], and a *moth*. Make the butterfly spirit Sola's lesser kin, a cousin-line of day-fliers to Sola's night-flier, and the mythology writes itself: moths serve the sun and the long watch against [[The Great Horrors]], while the butterflies stayed on Avira and fell in love with mortal things, dancing, silk, faces. The moth court considers the butterfly line's dalliance with humans an embarrassment at best and a theft of celestial inheritance at worst. This scales Shen's little family secret up into Great Spirit politics without changing anything at street level.
- An independent local spirit, Cedrela-scale. [[Cedrela, the Queen Bee Spirit]] proves insect spirits with small domains and human forms exist. The butterfly could be the same order of being: spirit of a specific garden, orchard, or dance hall back in Jiā, with a name, a temper, and opinions. Smaller stakes, more personal stories, and a spirit Shen could realistically track down and yell at.

Either way, decide whether the spirit is alive (watching, possibly the father from Option A), faded (poured the last of itself into the line, hence "gave the last of their generation powers"), or dead in a way that matters (killed, and the bloodline is all that's left of it, which makes Shen a walking relic other spirits want recovered).

**"Spirits trying to take my powers," answered.** Nick asked for spirits coming after her magic, and the bloodline frame gives that teeth in a way a pact never could. Candidates:

- Doctrine enforcers: spirits who hold that spirit magic in human blood is theft, full stop. [[Magic]]'s current text ("warlocks are the only humans that can cast spirit magic") could survive in-world as *their* position, the official line the spirit courts insist on while bloodlines like Shen's make a liar of it.
- Inheritance claimants: kin of the butterfly spirit who watched the previous holder die last summer and now see the family power sitting in an untrained fifteen-year-old. To them it's not an attack, it's probate.
- The moth court (if the Sola option is taken): formal, patient, and convinced the gift should be reclaimed and carried home to the sky where it belongs.
- And since they can't break a pact that doesn't exist, their options get ugly: end the line, marry into it, or convince Shen to surrender the power willingly. That last one is the interesting one. A spirit shows up wearing a beautiful beauty and offers to take the magic *and the curse* off her hands, right when she's terrified of what the bloodline did to her mother.
- [[Rosaline, The Weavedropper]] is sitting unused, and a spirit whose name suggests listening through weaves would be a natural first scout: something has been eavesdropping on Shen long before anything knocks.

**What the family knew.** The grandfather is the hinge. At the mirror scene [[Li LewMingzhi]] saw his granddaughter walk out wearing a face that wasn't hers an hour ago, and his reaction was a *slight surprised look, yet understanding*. That is not a man learning something new; that is a man watching something he'd been told about finally happen. Best version: LewMingzhi has known since SiSheng was young, kept the secret through his daughter's whole life, and has been waiting, exhausted and dreading it, to see whether it would skip Shen the way [[Sorcerer Magic and Bloodlines]] says bloodlines sometimes skip. He falls asleep in his factory uniform because he has spent his life working like a man who knows he can't protect his family from the thing that's actually coming. When Shen finally asks him, he doesn't deny anything. He just gets the box down from the closet: a photo, a dried wing pressed in paper, maybe a letter in the father's hand. The empty [[Li SiSheng]] and [[Li ShiWang]] stubs stay empty until the tree-position choice below is made, then get filled to match.

**Open decisions before this moves to real notes:**

- Whether the spirit is alive, faded into the bloodline, or dead.
- Whether to adopt the burns-bright-dies-young cost, and whether SiSheng's illness was that cost.
- Which faction is actually hunting Shen, and how patient they are.
- How much LewMingzhi knows and when he admits it.
- Correcting [[Magic]]'s "warlocks only" line to match [[Spirit Magic]] (or keeping the old text as in-world spirit-court doctrine).
