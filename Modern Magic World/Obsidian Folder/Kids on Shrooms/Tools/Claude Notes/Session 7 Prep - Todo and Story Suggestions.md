## Story Suggestions for Session 7:

Where things stand: [[Session 6]] ended Wednesday night, so the next session opens Thursday, the fourth day of school. The five know each other's powers, the HUMI raided [[Marlowe Melville (Rylee)|Marlowe]]'s apartment and left believing nothing was there, and [[Rachel De Narris]]'s birthday party is Saturday. A few threads below lean on Saga20 recap facts; those are marked **(Saga20, validate)** since the recaps are autogenerated.

### Thursday morning aftermath:

- **The dead lawn.** A hundred-foot ring of wilted, blackened vegetation around Marlowe's block is physical evidence that doesn't wash off. Does the HUMI send a cleanup crew dressed as city landscapers, or does the neighborhood just gossip about a gas leak? [[Benito Garcia (Mela)|Benito]]'s station should be able to feel the wound in the greenery, and [[Cedrela, the Queen Bee Spirit|Cedrela]] almost certainly felt it too. That's a free hook to pull Benito back to the hive.
- **The neighbor.** She was inside the ritual circle, so her memory of the balcony scene is gone. A scene where she waves pleasantly at Marlowe works as quiet horror, especially if one fragment survived (a note on her phone she doesn't remember typing, a photo of a giant succulent).
- **The couple who took the fall.** Now ruled: the strike team found [[Vincenzo (Vince) Melville|Vince]]'s decoy, the enchanted prayer lamp hidden next door, and took [[Sadie Marsh and Erin Kowalski]] into custody as the source. Their dark windows and piling mail are the party's first clue that someone else paid for Wednesday night, and Marlowe is the one positioned to notice. If the party ever learns two innocent women were taken because of them, that guilt is a whole arc on its own.
- **The lamp is a loose thread pointed at Vince.** The confiscated lamp is Neridoran make from an artificer's shop. If anyone competent at the HUMI examines it (or [[Howard De Narris]], the head HUMI representative for [[Lake Prospect]], audits the closed case), the questions become where a mundane young couple got Orochi craftsmanship, and who on that block immigrated from Neridora. Vince's lightning rod worked once; it also started a clock.
- **The suppression spell's clock.** Rule when [[Caspian Forrest (Kai)|Caspian]]'s aura-masking wears off, and whether five signatures flickering back on at once is itself something the detection net can notice. Even if the answer is no, Caspian will ask, and his anxiety about it is good material.
- **Fractured memories as a drip feed.** The four who got stunned woke with fractured memories. Let pieces resurface as flashes over the next few in-game days; it's a clean GM tool for re-revealing anything from the raid you want back on the table.
- **Vince already knows.** He came home to a repaired door, a dead lawn, and vanished neighbors, and unlike everyone else on the block he can read all three: magic happened at or near his home and his decoy fired. Marlowe lying to her dad's face now cuts both ways, because he is lying right back, and he has to decide whether to quietly plant a new lightning rod or start watching his daughter.

### Rachel's birthday party (Saturday the 16th):

This is the obvious session centerpiece and almost every PC has a reason to be there.

- **The venue is the trap.** The party is at her father's empty house instead of the beach house **(Saga20, validate)**. If that's a De Narris property, the players are partying on top of the place where [[Milani Toll]] was held under the manor and killed. Even if it's a separate property, it's Howard's world, and there can be a locked study, a warded basement door, a portrait with orange eyes.
- **Rachel's "magic" is the mystery they'll chase.** The party believes Rachel is magical; the truth is the aura Caspian detected is Howard's [[Modify Memory]] curse. If Caspian probes it up close at the party, decide what the curse looks like under real scrutiny, and whether poking an experimental Scholars enchantment pings its caster.
- **Which Rachel shows up.** The curse makes her fluctuate: good-day Rachel, cruel Rachel, or stay-home-sick Rachel. Her own birthday under Madison's pressure and Shen's ambition is a great place to show the crack in her, maybe the first moment a PC pities her.
- **Benito, the ex, at the party.** [[Jace Shotwell]] is her boyfriend and Benito's SGA colleague, and Benito still doesn't know they're dating. Let him find out in the worst possible way, mid-party.
- **[[Madison Leclair]] on the warpath.** She'll spend the whole party trying to humiliate Shen out of the clique, and she's carrying two secrets: she's in love with Rachel, and she's been Damien's paid watcher since prom night, when she caught him carrying Rachel with magic and traded silence for powers (see [[Madison and Damien Dynamic]]). Her sheet quietly has a Magic die; if you want to seed it, one impossible little moment at the party is enough.
- **Damien among prey he can't see.** A wizard-in-training brat working the room where five secretly magical teenagers are drinking punch. He might be arrogant enough to show off a "trick" to look cool, or observant enough to notice Shen's face doesn't photograph quite right.
- **Milani in the walls.** [[Milani Toll]] was erased, not just killed: almost nobody remembers the third girl in the old trio photos. If a framed photo survived at the venue, the horror beat is a party guest squinting at it and asking "who's that?" while Rachel and [[Lexi Conner]] genuinely can't answer. Fragments remain in the people who loved her and can grow back over time, so one wrong question at the wrong party could be the first crack in the whole De Narris secret.
- **Shen's disguise liability.** Shen has impersonated Rachel twice and has the photo on her phone. A party full of Rachel's friends is the worst possible place for that to surface, and the best.

### Benito threads:

- **Plant the tree.** [[Hazel Eyes' Scroll]] says the seed sprouts a new tree of hers, "a home you can always rely on." That makes planting it the party's path to a sanctum, and the decision of where is a real one: deep in [[Sweet Lake Forest]] under Cedrela's watch, or somewhere closer and riskier like the backyard. Worth deciding what a witch's home tree actually does (shelter, concealment from detection, a place the HUMI sigils can't read?) and whether a new tree of Hazel Eyes announces itself to everyone who knew the old one, [[Sabine Yew]] first among them.
- **The sisters warning.** "Trusting my sisters too much have led to many regrets in my life." That is the scroll's loudest line and it needs a ruling: her coven sisters in [[The Celestial Coven]]. Note the wrinkle: [[Sabine Yew]] is her *daughter*, so a reader who assumes the warning covers Sabine might be wrong in a way that costs him. Whoever the sisters are, the warning is Benito's shield the first time a friendly witch introduces herself.
- **"I am looking forward to meet you!"** Present tense, from a woman writing before her death, who also says she remembers very little of her own youth. Decide whether that's just an old witch's warmth or a promise: divination showing her a meeting she won't live to attend the normal way, some piece of her persisting in the new tree, or something about how witches end that the vault hasn't ruled yet. Even unanswered, it's a line Benito can lie awake chewing on.
- **Benito is keeping it secret.** He read the scroll privately; Shen and Cedrela don't know what it says. That's the first real secret held inside the party since the confessions, and it will strain exactly when the seed or the sisters come up. Decide how Cedrela reacts if she learns her dead friend's letter was withheld from her.
- **Her fight is now his.** "By being who you are, you are fighting for what I have fought for my entire life." Hazel Eyes fought the [[Scholars of Avira]] with the Celestial Coven and spent her last centuries wary of them. Benito just inherited a side in a war he doesn't know exists, written in his pocket, while his friends dodge HUMI strike teams. The scroll and the raid are the same story; let Benito be the one who connects them.
- **Sabine is live.** She broke his window and stole the seed, and the scroll now explains why: it's her mother's tree, the home and legacy she was cut out of. The seed came back to Benito via the chipmunk; decide whether Sabine knows he has it again, because if she does, planting it starts a race. [[Story Leads]] also says a chipmunk will steal the Apricot seed, so pin down the seed's current location cleanly before it becomes a continuity knot. [[Fulan (Familiar)]] makes a good first-contact scout: a strange animal watching Benito that Apricot instantly hates.
- **Witch politics.** The [[Planner]] wants Benito's witch politics built out: Fulan, and enemies of [[The Celestial Coven]]. Cedrela can carry the exposition; she fought beside [[Hazel Eyes]]' generation and can tell Benito what a station is actually worth, and who still keeps lists of witches. If Benito ever asks her about the sisters (without admitting where he heard of them), her reaction is worth planning in advance.
- **The honeycomb.** One bite summons Cedrela. Players forget items like this; have Apricot mention it at the worst time so it's loaded in their minds before a crisis.
- **The familiar line already paid off.** The scroll says familiars have a way of finding you, and [[Apricot (Alvin)]] had already done exactly that before Benito read it. Cheap, warm beat: Benito rereads that paragraph and looks at the chipmunk in the bow tie.
- **Family texture.** He promised to pick up the twins from school, his dad is supposed to investigate the regrown vines, and his little brother's cut hand hasn't healed. The vines are a ticking clock: what does Hido conclude when he actually looks?
- **[[Rebecca Kim]].** Her crush is sitting there unused. Her trying to ask Benito to Rachel's party and choking is a one-scene delight.
- **Soccer.** Daily practice puts Benito and Damien on the same field every evening. That's your recurring, low-stakes window into the De Narris family.
