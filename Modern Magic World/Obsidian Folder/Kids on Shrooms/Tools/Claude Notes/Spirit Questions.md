Questions about spirits, moved out of [[Worldbuilding Questions]] to build out spirits more in depth here. Answers can be written inline under each bullet and harvested into real notes later.

**The Great Spirits**

- How many Great Spirits are there? Is it a closed pantheon (the Goddess, Mori, the Whale, Sola, Aurorlin, Kánt, the Mountain Serpent, the Strong Goat, the Doorman) or an open class that the world keeps producing?
- [[The Goddess]] became the moon. Is she conscious and active, does she ever answer anyone, and do the [[Scholars of Avira]] know what the moon is?
- What are [[The Great Horrors]], concretely: entities, appetites, laws of the outer dark? Has one ever gotten through, and is there a scar somewhere in the world or the sky to prove it?
- [[The Space Spirit - Aurorlin]] grants a wish to those who glimpse their face. Has anyone in recorded history managed it, and did a wish quietly shape an era?
- Does Project: Touch the Stars risk physically poking through Sola and Aurorlin's protective layers, and is that why [[Georgia Forrest]] committed treason after reading the project's intentions?
- [[The Great Knowledge Spirit - Kánt]] created humanity. Why? Loneliness, experiment, argument with the Goddess? What does Kánt think of what humans did with the world, and where is Kánt now?
- [[The Mountain Serpent]] and [[The Strong Goat]]: domains, homes, and temperaments. Why does [[Ulism]] bundle them with the Whale; did the three act together in some event southern Orochi still remembers?
- [[Mori, the Witch of Life and Death]] predates the pilgrimages. Where does she reside, does she still personally weigh every dying protector, and what is her relationship to the Goddess: daughter, peer, employee?
- Do all Great Spirits carry taboos like [[The Doorman]]'s invitation rule? Is a taboo the structural price of great power, and what does breaking one cost the spirit?
- Is [[The Doorman]] humanity's creation or its creator's colleague: born from human behavior (doors, thresholds, opportunity) or made alongside humans by Kánt? And was he the one who produced Ian's enrollment papers?

**Spirits in general**

- Where do new spirits come from in 1621? [[Cedrela, the Queen Bee Spirit]] is only 92, so the world still makes them. What conditions birth a spirit: devotion, place, function, accident?
- What are the rules of a "beauty" (human form)? Does every spirit get one, can a spirit be stuck in one, and how do Ian's human form and Shen's shapeshifting differ mechanically from a true beauty?
- In the Age of Spirits, fire and water spirits killed each other endlessly and lived forever. What changed? Can spirits permanently die now, and what did it?
- How does spirit society organize? Cedrela is a Queen with subjects and stings. Do local courts owe fealty upward to Great Spirits, and who does Cedrela answer to, if anyone?
- What exactly did [[Apricot (Alvin)]] become when he gained sentience and bonded as a familiar? Can a familiar outlive its witch, and what is [[Fulan (Familiar)]] (species, form, temperament)?
- The Empire captured many spirits during Eras 4 and 5. Where are those spirits now? Vaults under [[Aviani]]? Fuel cells for [[The Nullcasting]]? Are any still alive and owed a rescue?
- Who is [[Rosaline, The Weavedropper]]? The name suggests listening through weaves and webs. Whose spirit is she, whose side is she on, and who does she report to?
- Ian is growing (Saga20, Session 6). Do spirits have life stages, and what does a Cloud Serpent Dragon grow *into*?
