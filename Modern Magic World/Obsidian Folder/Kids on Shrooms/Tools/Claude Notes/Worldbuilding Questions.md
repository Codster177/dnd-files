### Tier 1 — Fully fleshed out (finding the hidden holes):

**Detection and enforcement**

- What actually triggered the Session 6 strike team: the neighbor's call hitting a screening layer inside emergency dispatch, ambient detection of the balcony magic, or both? What is the escalation ladder from "weird 911 call" to "four operatives and a city-block sigil"?
- The operatives scanned four unconscious teenagers, declared them clean, and left without identifying anyone or following up. Is that standard doctrine (no signature, no case), or did Shen watch a sloppy team whose report is going to get audited by someone scarier?
- Are the white-armor operatives [[Astaria#The HUMI|HUMI]]? The HUMI keeps a wizard-led team in every major city. Was the wand-carrier the team's wizard, and which city's team covers [[Lake Prospect]]?
- The strike team's ground sigil shares elements with Marlowe's immigration rune but is "far more complex." Is there a standard runic toolkit that all Astarian magical enforcement draws from, and did the Scholars author it?
- If strike teams respond to one wilted lawn within the hour, how have five magical teenagers, a witch's forest, a queen bee spirit, and a wizard training his son all coexisted in one small city without a single prior raid? What does the detection net actually see, and what are its blind spots?
- [[Ian Long (Aaron)]] walked into a school with no records, and every paper he needed appeared overnight. What produced them? ([[The Doorman]]? [[The Great Knowledge Spirit - Kánt|Kánt]]? Something in the school?) And does that same benefactor shield him from the paperwork cross-checks that should have caught a student with no birth record?
- The janitor and the police officer investigated Portable #13 twice in one night. At what point does a mundane weird-occurrence report at a school cross into HCI or HUMI referral, and is there a form for it?

**Astaria's government**

- The Ruling Parliament is public and elected. How are the 100 chairman districts drawn, and what does an election season look like when the ruling party has structural control? Do Astarians believe their democracy works?
- Who designed the 25/75 vote split and the carried/developed law mechanics, and when? Is the system inherited constitutional DNA from [[The Grand Aviran Empire]], and would a legal historian notice the fingerprints?
- Astaria mirrors the United States culturally but has no visible judiciary in its structure notes. Who interprets law when chairmen disagree: courts, the Council of Experts, or nobody, by design?
- The Council of Experts serves six-year terms and reviews every law. How are Experts recruited, and are the Scholars' plants concentrated there rather than in the noisy elected chamber?

**The Kamihara and the blockade**

- The naval blockade is crewed mostly by mundane Astarian sailors. What do those crews believe they are blockading, what are their rules of engagement, and what happens to a sailor who sees the clouds part?
- What does the magical blockade actually stop: physical approach, the island's magic leaking out, or something trying to leave? Do the souls of the dead still pass through it freely on their way in?
- Has anyone breached the blockade in 110 years, in either direction? Is there a contingency for the day something comes *out*?

### Tier 2 — Mostly built, visible gaps:

- What does the military look like publicly: branches, size, where it deploys? And is the Rane conflict cold-war shaped, with proxy fronts and an arms race?
- Which war is the Astarian public *aware* of right now? The [[The Etheri]] engineered a war between [[Rane]] and "an ally of Astaria." Which ally, is it in the news in [[Lake Prospect]], and are there yellow ribbons on doors?
- What are Astaria's national holidays, and does any of them unknowingly celebrate a Scholar victory (a "peace day" that is actually Nullcasting day)?
- What is the currency called, and what does the cost of living look like for the campaign's families (the Melvilles scraping by, the Garcias comfortable, the De Narrises rich)?
- Is the Church of Abeus the mainstream Astarian faith, one option among many, or a minority? What does the average pew-sitter believe?

**Rane**

- Is [[The Etheri]] an official organ of the Ranen state, a rogue faction, or a deniable asset that the council can disown? Astaria calls it terrorism; what does Rane call it?
- Why are Rane and Astaria in passive conflict, specifically? Resources, ideology, a grudge war in living memory, or an arrangement engineered to keep both busy?
- What is daily life in Rane like: desert cities, palm jungle in the south, badlands in the middle? What do they export, and what does their oligarchy fear most, its neighbor or its own people?

**Jiā and Wàibù**

- [[Jiā]] is a representative theocracy. A theocracy of *what*? Is the state religion built on real spirits, and if so, how did the Nullcasting handle an entire national faith?
- Who is the head of religious state, how are they chosen, and what does "representative" mean under them?
- What split [[Wàibù]] from Jiā (the name literally means "outside")? Schism, secession, a conquest that stuck? Are they rivals or estranged siblings?
- How do Jiā and Astaria interact across the strait at [[Kenosha]]: trade, tourism, spy games?

**Orochi and the embargo**

- How is the embargo physically enforced along an entire continent? Naval patrols, magical barriers, or does Orochi simply lack the ships, and the patrols are theater?
- What does Orochi know about the other side? They kept their magic and their memory of the Severing War. Do their nations teach that the outside world is ruled by the wizards who won?
- Is there smuggling? Orochi goods, Orochi people (the Melvilles got out somehow). Every embargo breeds a black market; who runs this one and through which ports?
- What are Neridora's, Zimino's, and Palicia's actual governments? The notes have empty template sections; even one line each (Neridora's council of elders, Zimino's guild-lords, Palicia's volcano cult or lack thereof) would anchor them.

- Do Orochi nations have their own Kamihara relationship: open pilgrimages, still? Is Orochi where new [[Sorcerers]] are still being made in 1621?

**Religions**

- [[The Church of Abeus]]: core doctrine, clergy structure, and what the faithful believe [[Abeus Algori]] was. Prophet, saint, god-emperor? What do they think [[Aviani Palace]] contains?

- What other religions exist in the first world? Does anything worship [[The Goddess]] or the moon directly, and is there a rationalist/atheist current in a world where atheism is factually wrong?

**The DIC and world order**

- Who are the [[Developed International Concord]]'s member states, where is it headquartered, and what does it do besides the embargo: currency standards, war arbitration, disease control?
- Was the DIC founded as the Nullcasting's institutional arm, and does any member government resent or resist it openly?
- Is there a DIC military or peacekeeping force, and who commands the Kamihara blockade on paper?

**The spellcaster system**

**Lake Prospect and West Paradise**
