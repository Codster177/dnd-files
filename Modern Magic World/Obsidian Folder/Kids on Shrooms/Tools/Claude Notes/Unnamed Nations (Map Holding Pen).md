The world map is unfinished. These nations exist on it with borders, capitals, and roads, but no names yet; their specifics live here until they are named, at which point each gets a real note under `Countries/` and its settlements get notes under `Locations/`.

- **The northern nation.** Spans the entire top of the main continent, from the northwest corner to the gulf facing [[Jiā]]. Glaciers and arctic cover its northern half, where a single town sits between a large lake and the east coast. The southern half is well developed: several unnamed towns, cities, and a capital, a full highway system, and even a road out to the northern town. It borders [[Rane]] and [[Astaria]] and has highway connections to both, so relations with each are at least "ok"; the ideation is that it leans closer to Rane than to Astaria. A major first world country not yet built out.
- **The nation surrounding [[Aviani]].** A somewhat developed nation holding the rest of the large southern peninsula around Aviani's enclave.
- **The southwest cluster.** Six to eight small nations packed into the forested, mountainous southwest, each fenced in full borders with its own starred capital, plus one bordered island nation offshore. Jungles and dense rainforest, with tundra and arctic in the southmost countries; one of them holds several volcanos. Mostly imagined as second world countries, some third world.
- **The [[Orochi]] arc nations.** Beyond [[Palicia]], [[Neridora]], and [[Zimino]], the eastern continent is split into several more unnamed nations, each with a starred capital and nothing but dirt trails between them.
- **Rane's settlements.** [[Rane]] has a capital northeast of its center and several other settlements, none named yet.
- **Palicia's western archipelago.** The small islands off [[Palicia]]'s west coast are unclaimed territories.
