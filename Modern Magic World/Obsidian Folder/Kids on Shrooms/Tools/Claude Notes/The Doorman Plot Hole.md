Plot hole to resolve: [[The Doorman]] gained access to [[Caspian Forrest (Kai)|Caspian]]'s door in [[Session 5]] (voice-guised as Caspian's mom, invited through). Why didn't he just capture or do something to Caspian and [[Ian Long (Aaron)|Ian]] right then?

### Two candidate answers:

- **The invitation must be verbal.** The Doorman cannot have a person merely open the door; they have to say "Come in." in some fashion. Perhaps the easiest answer to dismiss the plot hole, but unsatisfying: it throws away the looming threat of the Doorman having permanent access to that room, and the fact that the boys never figured out it was not Caspian's mom or that something was awry.
- **The Doorman has a plan, or a struggle.** He has some reason of his own, or an individual struggle between helping the [[Scholars of Avira]] and rebelling against them. Preferred direction, with a caveat: the struggle should build over time rather than start visible; it would be better if he started off as a relentless hitman of sorts. Perhaps the plan or reason is related to Caspian's father, [[Georgia Forrest]].
