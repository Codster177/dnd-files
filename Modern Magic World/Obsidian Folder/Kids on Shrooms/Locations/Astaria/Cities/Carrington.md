Carrington is the capital city of [[Astaria]]. It sits on the country's east coast, south of [[Hanlin]] and north of [[Lake Prospect]]'s bay, and nearly every major highway in eastern Astaria eventually feeds into it.

The city anchors the busiest stretch of the Astarian coast, with [[Auburn]] just inland and the swamp country around [[Lake Prospect]] beginning to its south.

The Concordant Encyclopedia, the text holding [[Astaria]]'s laws, is kept on display here, treated the way the United States treats the constitution in Washington D.C.
