Lake Prospect is a city in the swamp country of far eastern [[Astaria]], sitting on its bay south of [[Carrington]]. It is the home of [[West Paradise High School]] and the stage of the campaign.

Because [[Astaria]] has no states and government runs Local to Federal, Lake Prospect's mayor holds something closer to a governor's power over the city and its surrounding areas.

# SECRETS

[[Howard De Narris]] is the head [[Astaria#The HUMI|HUMI]] representative stationed in Lake Prospect and the surrounding town areas. He holds no direct position on the city council, but he meets with the mayor to keep a close eye on local politics.
