West Paradise High School is the public high school of [[Lake Prospect]], and the stage for most of the campaign.

## The School Year

The year follows [[The Aviran Calendar]]. School starts the first Monday of First Autumn and lets out about halfway through Second Autumn, after finals.

- **First Autumn** — school starts the first Monday of the month.
- **First Winter** — Winter Formal season.
- **Second Spring** — midterms, then Second Spring Break.
- **Second Summer** — prom season.
- **Second Autumn** — finals about halfway through, then the year ends.

That leaves First Spring, First Summer, and Second Winter as full vacation months, with the Christmas-like holidays landing in Second Winter.

## The School Week

School runs Monday through Thursday, on alternating schedules: Monday/Wednesday classes and Tuesday/Thursday classes. Students carry six periods (see the `Classes/` notes).
