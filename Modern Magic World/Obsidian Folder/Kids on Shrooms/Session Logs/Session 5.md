## Saga20 Recap:
The third day of school began with secrets weighing on every corner of West Paradise. The previous evening had scattered the five teenagers across their separate homes, each carrying fresh burdens: Benito Garcia (Mela) faced the wrath of his mother over a mysteriously shattered window, Caspian Forrest (Kai) and Ian Long (Aaron) endured a haunting midnight knock at the Forrest household, and Li JiaShen (Nick) returned to a pair of missed calls from the popular Rachel De Narris. By the time the morning bell rang, the threads of magic had already begun to pull them toward a collision in the fluorescent-lit halls of high school.

### Key Events

1. **Benito’s Homecoming and the Vines’ Testimony**

The Garcia household simmered with tension as Benito pulled into the driveway. His mother stood on the porch, phone in hand, her expression a storm of worry and anger. Inside, the broken window in his room had become a family crisis: his seven-year-old brother had wandered in, cut his hand on the glass, and Benito had vanished without a word. The confrontation was fierce, his mother’s accusations sharp, and Benito’s defenses stumbled until he remembered the strange vines that had once crept around the window frame. Desperate to prove he was not merely careless, he reached out with his nature magic, coaxing the dormant vines to regrow and cling to the wall. When his mother came to see, he pleaded with her to look, to believe that something unnatural had occurred. A tense persuasion, fueled by raw emotion, finally cracked her resolve. She relented, agreeing to have his father investigate the vines, and lifted the threat of grounding, though she extracted a promise that he would pick up his younger siblings from school. Benito retreated to his room, cradling Apricot the Chipmunk in his hands, the small creature offering squeaky solidarity as the plastic-covered window rattled gently in the night breeze.

2. **The Midnight Knock at Caspian’s House**

In the quiet dark of the Forrest home, Caspian stirred to a soft knock at his bedroom door. He ignored it at first, but a second, louder rap pulled him from sleep. Ian, who had been hiding under the bed after a day of revelations, heard it too. When Caspian opened the door, the hallway stood empty, the house silent save for a distant snore drifting up from downstairs. A quick, wandless detection spell revealed nothing beyond the familiar electromagnetic hum of his phone and the faint magical signature of Ian himself. Unnerved but exhausted, Caspian chalked it up to tired minds and returned to bed. Ian, ever the cautious ancient spirit in a teenager’s form, remained still beneath the bed until the small hours, then slipped out before dawn to walk to a 24-hour store called Marcel’s, his new phone clutched like a talisman.

3. **Shen’s Missed Calls and Birthday Plans**

At the Li household, Li JiaShen finally checked her phone and found two missed calls from Rachel De Narris. Rather than call back at the late hour, she sent a casual text, and Rachel’s reply came quickly: the calls had been accidental, but she wanted to discuss her upcoming birthday party. The conversation turned to plans for the sixteenth, a Saturday, and Rachel proposed using her father’s empty house rather than the beach house, an idea Shen supported. The exchange was light, filled with the easy banter of popular girls, but beneath it hummed a new awareness: Shen now knew that Rachel’s circle was not the only world worth watching. The day’s events had planted a seed of suspicion about Ian, and she went to sleep with schemes already forming.

4. **The Advanced Biology Confrontation**

Second period brought Shen and Ian together in Advanced Biology, a class they shared but had barely acknowledged. During the break, Shen noticed Ian fiddling with a phone she had never seen him hold before. Curiosity piqued, she approached with a deceptively friendly question about the new device. Ian’s answers were stilted, his demeanor defensive; he claimed his parents had saved up, that he was not good with technology. When Shen pressed, he grew flustered and blurted that he needed to get to class, then fled the room. Shen’s attempt to read his intentions resulted in a deadlocked contest of charisma, leaving her certain only that he was hiding something significant. The encounter confirmed her growing theory: Ian was not merely odd; he radiated the same otherworldly dissonance she had sensed from the great bee spirit, Cedrela.

5. **The Principal’s Office and an Unlikely Punishment**

Before Shen could process the encounter, a summons arrived: Principal Reed and a math teacher awaited her. The accusation was bullying, the evidence a report from an unnamed student. Shen’s heart raced as she realized the likely source was Caspian, but she masked her panic with practiced poise. Activating her innate ability to appear innocent, she delivered a contrite apology and watched the dynamics in the room shift. A high charisma roll revealed that the math teacher was the true driver of the complaint, while the principal seemed almost indifferent. The punishment handed down was bizarrely lenient: attend one meeting of the newly formed math club. When Shen asked who else would be there, the teacher mentioned the club president, Caspian Forrest. She left the office with a forced smile, already planning how to turn this forced proximity to her advantage.

6. **Caspian’s Calculus Discovery**

In his own first-period Calculus class, Caspian sat with his wand hidden in his backpack, his senses attuned to the magical spectrum. A subtle tingle drew his attention to Rachel De Narris, seated nearby. Focusing without his wand, he confirmed it: a faint but unmistakable magical aura clung to her. Emboldened, he attempted a series of small provocations, sliding her paper across the desk with an invisible nudge. She noticed, looking around in confusion, but could not trace the source. A second attempt to bump her chair failed, and the teacher caught Caspian rummaging in his bag. He talked his way out of trouble with a stammered excuse about a lost calculator, but the revelation was momentous: the queen bee of the popular clique was touched by magic, a secret that would soon ripple through the entire group.

7. **Marlowe’s Choir and the Song of Control**

In the choir room, Marlowe Melville sat between her friends Isabella Renner and Kayla Mendez, her stomach knotted with anxiety. The memory of setting Caspian’s car ablaze with a passionate rendition of “Sicko Mode” was still fresh, and she feared that any heartfelt singing might unleash chaos again. As warm-ups began, Isabella squeezed her hand in silent support. Marlowe sang, but cautiously, and in that careful restraint, she experienced a breakthrough. A critical success on a charisma check with magic revealed the mechanism of her power: the magic did not reside in the notes themselves but in the passion she poured into them. By consciously dampening that emotional surge, she could sing normally without triggering flames or force. She had found the key to control, and with it, a fragile peace settled over her as the harmonies swelled around her.

8. **Lunchtime: The Standoff and the Detection Web**

The cafeteria became a stage for a silent magical duel. Marlowe sat with Ian, tentatively asking for advice on how to approach Caspian about her powers. Ian, after a brief telepathic consultation with Caspian, assured her that Caspian would be open-minded. The three then gathered, joined by Caspian, and their conversation turned to math club. Shen entered the lunch line with Benito Garcia (Mela) behind her, both of them acutely aware of the trio across the room. Benito, carrying Apricot in his bag, cast a spell through the grass beneath his feet, feeling the magical signatures of all three friends. Caspian, his senses now razor-sharp, detected the probing touch and spun around, locking eyes with Benito and Shen. In that charged moment, Caspian’s own detection spell flared, revealing not only Benito’s magic but also the tiny aura of the chipmunk in his bag. He telepathically alerted Ian: Benito had a magical animal, and Shen was magical too. The standoff was complete; everyone now knew that everyone else was touched by the supernatural, though the precise nature of Ian’s spirit remained a secret held only by Shen.

9. **The Bus Ride and Shen’s Vision**

The school bus became a crucible of revelation. Shen chose to sit beside Marlowe, while Ian and Caspian took the seat across the aisle. The atmosphere was thick with unspoken knowledge. Shen, her mind racing, decided to push her perception to its absolute limit. She spent nearly all her remaining adversity tokens, exploding her charisma die repeatedly until she achieved an eight-critical success, a roll so potent the Dungeon Master ruled it magical. As the bus rumbled along, she glanced at the window and saw the truth laid bare in the reflections. Marlowe’s breath shimmered with melodic magic. Caspian was wreathed in a bright aura, a tether of energy linking him to his backpack. And Ian, in the glass, was not a lanky teenager but a magnificent red dragon, coiled and ancient. Shen’s poker face held, but inside she had just learned the deepest secret of them all. She now understood the full scope of the group’s powers and knew with certainty that Ian was a spirit of immense power.

10. **Marlowe’s Apartment and the Memory Rune**

After the bus dropped them off, Marlowe led Caspian and Ian into her family’s second-floor apartment. In her room, she produced the sketch she had made after her vision: a rune she had seen on the floor of an immigration office, the symbol she believed had stripped her of her childhood memories. Caspian studied the drawing with intense focus. His intelligence check confirmed it was a spell, far simpler than the complex runes he had been deciphering from his father’s papers. It was a forget spell, a manipulation of knowledge. He explained that he could likely cast it, but to reverse it and unearth repressed memories would require blending this magic with detection and creation principles, a far more difficult undertaking. He suggested that to truly understand the spell, he might need to cast it on a living subject first, a prospect that made everyone uneasy. The conversation then turned to Marlowe’s culture and her prayers to The Great Whale, a protective spirit of the sea, which Ian listened to with a flicker of recognition, sensing a kinship he could not yet articulate.

11. **The Invisible Intruder and the Truce**

A sudden thump from the patio shattered the discussion. Caspian instantly cast detect magic and rolled a staggering 27, piercing the veil of invisibility to reveal Shen crouched just inside the sliding door. He alerted the others telepathically. Ian reacted swiftly, attempting to summon a cloud to ensnare the intruder, but Shen’s dexterity exploded into a 25, allowing her to dodge the misty trap with preternatural grace. Ian called out, pleading for her to stop, and the confrontation shifted into a tense negotiation. Shen dropped her invisibility, and the four of them stood in the living room, the air crackling with adrenaline. Ian, his voice raw with fear, confessed that he was terrified of being discovered, that he believed people were out to hurt him. Shen, in turn, admitted that she had broken in because she felt left out, desperate to understand the secrets swirling around her. She revealed that she had seen his true form in the bus window, and she swore she would tell no one. Marlowe, after stumbling back in shock, used a newly discovered echolocation hum to confirm Shen’s physical presence, then extended an olive branch: magical people had to stick together. The tension dissolved into a fragile alliance, the four of them now bound by shared vulnerability.

12. **The Final Revelation**

As the group caught their breath, Caspian remembered a critical piece of information he had neglected to share at lunch. He looked at Shen, then at the others, and delivered the bombshell: Rachel De Narris had magic. The revelation hung in the air like a thunderclap, reshaping the shape of their secret world. The popular girl, the one planning a birthday party and moving through the school with effortless grace, was one of them. The session ended on that note, the five teenagers now scattered but connected, their individual quests converging into a single mystery.
