## Saga20 Recap:

The lingering warmth of a summer evening gave way to a night of discovery across the town of Lake Prospect. Marlowe Melville (Rylee), reeling from the psychic shock of unlocking suppressed childhood memories, sought answers and comfort from her father, Vincenzo Melville, over a humble spaghetti dinner, piecing together the trauma of her immigration and the sinister, rune-etched room that stripped away her past. Deep within Sweet Lake Forest, Benito Garcia (Mela) and Li JiaShen (Nick) navigated an unexpectedly delicate audience with Cedrela, the ancient Queen Bee Spirit, who bequeathed a honeyed token of alliance and a cryptic scroll to the fledgling witch, revealing that the line between the mundane and magical was far thinner than they imagined. Meanwhile, in Caspian Forrest (Kai)'s bedroom, the laws of reality were rewritten as Ian Long (Aaron) learned not only to forge a functional, crimson-hued smartphone from the very clouds above but also unlocked a direct telepathic link with Caspian, leading to a revelation that the electrical hum of the digital world and the arcane essence of wizardry were one and the same.

### Key Events

1. **A Daughter's Inquiry and a Father's Tale**

The evening found Marlowe Melville (Rylee) in the kitchen, assisting her father, Vincenzo Melville, while her mind churned with the deluge of restored memories. The weight of her recent revelation pressed upon her, and she carefully broached the subject of their family's harrowing immigration, probing the narrative with questions about forgotten details and the symbols she now vividly recalled. Her father, speaking with a mixture of nostalgia for their homeland of Nearidora and immense gratitude for the man who found her on the beach, recounted the night he was reunited with her at the Coast Guard harbor. The conversation was a delicate dance, with her father sensing an unspoken depth but ultimately trusting his daughter. A poorly-drawn sketch of a rune, copied from the memory-erasing chamber beneath the police station, remained a silent reminder of the conspiracy lurking beneath their new life, as a civics lesson revealed the near-total isolation their government enforced upon the continent of Orochi.

2. **An Audience with** the Queen Bee Spirit

In the twilight of the Sweet Lake Forest, Benito Garcia (Mela) and Li JiaShen (Nick) stood before the immense, refrigerator-sized hive, utterly dumbfounded as the squirrel-sized bee spirit, Cedrela, shifted into a human form wearing a black and yellow sundress. The initial interaction was marked by awkwardness; Cedrela, who had only ever spoken to the previous witch, Hazel Eyes, was clearly unused to conversing with ordinary people, while Shen was captivated by her very existence, blurting out an inquiry about her bee nature. The queen spoke of her friendship with Hazel Eyes and her duty to protect the woods, formally acknowledging Benito as the new witch. The chipmunk, Apricot, briefly wandered off only to return dejectedly, sporting a tiny bee sting as punishment for straying too far, a moment that showed the strange new dynamic between the sprawling natural world and the bewildered teenagers.

3. **Bequeathals of Honey and Mystery**

Cedrela proved a generous but pragmatic host, scooping magically clean handfuls of honey directly from the hive for Apricot, who accepted the sticky shower with a stiff, formal bow to the queen. For Shen, who lacked a container, the encounter took a bizarre turn as she transmuted his own hands into a flesh-and-bone bowl to receive the golden offering, a grotesque but successful feat of shape-shifting. For Benito, the queen retrieved a more significant gift: a diamond-shaped honeycomb and a twine-bound scroll penned by the late Hazel Eyes, alongside the promise that a mere bite of the comb would summon her aid. The mood, however, was comically undercut by the revelation that Apricot could not read, staring blankly at the scroll and declaring the scribbles nonsensical before admitting that Benito was, in fact, his witch and familiar.

Benito read the scroll privately, not letting Shen or Cedrela know the contents. The scroll read as follows:

"Dearest Benito, 

I am looking forward to meet you! I can only imagine how you might be feeling; I remember very little about my youth. I know I do not have much time left, and I am leaving you with a lot of my problems, but try not to worry, time will make everything easier. 

You are now the witch that protects this woods, and all woods beyond. Hopefully, you will have the seed of my tree, as planting this will sprout a new one! If you ever need a home, this is one you can always rely on. 

My first advice to all new witches is to trust in what you represent. For us, the forest will always have our backs. 

But, I know that most early witches begin to find their way once they find a familiar, so try to do this. Although, in my experience, familiars usually have a way of finding you. 

Be strong, trust in the woods, trust in yourself, and be careful with trusting more. Trusting my sisters too much have led to many regrets in my life. 

Thank you. By being who you are, you are fighting for what I have fought for my entire life. 

Hazel Eyes"

4. **A Flight of Folly and a Queen’s Repose**

As the forest darkened, Benito attempted a practical application of her supposed weather magic by asking a tree for a bowl, only to blush profusely when Cedrela simply ripped a chunk of bark free with her bare hands as a tool. After Shen managed to revert her hands from their bowl state with a surge of concentration, the group began their goodbyes, with Benito expressing sincere gratitude. In a spectacle of quiet power, the queen’s human form began to dissolve, her body becoming a host to hundreds of bees that enveloped her until she was nothing more than a single, tiny insect once more, who watched them depart before retreating into the hive. The walk back to the car was filled with stunned, repetitive affirmations of the absurd truth: they had just met a bee.

5. **Dinner with a Mother’s Fretful Eye**

At Caspian Forrest (Kai)'s home, the evening took a turn for domestic tension. During a simple dinner of chicken sandwiches and potato salad, Ian Long (Aaron) carefully navigated the alien ritual of a family meal, observing Caspian to learn how to eat the unfamiliar side dish before complimenting Jessica May on the cooking. The trouble began when the boys prepared to retreat upstairs; **Jessica**’s parental instincts flared at the inconsistent stories about how Ian would get home, her anxiety mounting until she was on the verge of demanding the truth. With a flash of panicked brilliance, Caspian pulled his mother aside and spun a flawless lie: Ian was mortified by his parents’ dilapidated car and didn't want her to see it. The lie landed perfectly, securing their privacy and leaving **Jessica** content not to pry further into her son’s kind-hearted, if odd, friend.

6. **The Cloud-Phone Materializes**

Upstairs, the true nature of their evening’s work began. Ian Long (Aaron), having studied Caspian's phone with an almost forensic intensity, decided to replicate the device through sheer will. He expressed a deep, longing desire for a phone, compressing wisps of cloud he pulled from the night sky into a solid, dark-red smartphone. While its initial functionality was limited to calling Caspian and playing a word puzzle game, the very act of its creation was a breakthrough. This sparked a lengthy tutoring session where Caspian, playing the role of a patient tech support agent, walked the dragon-boy through the fundamentals of social media, web browsers, and the all-important App Store, with Ian absorbing the concepts and willing the new features into his magical device until it achieved full, standard phone capabilities.

7. **Detecting the Arcane Current**

The atmosphere shifted from wonder to academic euphoria when Caspian analyzed the cloud-phone with his wand. He felt a familiar, faint magical energy radiating from the device, an energy he realized was identical to the waves emanating from his own phone and the computer on his desk. Following a cascade of deductions, Caspian arrived at a hypothesis: electricity itself was a method of transmitting magical energy, and the Internet was therefore an impossibly vast repository of magical information just waiting to be manipulated. This understanding crystalized into a new, permanent ability, a magic detection sense that allowed him to perceive the subtle arcane hum of all electronics, a discovery that left him pacing the room, verbally sketching the outlines of a unified theory of science and sorcery.

8. **From Signal to Telepathy**

Driven by his instinct to simply connect things, Ian Long (Aaron) observed the magical signal path between the phones and saw no reason why a device was necessary as an intermediary. With a surge of will, he grabbed the ethereal lightning bolt of the connection mid-air and redirected it, forcing the call straight into his own mind. Caspian’s voice resonated inside Ian's skull, a successful experiment that immediately evolved into a full telepathic link. The pair tested the limits of their new bond, with Ian correctly plucking the number 74 and the complex image of a dodecahedron from Caspian's thoughts, a silent exchange that promised a level of communication no one else could intercept.

9. **The Deluge of the Digital Sea**

Emboldened, Ian attempted to apply his “just connect it” philosophy on a larger scale. As Caspian looked on with a mix of concern and curiosity, Ian grabbed the magical link and physically plunged the crackling, thunderous energy directly into the tower of Caspian’s desktop computer. The result was instantaneous and catastrophic. The vast, infinite ocean of the Internet poured into his mind with the force of a tidal wave; billions of pages of information, images, and raw data flooded his consciousness, overwhelming his senses. A violent electrical shock seized his body before he could forcibly sever the connection, leaving him reeling with a minor injury and a hard-learned lesson about the sheer scale of the digital realm he was so eager to commune with.

10. **The Quiet Thoughts of a Restless Mind**

As the night stretched toward midnight, practical matters reasserted themselves. The pair crept downstairs, finding Jessica May mercifully asleep on the couch, allowing them to slip Ian out of the house without further interrogation. With a silent, telepathic agreement to maintain their mental link for secure communication, they finalized their plan for Ian’s pre-dawn return through the bedroom window. Back upstairs as Ian curled up in a bed of conjured clouds, he remained connected to Caspian's mind and was therefore hit with the full, unshielded force of the overthinker’s nightly ritual: a chaotic maelstrom of replaying every social interaction, re-litigating the moment he'd snitched on Shen, and a constant, exhausting calculation of how every person in his life perceived him, a deluge of neuroses that was, in its own way, as overwhelming as the Internet itself.
