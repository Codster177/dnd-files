Aviani is a second world nation in the world of [[Avira]], a small country folded into the large southern peninsula and surrounded by a larger, more developed neighbor that has yet to be named.

## Geography
Aviani holds a small band of tundra in the north around [[Port Moonbeam]], its only port. The rest of the country is dense hills and mountains.

The capital, [[Aviani Palace]], sits atop a mountain in the interior and can only be reached by the single trail that climbs from [[Port Moonbeam]].

## Technological and Scientific Level
Both [[Port Moonbeam]] and [[Aviani Palace]] run almost medieval on the technology scale, closer to [[Orochi]] than to the first world nations around them.

## Religion
[[Aviani Palace]] is a holy site of [[The Church of Abeus]], an elaborate castle said to be where the God-Emperor [[Abeus Algori]] once lived.

# SECRETS
Aviani is the successor state of [[The Grand Aviran Empire]]. In a sense nearly every nation off of [[Orochi]] is, since the Empire corrupted its way into all of their governments, but Aviani is different: it is the active headquarters of the [[Scholars of Avira]].

The country's medieval face is the coverup. [[Port Moonbeam]] presents itself as a sleepy pre-industrial harbor while functioning as a minor port for the Scholars and not much more.

The mountain beneath [[Aviani Palace]] is hollow. Inside it is one of the biggest cities in the world, home to the [[Scholars of Avira]]. The whole nation exists so the Scholars can have a home that goes undetected.
