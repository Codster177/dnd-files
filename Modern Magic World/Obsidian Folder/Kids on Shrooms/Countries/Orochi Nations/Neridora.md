Neridora is a nation of the [[Orochi]] continent, sitting at the northwestern head of the continent's long southern arc, across the water from [[Astaria]]'s eastern coast. Its names follow a Vietnamese and Moroccan basis.

As an Orochi nation, Neridora sits behind the [[Developed International Concord]]'s embargo of 1511, cut off from trade, travel, and immigration with the developed world.

## Geography
The country is deep swamp from end to end. [[Safilari]], the capital, sits on its southern edge, and [[Arez]] is its northern town.

The city state of [[Zimino]] borders it to the east, the gateway to the rest of the Orochi arc.
