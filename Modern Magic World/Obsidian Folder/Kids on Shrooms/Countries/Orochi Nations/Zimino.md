Zimino is a city state on the [[Orochi]] continent, sitting immediately east of [[Neridora]] at the head of the long arc of nations that runs down the continent's south. The nation amounts to the [[City of Zimino]] and the deep swamp around it.

As part of [[Orochi]], Zimino sits behind the [[Developed International Concord]]'s embargo of 1511, cut off from trade, travel, and immigration with the developed world.

## Geography
Deep swampland in every direction. The city is the gateway between [[Neridora]] and the rest of the Orochi arc, and like everywhere on the continent its roads are dirt trails; no highways run here.
