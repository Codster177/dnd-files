Palicia is a nation of the [[Orochi]] continent, a heart-shaped island in the far northeast of [[Avira]]. Its names follow a Greek basis, and the island wears them bluntly: [[Agapi]], love, in the north, and [[Thanatos]], death, in the south.

As an Orochi nation, Palicia sits behind the [[Developed International Concord]]'s embargo of 1511, cut off from trade, travel, and immigration with the developed world.

## Geography
An active volcano rises at the island's north-center, and the northern half of Palicia is ashen because of it. The south stays green and forested.

[[Thanatos]], the capital, sits in the south. [[Agapi]] sits in the north under the volcano's ash.

Off the island's western shore lies a small archipelago of unclaimed territories.
