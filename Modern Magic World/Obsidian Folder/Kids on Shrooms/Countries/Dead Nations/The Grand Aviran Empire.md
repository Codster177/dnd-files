The Grand Aviran Empire is the dead empire the modern world is built on, and its founding is the hinge of the calendar itself: year 0. AV counts the years since the Empire's founding, and BA the years before it (see [[The Aviran Calendar]]).

The Empire fought and won [[Era 5 - The Severing War]] by casting [[The Nullcasting]], then fell as a public power. Its core never died; it stepped into the hollow mountain of [[Aviani]] and continues today as the [[Scholars of Avira]].
