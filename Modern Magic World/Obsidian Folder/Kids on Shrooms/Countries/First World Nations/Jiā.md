Jiā is a first world country in the world of [[Avira]].

## Geography
Jiā occupies the western half of the northeastern subcontinent, separated from the mainland by a strait. Tundra covers its north and true arctic its northeast, with the country turning green along the south.

Its two named cities sit on the southern coast: [[Dǎogào]], the capital, with [[Méilí]] just to its west. More cities exist but are not named yet.

Four towns are named. [[Tiānmén]] sits on the southern coast between Méilí and Daogao, and [[Diéyuán]] lies on the coastal hillside of the west. Northward, [[Báisōng]] sits among the snowy pine forests of the tundra, and [[Dōngyè]] is the northernmost settlement in the country, out in the true arctic.

[[Wàibù]] holds the other half of the subcontinent across Jiā's eastern land border. Across the strait to the south lies [[Astaria]]'s northern coast at [[Kenosha]].

## Organization Structure
Jiā is a representative theocracy, with the true ruler of the nation being the head of religious state.
