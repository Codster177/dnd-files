Wàibù is a first world country in the world of [[Avira]]. It holds the eastern half of the northeastern subcontinent it shares with [[Jiā]], along with a second landmass across a narrow strait to its east.

The name follows the same Chinese linguistic basis as Jiā's (wàibù, "outside" or "external"), a pointed thing to be called when you are Jiā's only land neighbor.

## Geography
Tundra covers Wàibù's north and forested green country its south, with a single mountain cluster at the nation's center.

Its capital sits in the south, tied into a highway network, though neither the capital nor any of its other settlements are named yet.

[[Jiā]] lies across the western land border.
