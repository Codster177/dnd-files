Rane is a first world nation in the world of [[Avira]].

## Geography
Rane is desert country: yellow sands and dry savanna with a stretch of brown badlands dead in its center. Its south and southwestern borders grow heavy with palms, turning to rainforest and jungle at the edges.

The capital sits northeast of the country's center, and the country is threaded with highways, but none of its settlements are named yet.

[[Astaria]] lies to the east, the border running partly along the great mountain chain and partly between the two countries' desert cities, with [[Goodsprings]] sitting right on the line. No highway crosses the border; the two are in a quiet, passive conflict.

To the north sits the large unnamed arctic neighbor. Rane's highways connect to it, and of the two southern powers Rane is the closer to being its ally.

## Organization Structure
Rane is a pure Oligarchy, with a small council of people who rule the country.
