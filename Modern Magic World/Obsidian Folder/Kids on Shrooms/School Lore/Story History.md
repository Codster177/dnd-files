## Benito, Rachel, and the Erasure of Milani Toll:

In middle school, [[Rachel De Narris]], [[Milani Toll]], and [[Lexi Conner]] were a trio of friends, and [[Benito Garcia (Mela)|Benito]] was Rachel's friend from those same years. Milani was Rachel's best friend for a long while.

At the end of freshman year (Second Autumn, 1619), [[Madison Leclair]] entered Rachel's circle. She started off as just a part of it.

At Rachel's 16th birthday party (First Autumn, 1620), she and Benito started dating. The relationship was real and it was good: Benito dated the Rachel from before the curse, and Milani became pretty good friends with him along the way.

At the end of Second Summer 1620, the girls went to prom together, some with dates and some without, and Rachel brought Benito. After prom Benito went home, and Rachel, Milani, Lexi, and Madison went back to the De Narris manor for the night.

While the girls slept, [[Howard De Narris]] captured Milani. He had realized, either before that day or during it, that she was a [[Sorcerers|sorcerer]] who had not yet come into her powers. In a secret room somewhere in the mansion, he dissected and experimented on her with [[Damien De Narris]].

Damien did not seal the entrance to the room correctly, and Rachel witnessed what was happening inside. It was not her first encounter with that room or with [[Magic]], so a simple mind erase would no longer hold: Howard placed the advanced, permanent curse on her instead (the Continuous casting of [[Modify Memory]]). The curse is the entire reason Rachel began changing.

Then Milani was erased. The [[Scholars of Avira]] hold a captured demi-god level spirit of erasure and oblivion, and people who need to be completely removed from the world's memory are fed to it; the spirit rewrites them out of the world (see [[The Spirit of Erasure and Oblivion]]). Most people forgot Milani entirely. The closer someone was to her, the more thoroughly they were scrubbed; Benito, Lexi, and even Madison keep only the smallest fragments, a name or a photo that feels familiar, though time could bring it all back. Strangers keep incidental glimpses precisely because they had no relationship to lose: a shop owner remembers Rachel walking in once with some girl.

When Damien was putting Rachel back in her room, using magic to carry her, Madison caught him with his wand out. She made a deal: say nothing, and keep an eye on Rachel. Damien has granted her powers as part of it. Madison caught the magic, not the murder; she does not know Milani was killed, and a friend vanishing without answers does not sit well with her, whatever she gained.

In the aftermath the group quietly reshaped itself. Lexi retracted a little, without being able to name why, but stayed close; under the dumb act Rachel likes her to wear, Lexi is smart, a little cold and calculated, and Rachel is a great connection to have. [[Li JiaShen (Nick)|Shen]] joined the group towards the end of Rachel's sophomore year (Second Autumn, 1620), a freshman at the time, and is now the fourth of the group. Madison reached best friend status around the end of sophomore year (Second Winter, 1620), after the curse and after Milani.

Through those months Benito watched his girlfriend turn into someone else. At the beginning of First Summer 1621, after a day of pushback from Rachel, the two ended up alone in her room and Benito pressed her on why she had been so different. It became a fight, Rachel shouting in his face to let it go, Benito persisting that he needed to know where the person he fell in love with went. She snapped and yelled "I never loved you." After his stunned silence, she added "but we can still date," in an inflection that meant they could still fuck and still be seen in public together. Benito broke up with her and stormed out of her house.

A couple weeks later, during that same First Summer, Rachel began dating [[Jace Shotwell]].

Two rumors spread through [[West Paradise High School]] after the breakup. Madison started the rumor that Benito cheated on Rachel. The rumor that Rachel cheated on Benito started on its own after Rachel began dating Jace.

They have been apart for a month now. Benito sees Rachel in a parking lot and sprints ([[Session 1]]); he trades advisement assignments to stay out of her study hall ([[Session 2]]). Neither of them knows the truth about why she changed.
