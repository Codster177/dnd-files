Important ruling on time travel is how the timeline is handled. This campaign will aim for a mutable timeline, one that has a central timeline where people and entities can travel forward and back in time and change events. It is typically best to keep things as unmutated as possible, as changing events will spawn paradoxes and altercations. However, it is certainly possible.

Many worlds is also considered in this approach, but instead of many timelines all existing at the same time, this world more so takes the idea of one central timeline, with other erased and possible timelines, with various possibilities of remembering, bringing back, or even temporarily splitting and recreating other timelines.

There are different forms of time travel both forward and back in time. These forms differ in origin and magic type. Here, they will be organized by origin with traveling back and traveling forward covered.

## High Magic with Conjuration:

Utilizing high magic to physically travel in time is very high in magical cost. Chronomantic magic is extremely rare with only a handful of wizards in the world knowing the simplest of [[Chronomancy Spells]]. Traveling through time is a feat only few entities are capable of, let alone wizards and sorcerers. Legendary sources of time travel included Time Dragons, powerful 9th level rituals, and boons from gods to serve a divine mission.



## High Magic with Divination:

Instead of using magic to physically allow travelers to go through time, spells of this nature send information through time. Similar to high magic with conjuration, this manner of spells too is extremely rare and is taxing, with the need for very strong magic users for casting.
Although this magic does not send travelers physically, travelers report similar sensations to being present in the past and future when executing these spells. Travelers to the past send the information contained in their conscious self to the past, rewriting how the past version of themselves understood and perceived the world. Some may only send limited details, with others effectively transferring the entire consciousness to the new time period.
