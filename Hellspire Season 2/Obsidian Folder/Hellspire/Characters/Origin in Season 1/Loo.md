### Backstory

Loo is a colossal **dragon turtle** and the father of [[Oogloo]]. He was the very creature summoned by a dying merfolk priest at the start of Season 1 — the beast that tore apart [[Camila Aleleaf]]'s ship and set the whole story in motion.

### Season 1 Events

**The Ship's Destruction.** Answering a merfolk priest's dying call, Loo destroyed the pirate ship carrying [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], scattering the survivors and beginning their journey.

**Return of Almint.** After [[Lord Gallium]]'s defeat, Loo approached the beach alongside his son [[Oogloo]], bearing the monstrous body of [[Almint Freia|Almint]] up from the sea floor — allowing the party to hold his funeral and recover the **second vault key**.

### Relationships
