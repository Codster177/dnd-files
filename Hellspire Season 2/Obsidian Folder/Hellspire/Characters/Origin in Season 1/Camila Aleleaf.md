### Backstory

Camila Aleleaf was a **captain of the Damned Guild** who commanded the pirate ship aboard which [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] were recruited, only days before Season 1 began.

### Season 1 Events

**The Dragon Turtle.** As Aleleaf's ship neared the City of the Damned, it was ambushed by merfolk and a merfolk priest, whose dying call summoned a **Dragon Turtle** that tore the ship apart. As Aleleaf fled toward the escape rowboat, [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] **vortex-warped her deep into the ocean**, ensuring she never reached it.

**Condemned and Executed.** Aleleaf survived, only to be captured and condemned to death by [[Lord Gallium]] for losing her ship and crew. At the city's church, as [[Griffin Bullethole]] prepared to execute her, she named [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] as her former crew — turning the pirates against them — before Griffin killed her.
