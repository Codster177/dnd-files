### Backstory

Frink is an artist at heart who makes his living as a **potion seller**. His wares are unlabeled and of mysterious effect, and he is given to all but begging passersby to buy them. Despite his eccentricity, Frink proved a recurring friend to the party, turning up where he was needed with healing potions in hand.

*(Frink's origins and how he came by his strange brews are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The City of the Damned.** In the marketplace, Frink pressed his unlabeled potions on the party at a steep discount. Among them was the **Tonic of God's Touch** — which would later let [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] summon **Asmodeus** himself — along with a Philter of Cold Resistance and a Draught of the Berserker.

**Lootbridge.** Frink resurfaced among the resistance holding out in the Lootbridge cathedral, where he supplied [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and [[Malm]] with healing potions for the coming battle.
