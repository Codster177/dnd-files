### Backstory

Lord Gallium was the secret ruler of the **Damned Guild**, a vast pirate organization operating out of the City of the Damned. Though few had ever seen him and fewer still knew his name, Gallium was in truth a **mind flayer**, orchestrating an illithid scheme to harvest brains and breed tadpoles beneath the city. He ruled through fear and a network of Pirate Lieutenants, holding the two keys to his vault at **Safekeeps** and plotting to extend his reach across the world.

*(Gallium's origins, his colony, and how he came to rule the Damned Guild are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Unseen Hand.** Gallium first touched the party through [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s mind — *"I see, royalty hmmm…"* — as they cleared the Cranium Rats beneath the Broken-Bone Tavern. He had [[Almint Freia|Almint]] abducted and infested with a **tadpole**, opening a window into the party's movements.

**The Lieutenants and the Dinner.** Gallium condemned **Captain** [[Camila Aleleaf]] to execution for losing her ship and crew. He later hosted the party at the **Damned Manor**, revealing himself as a mind flayer and serving the cooked brain of [[Violence]]. When the party refused to become his lieutenants, he ordered [[Barnacle]] to kill them — then **disintegrated Barnacle** himself in a flash of green when the sorcerer failed.

**The Imposter Prince.** Gallium reanimated and puppeted [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s body, parading him as the living prince to turn the Knights of Freia against the Hellbringers, before the deception was exposed aboard [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]]'s ship.

**The Nautiloid and His End.** Gallium commanded a flying **nautiloid**, which he brought against [[Ig]] and the royal ship. When the party boarded, he sprang a trap with his fallen lieutenants remade into mind flayer abominations. As he loosed a ray of **disintegration** at [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], [[Kix]] threw himself into its path, grabbed hold of Gallium, and let the energy consume them both — reducing the pirate lord to ash.
