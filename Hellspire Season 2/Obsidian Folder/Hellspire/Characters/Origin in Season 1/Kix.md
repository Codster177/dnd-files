### Backstory

Kix was the lone other survivor of [[Camila Aleleaf]]'s ship alongside [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]. Once bearing a hook for a hand and blind in one eye, he drifted in and out of the party's story before becoming a resistance leader — and ultimately giving his life to destroy the pirate lord.

*(Kix's past and how he came to the Damned Guild are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Wreck and Early Days.** Kix escaped the dragon-turtle wreck with [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], was rescued by [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s yacht, then split off in the City of the Damned. He rejoined briefly for the smithy ambush before departing north toward the **Bruibary Monarchy**.

**The Lootbridge Resistance.** Kix resurfaced in ruined **Lootbridge City**, finishing off a mind flayer and leading the party to a resistance holding out in a cathedral. He had taken [[Badger]] "prisoner" — though Badger's shackles were illusion.

**Eternal Life.** [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]'s **Wish** named Kix among those to retain their sense of self and gain **eternal life**, restoring his lost hand and sight in an instant.

**The Sacrifice.** Aboard [[Lord Gallium]]'s nautiloid, when Gallium loosed a ray of disintegration at [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], Kix **threw himself into its path**, held the energy in his chest, rushed Gallium, and grabbed hold — the disintegration consuming them both. Gallium was reduced to ash; Kix **vanished without a trace**. Given his wished eternal life, his true fate remains uncertain.
