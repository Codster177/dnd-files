### Backstory

Myla Skipplestump was [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]'s mother and the wife of [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]] — freckled, with a kind face, green eyes, and a thin figure. In her adventuring days she was a **warlock** who, like her son would later, signed her soul away to a devil. When she tried to leave that life and break her pact, the devil refused and began **possessing her** in fits. Fearing she would one day harm Aji or Gabble during one of these episodes, Myla **took her own life**. She died when Gabble was 14.

### Season 1 Events

**The Prophetic Dream.** Myla appeared to [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] in a dream, knitting outside his childhood home. She asked, *"Was it worth it?"* before fire erupted behind her melting eyes — foreshadowing both her own warlock pact and the melting of Gabble's left eye.

**Reunion in Stygia.** When **Asmodeus** finally claimed [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]'s body at Safekeeps, Gabble's consciousness returned to his soul on the cliffs of **Stygia**, where Myla's soul waited beside him. She revealed the truth of her pact and her suicide. When Gabble asked what they should do now, she answered, *"There is nothing to do now,"* and they returned together to the **River Styx**.
