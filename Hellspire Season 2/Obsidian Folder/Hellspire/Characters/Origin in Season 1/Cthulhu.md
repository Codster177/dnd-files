### Backstory

Cthulhu is a vast **eldritch entity**, a Great Old One dwelling beyond the world. Unknown to [[Almint Freia|Almint]], it became the **true source of his cleric powers**: having never devoutly worshipped any god, Almint's magic quietly began drawing its strength from Cthulhu once he reached the City of the Damned.

### Season 1 Events

**The Hidden Patron.** After the party escaped the pirate-overrun ship, [[Almint Freia|Almint]] received a vision from Cthulhu and realized the truth of where his power came from.

**"Not yet."** Upon Almint's final death, his soul ascended into space, witnessing the ages of the world pass before coming face to face with Cthulhu. The entity cast his soul toward a **pit of endless torment** — only to retract it at the last moment with the words *"Not yet,"* sparing Almint for some unknown purpose.

### Relationships
