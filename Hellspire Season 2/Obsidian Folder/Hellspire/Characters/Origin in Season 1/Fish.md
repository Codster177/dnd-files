### Backstory

Fish was a mute **plasmoid** brute and the **true second-in-command** of [[Lord Gallium]] — the real power behind the title [[Barnacle]] falsely claimed. Embedded in the center of his translucent chest was a genuine, magical **vault key**. He wielded the **Greataxe of Certain Death** and could unleash bursts of **wild magic**. He was also the subject of [[Malm]]'s century-long investigation.

*(Fish's origins and his rise under Gallium are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**Beneath the Manor.** Fish first overwhelmed the party in Gallium's lair, holding the severed head of [[Gus]] as he beat both [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] unconscious.

**The Sinking Ship.** Fish broke into [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]]'s royal ship with his greataxe, and a critical blow **split the ship in two**, unleashing a storm of wild magic as it sank. In the chaos, [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] finally **killed him** and claimed the **Greataxe of Certain Death** — the very axe from his prophetic dream. The vault key was recovered from his body.
