### Backstory

Ig is an **emerald dragon** who was captured by [[Lord Gallium]] and imprisoned in a transformation chamber deep within the Damned Manor's lair, where the mind flayers pumped tadpoles into his head.

*(Ig's life before his capture is left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**Freed from the Chamber.** Investigating Gallium's lair beneath the ruined Sunken Arena, [[Malm]] found Ig in the transformation chamber and **removed the tadpoles** from his head, freeing him. Grateful, Ig tunneled the party out of the underground and became their flying mount and ally.

**Servant and Steed.** Ig carried the party to **Lootbridge City** and ferried them toward the royal ship. In the campaign's climax he engaged [[Lord Gallium]]'s flying **nautiloid** in single combat, and tunneled the party aboard the enemy vessel — though he could not follow them through its strange material.
