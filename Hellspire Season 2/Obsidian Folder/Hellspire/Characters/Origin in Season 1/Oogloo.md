### Backstory

Oogloo is a **baby dragon turtle**, the son of the great dragon turtle **[[Loo]]**. Captured by the Damned Guild, he was kept as a weapon to be loosed upon gladiators in the Sunken Arena.

*(Oogloo's capture and early life are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Arena.** [[The Treasurer]] released Oogloo into the arena to destroy the party, but [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] **calmed the creature** and helped him cough up a lodged **javelin** — Deyrale's own, thrown into the sea during the first dragon turtle attack. With Oogloo's help the party destroyed the arena and escaped to a cove, where the dragon turtle returned to the ocean.

**The Funeral.** After Gallium's fall, Oogloo returned with his father **[[Loo]]**, who bore the body of [[Almint Freia|Almint]] up from the sea floor for the party's funeral.
