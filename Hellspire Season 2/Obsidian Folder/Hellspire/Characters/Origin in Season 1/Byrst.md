### Backstory

Byrst is an **amnizu devil** who serves as a **representative of Asmodeus** — not a deity himself, but an emissary who handles the Archdevil's pacts and bargains on the mortal plane. He answers the call of the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** when summoned by Asmodeus's servants.

### Season 1 Events

**The Pact.** [[Badger]] used the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** to summon Byrst, who negotiated [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]'s **warlock pact** with Asmodeus, taking his soul.

**Disfigurement and the Gift.** When [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] was infested with a tadpole, Byrst was summoned again and, with [[Badger]], **burned away the left side of Gabble's face** to kill the parasite — both wearing evil grins. Byrst later left Gabble a **glowing eye** set in the melted ruin of his face, calling it a "gift," along with a note.

### Relationships

- **Asmodeus** — The Archdevil Byrst represents; Gabble mistook Byrst for his true patron until Asmodeus himself appeared.
- **[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]** — Bound to Asmodeus through Byrst; recipient of his glowing-eye "gift."
- **[[Badger]]** — The warlock who repeatedly summons Byrst through the Brazier of Hellish Fire.
