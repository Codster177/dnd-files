### Backstory

Badger is a goblin barkeep who ran the **Broken-Bone Tavern** in the City of the Damned — and, secretly, a powerful **warlock of Asmodeus**. Beneath his tavern he kept a ritual room dedicated to the Archdevil and a check-in book that let him **scry** on anyone whose name it held. Patient and manipulative, Badger maneuvered events from the shadows toward a single end: the opening of a permanent Gate to Hell.

### Season 1 Events

**The Tavern and the Pact.** Badger hired the party to clear an infestation beneath his tavern and had them sign his scrying book. When [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] asked about his powers, Badger led him to the basement, used the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** to summon [[Byrst]], and brokered Gabble's **warlock pact** with Asmodeus.

**The Puppeteer.** Badger recovered the infested [[Almint Freia|Almint]] by scrying, then set the party to hunting the Pirate Lieutenants for the two vault keys. He executed [[Violence]] before she could speak, and detected the tadpole in [[Almint Freia|Almint]] before casting the party out of his tavern.

**Disfigurement and Survival.** When [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] was infested, Badger summoned [[Byrst]] and the two **burned away the left side of Gabble's face** to kill the parasite, smiling as they worked. Badger survived the burning of his own tavern by turning into a **raven**.

**Lootbridge and the Brazier.** In Lootbridge, Badger let himself be "captured" by the resistance — his shackles mere illusion — and revealed his plan to Gabble: use the resistance to seize Gallium's keys. He handed Gabble the **Brazier of Hellish Fire**, warning that resisting his infernal calling would only bring worse.

**The Gate.** At Safekeeps, Badger arrived to ensure the ritual succeeded, transforming into a **giant ape** and **hurling [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] into the Nine Hells**. With his help, the possessed [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] opened the Gate to Hell.

### Relationships

- **Asmodeus** — The Archdevil Badger serves; his every scheme advances the opening of the Gate.
- **[[Byrst]]** — The amnizu devil Badger summons to do Asmodeus's bidding.
- **[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]** — Badger's chief instrument; he initiated Gabble's pact and pressed him toward the Gate.
- **[[Lord Gallium]]** — A rival power in the City of the Damned with his own designs.
- **[[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]** — Hurled into the Nine Hells by Badger.
- **[[Kix]]** — The man who once held Badger prisoner in Lootbridge.
