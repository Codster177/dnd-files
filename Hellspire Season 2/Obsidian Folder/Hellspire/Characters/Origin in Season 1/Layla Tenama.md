### Backstory

Layla Tenama was a captain of the **Knights of Freia** and, by her own history, [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s **childhood best friend**. When the prince vanished in the City of the Damned, she led Freia's forces in his name — though she harbored growing suspicions about the "Almint" who reappeared. By Season 2 she has risen to become **Queen Layla Tenama of New Freia**, the monarch the Fellowship of the Gate serves.

### Season 1 Events

**Suspicion of the Prince.** Captain Layla had distrusted the reappeared [[Almint Freia|Almint]] since arriving at the City of the Damned. When the party was brought before her, they confirmed her fears: Almint was controlled by [[Lord Gallium]]. Confronting him triggered the abomination's reveal and a massive battle across the royal ship.

**The Sinking Ship.** Layla fought through the battle as [[Fish]] broke into the ship and split it in two. The party saved her from the sinking wreck, and she stood with them through the final clashes.

**The Vault.** Layla traveled with the party to **Safekeeps** and fought beside [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and [[Malm]] to stop the possessed [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] — but the ritual succeeded and the Gate to Hell opened. She survived to carry on into Season 2.
