### Backstory

Dama was an enslaved gladiator forced to fight in the **Sunken Arena** — and, like [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]], a **warlock**.

*(Dama's life before her enslavement and the nature of her pact are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**Rescue.** The party freed Dama from the Sunken Arena during their escape and brought her to their cove base, where it was revealed she was a warlock.

**Taken and Lost.** When the party returned to Gallium's lair and freed [[Ig]], Dama was **quietly taken by creatures of the lair** without the party noticing. That night [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] dreamed of her being **dissected to death by mind flayers** — a vision that felt true — and the party reluctantly chose not to search for her.
