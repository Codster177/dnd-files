### Backstory

Violence was a **Pirate Lieutenant** of the Damned Guild and the sister of [[Peace]]. Unlike most of Gallium's lieutenants, she sought to **undo the pirate lord's corruption** from within, and showed unexpected mercy.

*(Violence's turn against Gallium and her bond with her sister are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**Freeing Almint.** Working in secret, Violence broke [[Almint Freia|Almint]] free from the flesh cage beneath the Damned Manor and left him where [[Badger]] could scry and recover him.

**Pleading for Peace.** Violence arrived at the smithy fight pleading for her sister [[Peace]]'s life, but an enraged [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] killed Peace anyway, and Violence refused to aid the party further.

**Execution.** The party brought Violence to [[Badger]], who **executed her before she could plead her case**. [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] buried her on the coast. [[Lord Gallium]] later served her **cooked brain** as the main course at his dinner.
