### Backstory

The Treasurer was the cruel owner of the **Sunken Arena**, a gladiator pit where enslaved fighters battled for the entertainment of the pirate guild. He reveled in the bloodshed and sold the audience **cannon shots** to fire upon the combatants.

### Season 1 Events

**The Arena.** The Treasurer presided over the gladiator battle in which [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and [[Malm]] were forced to fight, gleefully promoting cannon shots against the fighters. He loosed a baby dragon turtle — later named [[Oogloo]] — into the pit, but [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] calmed the creature and used it to destroy the arena and escape.

**The Rematch.** When the party returned through the tunnels beneath the ruined arena, they encountered the Treasurer again and **defeated him in a fierce rematch** on their way deeper into Gallium's lair.
