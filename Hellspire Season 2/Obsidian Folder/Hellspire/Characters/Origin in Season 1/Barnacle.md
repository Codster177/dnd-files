### Backstory

Barnacle was a **sorcerer** who claimed to be [[Lord Gallium]]'s **second-in-command** — a boast that was not, in truth, accurate. He carried one of the vault keys as bait to lure the party to Gallium's dinner.

*(Barnacle's role in the Damned Guild is left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Invitation.** Barnacle met the party at the abandoned smithy, presenting himself as Gallium's second-in-command and showing them a vault key to entice them to the **Damned Manor** dinner. (His key was later revealed to be a fake — disintegrated harmlessly, unlike the real magical keys.)

**Disintegration.** At the dinner, when the party declined Gallium's offer, the pirate lord ordered Barnacle to kill them. As Barnacle faltered in the fight, [[Lord Gallium]] **disintegrated him** in a flash of green light and rose from his chair himself.
