### Backstory

Asmodeus is the **Archdevil and supreme ruler of the Nine Hells**, the true power behind the warlock pacts struck in the City of the Damned. Though his servants [[Badger]] and the devil [[Byrst]] act in his name, Asmodeus himself holds the souls bound to him — and works through patient manipulation toward the opening of a permanent Gate to Hell and, ultimately, the end of the world.

*(Asmodeus's broader cosmology and aims unfold further in Season 2.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Soul.** When [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] forfeited his soul through [[Byrst]], it was Asmodeus — not the devil emissary — who truly claimed it.

**The Deal.** Desperate aboard the royal ship, [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] drank the **Tonic of God's Touch** expecting Byrst; instead **Asmodeus himself** emerged. He offered power to defeat [[Lord Gallium]] in exchange for Gabble's **body and mind**, to be possessed indefinitely whenever he chose. When Gabble hesitated, Asmodeus dragged him to **Stygia**, the fifth layer of Hell, and left him to freeze until he signed — granting him a contained **Wish** in return.

**Claiming the Vessel.** In the vault at Safekeeps, Asmodeus **claimed Gabble's body**, pulling his consciousness back to Stygia (where the soul of [[Myla Skipplestump]] waited) while his possessed form opened the **Gate to Hell**.

### Relationships

- **[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]** — Holds his soul, body, and mind; his chosen vessel.
- **[[Byrst]]** — His amnizu emissary who brokers pacts in his name.
- **[[Badger]]** — A mortal warlock who serves his ends in the City of the Damned.
- **[[Myla Skipplestump]]** — Another soul bound to him, now drifting in his realm.
- **Cthulhu** — A rival cosmic power whose touch fell on [[Almint Freia|Almint]].
