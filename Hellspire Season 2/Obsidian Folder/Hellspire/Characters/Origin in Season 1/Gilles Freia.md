### Backstory

Gilles Freia is the **King of [[Freia]]** and adoptive father of **Prince** [[Almint Freia|Almint]]. When he fell gravely ill, his son set out across the sea to the City of the Damned in search of a cure — the journey that drew Almint into the Hellbringers and the whole of Season 1's events.

### Season 1 Events

Gilles does not appear directly during Season 1, but his illness is the cause that sets [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s voyage in motion. He appears in Almint's **prophetic dream**, lying on his deathbed and asking *"Who are you?"* before sinking into an inky mass — a vision foreshadowing the tadpole's conquest of Almint's body. Whether a cure ever reached him is unresolved.
