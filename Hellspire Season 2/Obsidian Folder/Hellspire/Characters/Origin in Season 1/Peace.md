### Backstory

Peace was a mute **dwarf** and a trusted **Pirate Lieutenant** of the Damned Guild, one of the two lieutenants entrusted with a key to [[Lord Gallium]]'s vault. She ran the **Water-Blade Smithy** in the City of the Damned and was the sister of the lieutenant [[Violence]].

*(Peace's life and her bond with her sister are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Ambush.** The party tried to ambush Peace in her smithy, but she discovered them and got the drop on them instead. In the fight, [[Almint Freia|Almint]] fell and died after a botched medicine check by [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] (though the parasite revived him). When her sister [[Violence]] arrived pleading for peace, an enraged Deyrale **killed Peace anyway**, believing she had slain Almint.
