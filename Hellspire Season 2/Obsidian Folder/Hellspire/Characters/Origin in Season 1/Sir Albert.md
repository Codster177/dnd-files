**Kidnapped and Convinced.** Sir Albert spotted the party as they slipped out of the captured encampment, and they kidnapped him aboard [[Ig]] bound for Lootbridge. On the flight, the party convinced him that [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s accusations were lies crafted by [[Lord Gallium]].
