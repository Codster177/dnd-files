### Backstory

Griffin Bullethole was a **Pirate Lieutenant** of the Damned Guild — a staggeringly tall **Giff** who wielded a blunderbuss and commanded pirates in the City of the Damned.

### Season 1 Events

**The Execution.** At the city's church, Griffin commanded the assembled pirates and carried out the execution of **Captain** [[Camila Aleleaf]] on [[Lord Gallium]]'s orders. When Aleleaf named [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] as her crew, Griffin turned the pirates on them.

**The Tunnels.** Griffin pursued the fleeing [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] into the fleshy tunnels beneath the church. Though the two were gravely wounded, they **killed him** there.
