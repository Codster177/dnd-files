### Backstory

Fel Sinderheim was born Fel Sirdhemion, the child of a forbidden romance between Princess Miabella Galanor, an elven princess of the Evergreen Kingdom, and Din Sirdhemion. The couple fled the kingdom, hired a crew, and turned to piracy when their money ran out. Eventually the crew was caught, and Fel was separated from his parents. Deemed an improper heir and a threat to the throne, he was imprisoned in the deepest dungeon of the Evergreen Kingdom as a child. He grew old and died there, his only companions the rats that lived among him.

After death, Fel's soul entered a rare judgment state. Having never dedicated himself to a deity and having lived too little of a life to be claimed by any domain, he was exhaled from Sigil and cast randomly into the universe. He was eventually captured by Charon, ruler of the River Styx, and pulled into that realm. For several thousand years, Fel existed as a lost soul drifting through the Styx.

His existence changed when [[Aji Skipplestump]] fell into the river. Aji had been searching Hell for his wife Myla and his son [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]], and in desperation reached into the Styx to grab a soul. Fel's spirit entered Aji's body instead, taking control. Now Fel inhabits Aji's gnomish form, slowly uncovering the man's past and the desperate pleas Aji makes from within for Fel to fulfill his goals.
