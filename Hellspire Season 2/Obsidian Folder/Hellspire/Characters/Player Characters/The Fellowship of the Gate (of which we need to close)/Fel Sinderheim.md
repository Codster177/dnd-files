### Backstory

Fel Sinderheim was born Fel Sirdhemion, the child of a forbidden romance between Princess Miabella Galanor, an elven princess of the Evergreen Kingdom, and Din Sirdhemion. The couple fled the kingdom, hired a crew, and turned to piracy when their money ran out. Eventually the crew was caught, and Fel was separated from his parents. Deemed an improper heir and a threat to the throne, he was imprisoned in the deepest dungeon of the Evergreen Kingdom as a child. He grew old and died there, his only companions the rats that lived among him.

After death, Fel's soul entered a rare judgment state. Having never dedicated himself to a deity and having lived too little of a life to be claimed by any domain, he was exhaled from Sigil and cast randomly into the universe. He was eventually captured by Charon, ruler of the River Styx, and pulled into that realm. For several thousand years, Fel existed as a lost soul drifting through the Styx.

His existence changed when [[Aji Skipplestump]] fell into the river. Aji had been searching Hell for his wife Myla and his son [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]], and in desperation reached into the Styx to grab a soul. Fel's spirit entered Aji's body instead, taking control. Now Fel inhabits Aji's gnomish form, slowly uncovering the man's past and the desperate pleas Aji makes from within for Fel to fulfill his goals.

### Season 2 Events

#### Session 4 - Fort Lake Gap
Fel passed **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]]'s detection amulet test alongside [[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. During exploration of the fort, he summoned a spectral reaper against a Barlgura demon and played a countercharm on his violin to protect allies from the psychic attacks of an Oblex. He recognized the detection spell and noted that it should have exposed [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s fiendish nature, but said nothing. When the Queen mentioned searching for a plasmoid named [[Malm]], Fel recalled the journal pages describing such a creature but kept the knowledge to himself.

#### Session 5 - The Dungeon Beneath
In the dungeon below the fort, Fel produced torches and investigated ancient bloodstains. During a fight with a Babau demon, he accidentally struck himself with his own maul. He recognized the Evergreen Kingdom on a war room map, triggering a pang of distant memory. When **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] interrogated the group, Fel confessed he had escaped from Hell itself, a lost soul trapped in a body not his own. The Queen noted that Fel's maul had belonged to [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], connecting the voice Fel had heard in his visions to that lost warrior.

#### Session 6 - The Stomach Devil and the Stone
During the battle with a stomach devil on the battlements, Fel made a desperate gamble: he summoned a reaper spirit directly inside the creature's gullet to force [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] free after the warlock was swallowed. The gamble succeeded. He then used mass healing to revive the unconscious [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. Later, Fel investigated the abyssal sigil in the dungeon and discovered a vein of hellish stone weeping blue blood. Despite the risk, he placed his hand on the stone and received a vision: he saw himself holding a spiraled unicorn horn slick with stag's blood, the hellish root parting before their combined power. This vision established the party's next objective. During the scramble to salvage [[Ig]]'s lair before The Engine of Nessus arrived, Fel attempted to recall [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]'s belongings but his memory failed him. He focused on retrieving more of [[Malm]]'s notes instead.

#### Session 7 - New Freia
Upon arriving in New Freia, Fel climbed onto a table in the gathering hall and played his spectral violin, earning a gold piece from a passing dwarf. **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] offered the party a commission as a garrison of the Royal Knights, which Fel accepted enthusiastically. He received a cloak of displacement from [[Harold Gobblefern]] and attempted to barter with the blacksmith through performance, though his nervous solo fell flat. At Cats tavern, his music flowed effortlessly, earning steady tips. That night, Fel dreamed of the dungeon where [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]] confronted him, accusing him of stealing his body and demanding it back. Fel apologized but refused to return it.

#### Session 8 - The Mind War Begins
Fel retrieved plate armor from the Tunnel Hall Smithy. He entered the Church of the Watcher with [[Zilton Zalzalm]], where he met [[Solace Forger]], a dwarven cleric who recognized his face from over thirty years prior, and recruited her into the garrison. He discovered the plate armor was too heavy for him and reconfigured it into half plate using a modular design the blacksmith had built in. Later, at Smelter's Heaven, Fel attempted to charm the wood elf armorer [[Lulin]] into a discount on a dagger. During the encounter he collapsed unconscious as his mind was pulled into a violent vision: [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]] broke through a cell door and attacked him with his fists. Fel cast a sleeping spell on himself to escape the beating, and woke on the armory floor. He purchased the dagger at a reduced price and left shaken.

#### Session 9 - The Spirit Crisis
During the night at the Home Away Inn, Fel was violently ejected from his body during a psychic struggle with [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]]. He became a disembodied spirit with only moments to find a new vessel or face oblivion. He possessed the innkeeper to stabilize, then sought help from [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. Attempts to enter the mechanical dog Dot and [[Skip Leafing]] both failed. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] offered [[Nampha]]'s body, and Fel successfully entered the imp. While sleeping inside [[Nampha]], Fel was pulled into a calmer mindscape where he met [[Nampha]] chained in a cave. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s sentient sword then expelled Fel back into his own body with a psychic lance.

#### Session 10 - The Vision Begins
While traveling through snow north of Gemstone City, Fel opened the enigmatic tome _Outside the Grain of Salt_ and was presented with a riddle: "What is my name?" He answered "The Watcher," and the book collapsed in on itself and vanished. From this point onward, the events Fel experienced were a prophetic vision of the future, though neither he nor his companions were aware of this at the time. In the vision, Fel played a mournful tune over the body of a celestial stag that [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s sword killed, and used his song of rest to soothe the party during a frigid camp.

#### Session 11 - The Infernal Workshop (Vision)
In the vision, Fel rallied the group with a motivational speech and mass cure wounds during a brutal fight against lava elementals in an infernal workshop hidden inside a mountain cave.

### Relationships

- [[Aji Skipplestump]] - The gnome whose body Fel inhabits. Aji is trapped within their shared mindscape and is hostile, demanding his body back. Fel's presence has reshaped Aji's mental landscape into a dungeon.
- [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] - Party member and fellow garrison member. Na'Hal helped Fel survive his spirit crisis by offering [[Nampha]]'s body as a vessel.
- [[Zilton Zalzalm]] - Party member and fellow garrison member. A reliable ally in combat.
- [[Layla Tenama]] - Queen of New Freia and Fel's liege. She is wary of Fel but accepted him into her garrison. Fel's attempts at charm have met cold responses.
- [[Deyrale Dinglebottom]] - A Season 1 hero whose maul and gear Fel now carries. Fel has heard Deyrale's voice in visions.
- [[Solace Forger]] - A dwarven cleric Fel recruited into the garrison. She recognized his face from over thirty years ago.
