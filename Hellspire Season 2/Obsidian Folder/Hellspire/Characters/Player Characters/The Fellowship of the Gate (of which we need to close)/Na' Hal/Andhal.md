Andhal is one of two imps that together form [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], created from a single soul alongside [[Nampha]]. Andhal handles physical combat and practical tasks for the pair. When combined, Andhal forms the lower half of their tiefling disguise.

Andhal can shapeshift into a rat, a skill he used to squeeze through the armory portcullis at Fort Lake Gap. When Na'Hal's tiefling disguise falters, Andhal works frantically to maintain the illusion while [[Nampha]] keeps the body upright.
