Na'Hal is not a single being but two imps, [[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]], created from one soul. Together they combine into the form of a tiefling, with [[Nampha]] handling spellcasting and [[Andhal]] handling physical combat. Their patron is a sentient greatsword connected to the Archdevil Angmar.

### Backstory

[[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]] are two imps created from a single soul. They banded together to survive, combining their complementary abilities: [[Nampha]]'s magic and [[Andhal]]'s combat prowess. At some point, they were denied a promotion in the hierarchy of the Nine Hells, an injustice that fuels their desire for revenge against the devils who wronged them.

In a dream, Na'Hal recalled a spire in Avernus where a sentient sword in a temple of flame demanded their loyalty and severed their wings, binding their souls to darkness. This sword became their hexblade patron, granting them warlock powers but also exerting control over their actions at times.
