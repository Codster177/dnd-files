Na'Hal is not a single being but two imps, [[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]], created from one soul. Together they combine into the form of a tiefling, with [[Nampha]] handling spellcasting and [[Andhal]] handling physical combat. Their patron is a sentient greatsword connected to the Archdevil Angmar.

### Backstory

[[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]] are two imps created from a single soul. They banded together to survive, combining their complementary abilities: [[Nampha]]'s magic and [[Andhal]]'s combat prowess. At some point, they were denied a promotion in the hierarchy of the Nine Hells, an injustice that fuels their desire for revenge against the devils who wronged them.

In a dream, Na'Hal recalled a spire in Avernus where a sentient sword in a temple of flame demanded their loyalty and severed their wings, binding their souls to darkness. This sword became their hexblade patron, granting them warlock powers but also exerting control over their actions at times.

### Season 2 Events

#### Session 4 - Fort Lake Gap
Na'Hal passed **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]]'s detection amulet, but not through his own doing. His sentient sword manipulated the spell's result to conceal his fiendish nature. During the Barlgura fight, Na'Hal launched himself through a hole to the upper floor and landed a critical eldritch blast through the demon's chest. To access the locked armory, Na'Hal split into his two imp forms: [[Nampha]] cast a spell while [[Andhal]] transformed into a rat, squeezed through the portcullis bars, and opened the gate from within, securing two flintlock pistols, a musket, and portable cannons.

#### Session 5 - The Dungeon Beneath
Na'Hal found a hidden key in the dungeon's supply closet and broke through a rusted door with his maul, only to be ambushed and grappled by a Babau demon clinging to the ceiling. When questioned by the Queen, he claimed to be a wanderer without a homeland. In the abyssal sigil chamber, Na'Hal channeled a banishing smite through his blade, sending a moss demon hurtling back through a portal to the Abyss. Afterward, he sought counsel from his sentient sword about whether to reveal his fiendish nature to the Queen. The sword warned him to keep the secret at all costs.

#### Session 6 - The Stomach Devil and the Lair
Na'Hal spotted the stomach devil emerging from the acid lake and warned the party. During the battle on the battlements, his hexblade's curse empowered his strikes, but the devil's ribcage opened like a maw and swallowed him. Suffering necrotic damage inside the creature, he was freed when [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] summoned a reaper spirit into its gullet. Na'Hal channeled all his remaining power into a banishing strike that sent the devil into the void, then collapsed unconscious and was revived by [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s healing. He recognized the hellish stone root as a manifestation of the Hells' dominion using his infernal knowledge. During the scramble to salvage [[Ig]]'s lair before The Engine of Nessus arrived, Na'Hal secretly consulted his sword about taking [[Gabble Skipplestump]]'s amulet, then grabbed it.

#### Session 7 - New Freia
Na'Hal gave a cautious affirmation to the Queen's garrison commission. He received a helm of comprehend languages from [[Harold Gobblefern]] and studied the retrieved amulet, finding it to be a potent spell focus that could enhance his magic if attuned through a ritual with his sword. At Cats tavern, Na'Hal lost all his copper at blackjack and drank heavily, pushing his constitution to the limit. His disguise began to falter as [[Andhal]] worked frantically to maintain the illusion while [[Nampha]] kept the body upright. He eventually collapsed into two separate imps before passing out. His companions carried him to a room before anyone noticed.

#### Session 8 - The Blind Man's Bargain
Na'Hal was physically paralyzed at the threshold of the Church of the Watcher, his fiendish nature rebelling against the hallowed ground, and was forced to retreat. He waited outside the Amethyst Library as well, fearing a magical sensor might reveal him. At the Fisted Dwarf tavern, he received a flyer for the charlatan [[Tip Trinket]]. After the charlatan fled, [[Leolar Spirit|the blind man]] appeared and offered a tuft of unicorn hair in exchange for a soul coin. After tense negotiation, Na'Hal secretly handed over the coin while his companions were distracted. The blind man crushed it, freeing the trapped soul, and vanished. Na'Hal concealed the transaction from the party.

#### Session 9 - The Sword's Rebuke
Na'Hal helped [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] survive his spirit crisis, ultimately offering [[Nampha]]'s body as a vessel when all other options failed. He struck the possessed innkeeper to eject Fel's spirit so it could enter [[Nampha]]. Later, Na'Hal's sentient sword confronted him about the soul coin trade with [[Leolar Spirit|the blind man]]. The blade expressed genuine worry, revealing that the blind man's power was significant enough to cloud even the sword's perception, and warned Na'Hal to be far more careful. Na'Hal also helped recruit [[Scarlet]], their flirtatious banter creating a moment of levity. He studied _Anatomy of Celestial Creatures_, learning that white stags were celestial beings.

#### Session 10 - The Sword's Betrayal (Vision)
A celestial white stag appeared and offered its blood freely, warning Na'Hal that if he was truly different from other imps, he would not attack the unicorn. In that moment, Na'Hal's sword acted of its own accord, forcing his hand to summon a greatsword and drive it through the stag's heart. Na'Hal staggered back, horrified and unable to explain his actions to his stunned companions. That night, [[Zilton Zalzalm]] confronted him about the sword. Na'Hal confessed his backstory: he had been denied a promotion in the Hells and sought revenge. He admitted he did not fully understand why the sword helped him, but conveyed his commitment to closing the Gate.

#### Session 11 - The Infernal Workshop (Vision)
In the mountain cave, Na'Hal found a cauldron of swirling purple energy and dipped his finger in, absorbing its contents for a temporary attack boost. He discovered a side room filled with fifty-seven soul coins and pocketed them all. His curiosity led him to insert a broken soul coin into a hellfire engine's deposit slot, accidentally triggering an alarm that summoned lava elementals. During the battle, he unleashed eldritch blasts and a psychic lance that cracked an elemental spellcaster's helmet.

### Relationships

- **The Sentient Sword** - Na'Hal's hexblade patron. It grants him power but exerts terrifying control, having killed a celestial stag against his will and paralyzed [[Zilton Zalzalm]] when he tried to examine it. It manipulated [[Layla Tenama]]'s detection amulet to conceal Na'Hal's fiendish nature. It rebuked Na'Hal for trading a soul coin to [[Leolar Spirit|the blind man]], expressing genuine worry about the blind man's power to cloud the sword's perception.
- [[Zilton Zalzalm]] - Party member. Their relationship is marked by tension over the sword's influence. After a midnight confrontation, they reached a fragile understanding based on their shared goal of closing the Gate.
- [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] - Party member. Na'Hal helped Fel survive his spirit crisis by offering [[Nampha]]'s body. They share a bond forged through mutual vulnerability.
- [[Leolar Spirit]] (the blind man) - An enigmatic figure who traded unicorn hair for a soul coin. Na'Hal distrusts him, and the sword considers the blind man dangerously powerful.
- [[Layla Tenama]] - Queen of New Freia and Na'Hal's liege. Na'Hal is guarded around her, keeping his fiendish nature hidden.
- [[Scarlet]] - A tiefling woman recruited into the garrison. There was a flirtatious dynamic during her interview.
