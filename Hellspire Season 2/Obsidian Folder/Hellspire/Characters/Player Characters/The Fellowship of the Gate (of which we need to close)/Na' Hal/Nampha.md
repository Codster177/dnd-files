Nampha is one of two imps that together form [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], created from a single soul alongside [[Andhal]]. Nampha handles spellcasting and magical abilities for the pair. When combined, Nampha forms the upper half of their tiefling disguise.

When Na'Hal's disguise falters, such as during heavy drinking, Nampha works to keep the body upright while [[Andhal]] maintains the illusion. Nampha's body was temporarily inhabited by [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s spirit during a crisis in Gemstone City when Fel was ejected from [[Aji Skipplestump]]'s body and needed a vessel to survive.
