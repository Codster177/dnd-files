### Backstory

Zilton Zalzalm is a Warforged sent from the plane of Mechanus as a disaster response drone. His primary objective is to close the portal to the Nine Hells; his secondary objective is to determine how the catastrophe occurred.

Zilton was previously deployed to the mortal plane of Al in the year -4700 during the Crisis of Tiamat, when the Cult of Tiamat attempted to summon their draconic god to end the world. Zilton helped resolve the crisis and was placed into suspension afterward. With the Gate to Hell now open, Tiamat has a direct entrance to Al, making Zilton's mission all the more urgent.

### Season 2 Events

#### Session 4 - Fort Lake Gap
Zilton fought a Barlgura demon with his surugi blade and a flurry of punches, driving it to use invisibility as a desperate escape. He used his warforged body to run vertically along the fort's inner walls, discovering a cannon gallery with a harpoon cannon and archer slits overlooking the acid lake. He delivered the killing blows against an Oblex with a critical flurry of slashes that scattered it across the walls.

#### Session 5 - The Dungeon Beneath
Zilton's eidetic memory proved invaluable as he studied a recruitment journal found in the war room, piecing together a timeline of the fort's fall: a massive earthquake, a week of hellish incursions, and a prisoner uprising. He calculated that the prisoner responsible for the killings was likely still somewhere in the dungeon. During the fight with a Babau demon, his precise surugi strikes drew crucial blood. He revealed the existence of The Engine of Nessus to **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]], which cracked her composure and led her to share the history of how [[Gabble Skipplestump]] opened the Gate, and that [[Malm]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom]] had been lost in the process.

#### Session 6 - The Stomach Devil and the Stone
Zilton stood sentinel during the eight-hour flight on [[Ig]]'s back, observing his allies in sentry mode. He noted subtle imperfections in [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s tiefling illusion: hair that moved unnaturally in the wind, a faint shimmer at the edges. During the stomach devil battle, he dashed up the wall and fired a crossbow bolt into the creature's shoulder. Back at the fort, he studied the hellish stone vein and noted it resembled a root more than simple ore, bluntly suggesting that [[Malm]] might be the gate itself. He also snatched a newspaper clipping headlined "King's Son - Turmoil" from [[Ig]]'s lair during the evacuation.

#### Session 7 - New Friea
Zilton accepted the Queen's garrison commission immediately and without hesitation. [[Harold Gobblefern]] gifted him a mechanical dog construct, which he named Dot. He visited the Friea Monument, studying the silver effigies of King [[Gilles Freia]] and Prince [[Almint Friea|Almond]], and reading a newspaper article about the king adopting the bird-like humanoid prince, which had sparked tensions with the Empire of Al. At Cats tavern, he played blackjack for the first time, his mechanical mind grasping the probabilities instantly, and won his first hand. He spent the entire night reading an ancient tome of dragon history, committing every restored page to his eidetic memory.

#### Session 8 - Draconic Lore and Diplomatic Disaster
Zilton shared his overnight discoveries about the draconic gods: how the five great dragon worms merged into three gods (Bahamut, Sardior, and Tiamat), the thousand-year war between them, and a restored map of chromatic territories showing a desert of ice, a swamp of acid, an island of hell, an overgrown jungle, and hills of sand surrounding a mysterious central symbol. He visited the Church of the Watcher and asked the pastor about unicorns. When [[Solace Forger]] cast a mending spell on his ancient book, she restored scratched-out names and unblacked a crucial page. At the Gemstone City keep, Zilton's blunt demand for assistance and an insult directed at a gnome councilwoman nearly caused a diplomatic disaster, saved only by [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s charm and the Queen's token.

#### Session 9 - Recruits and Frustrations
Zilton staged the unconscious innkeeper's body at the bottom of the stairs to cover for [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s possession incident. He helped interview [[Percy]] and [[Scarlet]] as potential garrison recruits, grilling them with quiet, robotic directness. When he spotted workers hauling banana tree saplings into Gemstone City, an idea he had proposed to the council, he was furious at receiving no credit and lodged a formal complaint at the keep. He donned the Helm of Comprehending Languages to read _Creatures of the Outer Planes_, and when he spoke the Celestial word for "stag," the book's ink swirled into a living painting showing the party's own figures moving through the forest.

#### Session 10 - The Midnight Confrontation (Vision)
After [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s sword killed the celestial white stag against its wielder's will, Zilton pulled [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] aside to question the nature of the blade. That night, while the party slept, Zilton crept into the tent and attempted to examine the greatsword. He sensed it was the source of much of [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s power and that it was aware of his presence. When he reached for the blade, it cast hold person on him. After the paralysis broke, he followed [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] into the snowy woods for a tense confrontation. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] confessed he had been denied a promotion in the Hells and sought revenge. Zilton made clear that closing the Gate was his only priority, and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] conveyed his commitment to the same goal.

#### Session 11 - The Infernal Workshop (Vision)
At Forest Guard Village, **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] reported that [[Ig]] was scouting The Engine of Nessus, which had been spotted moving into the ocean. [[Sabrina Liaro]] officially joined the party. In the mountain cave, Zilton lifted a portcullis off its damaged hinges and recognized a prototype hellfire engine. He studied a dissected spider on a table and learned that souls consist of both a pointer and a memory address. During the lava elemental battle, he darted across lava using his crossbow and surugi to harry the enemy.

### Relationships

- [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] - Party member. Zilton distrusts Na'Hal's sentient sword after it paralyzed him and killed the celestial stag. Despite this, he and Na'Hal reached a fragile understanding based on their shared goal of closing the Gate.
- [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] - Party member and fellow garrison member. A reliable ally whose bardic magic has saved the party multiple times.
- Dot - Zilton's mechanical dog companion, gifted by [[Harold Gobblefern]]. Dot has combat capabilities and can track scents.
- [[Layla Tenama]] - Queen of New Friea and Zilton's liege. He respects her authority and accepted her commission without hesitation.
- [[Harold Gobblefern]] - Gnome inventor in New Friea who provided Dot, magical items, and agreed to research countermeasures against The Engine of Nessus.
- [[Skip Leafing]] - Half-elf engineer whom Zilton accepted into the garrison on the spot, though [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] intervened with conditions.
- [[Ig]] - The emerald dragon companion of the Queen. Zilton has flown on Ig's back and studied draconic lore connected to him.
