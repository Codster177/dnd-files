### Backstory

Malm is a plasmoid **interdimensional detective from [[Mechanus]]**, employed by the **Realm Central Control Bureau**. For roughly **100 years** he has been spying on the plasmoid lieutenant [[Fish]], and through that investigation came to learn nearly all there is to know about Fish — and much about Cthulhu — by way of [[Lord Gallium]], whose interdimensional crimes Malm was sent to bring to justice. He is a meticulous record-keeper, logging his findings in a **journal** that would prove important in Season 2.

*(Malm's life on Mechanus and how he first picked up Gallium's trail are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Sunken Arena.** Malm first appeared stuck to [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]'s back in a cell of the **Sunken Arena**, revealing himself as the interdimensional detective investigating [[Lord Gallium]]. Having decided [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and Deyrale were his best path forward, he bonded with Deyrale over his trauma and fought beside him in the pit, escaping with the party aboard [[Oogloo]].

**The Journal at the Cove.** At the party's cove base, Malm spent the night logging his evidence against the pirate lord in his journal — a record he continued to keep throughout the story.

**Freeing Ig.** Investigating Gallium's lair beneath the ruined Sunken Arena, Malm found the emerald dragon [[Ig]] being pumped full of tadpoles in a transformation chamber. Malm **freed Ig and removed the tadpoles**, and the grateful dragon tunneled the party to freedom and became a key ally.

**Intelligence Gathering.** Infiltrating the Damned Guild's main encampment (then held by the Knights of Freia), Malm uncovered Gallium's plan to invade **Lootbridge City** in the Bruibary Monarchy, setting the party's next course.

**The Royal Ship.** Aboard [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]]'s ship, Malm grieved [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]'s amnesia and was rejuvenated by [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]'s **Wish**. Spotting the brilliant light of the wish-mote, he confronted Gabble over what he had done — only for [[Fish]] to break in and split the ship. Malm later drew the **Rogue** card from the **Deck of Many Things**, turning an ally against him.

**The Fall of Gallium and the Gate.** Malm boarded the nautiloid for the final battle, witnessing [[Kix]] sacrifice himself to destroy [[Lord Gallium]]. In the vault at Safekeeps, Malm fought alongside [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]] to stop the Asmodeus-possessed [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] from opening the **Gate to Hell** — but the ritual succeeded, the vault collapsed, and Malm was buried in the Gate's infernal roots.

### The Roots of the Gate

The interdimensional detective from Mechanus. Malm is a Plasmoid Rouge who sought to bring down Lord Gallium, but now seeks to survive the apocalypse.

Malm is in the midst of battle, facing the corrupted Gabble Skipplestump and Badger, who has just turned into a giant ape. Malm is knocked back, and witnesses Badger grab his companion Deyrale Dinglebottom, and slam him into the floor. However, before Deyrale hits the floor, a fiery infernal chasm opens across the floor, and Deyrale is sent into the depths of hell. Now bring down to just him and Queen Layla, he calls soon Ig the Dragon to help win this fight. But it is to no avail. Just as Ig breaks through the ceiling, an immense shockwave is felt as a blinding red light shines from across the room. The wand hovering above the infernal fire ignites and shoots a quick beam at the cobblestone wall. The vault begins to collapse. As the wall begins to rearrange itself into the portal to the nine Hells, the floor beneath Malm collapses, and he falls just as the portal settles onto the ground. Somehow surviving a lethal fall, his last sight before him is a large and jagged obsidian pillar barreling towards him as he is crushed between the ground and the pillar. Malm is now encased in the infernal roots of the Gate to hell. 

While encased, Malm awakens to find himself in a red and stormy wasteland. Hearing the crackling of energy and the shifting of stones, he turns to see the gate to hell, growing rapidly in size and collecting stones around him. Suddenly, a deafening roar erupts as a colossal 5 headed dragon flies out of the portal. After climbing hundreds of feet, the dragon is clamped back by each of it's necks. A tall evil figure emerges from the portal and tugs at his five headed leash, keeping Tiamat at bay. Asmodeus has come to end the world. The figure points his crooked finger at Malm, and he is killed by a barrage of fire, lighting, ice, poison, and acid. He then wakes back up to the same sight, reliving this same fortune for 2 decades long.
