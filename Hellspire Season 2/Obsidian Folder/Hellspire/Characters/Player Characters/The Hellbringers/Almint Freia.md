### Backstory

Almint Freia was an aarakocra **prince of [[Freia]]**, son of **King** [[Gilles Freia]]. Raised in the practices of the clerics of Freia, he learned their rites and healing magic without ever devoutly worshipping a god — a gap that would later define his fate. When his father fell gravely ill, Almint set out aboard a royal yacht, with his royal advisor, toward the City of the Damned in search of a cure.

### Season 1 Events

**Rescue at Sea.** Traveling to the City of the Damned to cure his dying father, Almint's royal yacht discovered [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]], [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], and [[Kix]] stranded after the wreck of their pirate ship. Hoping the survivors could lead him to a cure, Almint took them aboard against his advisor's judgment, forming the party.

**The City of the Damned.** In the marketplace Almint failed to find any cure good enough for his father, but the party bought mysterious potions from the artist [[Frink]] — including the **Tonic of God's Touch**. At the Broken-Bone Tavern, [[Badger]] hired them to clear an infestation of **Cranium Rats** from the basement. As the last rat died, Almint heard a deep voice in his mind — *"I see, royalty hmmm…"* — the first unknowing contact from [[Lord Gallium]].

**Abduction and Infection.** Almint followed a frantic pirate to a hut by the docks and witnessed the man assassinated by the Damned Guild. That night his advisor was killed and Almint was kidnapped. He awoke trapped in a fleshy capsule, where an alien hand pressed a **tadpole** into his eye, infesting his head. Freed in secret by the lieutenant [[Violence]] and recovered by [[Badger]], he awoke in the tavern now able to read Deep Speech — and translated the note naming **Lord Gallium**.

**The Prophetic Dream.** Almint dreamed of his dying father [[Gilles Freia]], who asked *"Who are you?"* before sinking into an inky mass that birthed a dark silhouette of Almint himself — foreshadowing the tadpole's coming conquest of his body.

**First Death and Blindness.** During the ambush of the lieutenant [[Peace]], Almint fell and died after a botched medicine check by [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] — but the parasite revived his brain, leaving him **blind**. His blindness was offset by a new **creature sense** that let him see souls through walls, accompanied by tentacles sprouting from his eye sockets.

**The Truth of His Power.** After escaping Almint's pirate-overrun ship, Almint received a vision from **Cthulhu** and realized the truth: never having truly worshipped a god, his cleric magic had been drawing its strength from Cthulhu ever since he reached the City of the Damned.

**Final Death.** Returning to the Damned Manor, a mind flayer seized control of [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and turned him on Almint, beating him unconscious; Almint died once more when Deyrale again failed to save him. His soul ascended into space and came face to face with **Cthulhu**, who cast it toward a pit of endless torment — only to retract it with the words *"Not yet."* The party preserved his body in a **Bag of Holding**.

**The Imposter Prince.** Lord Gallium reanimated and controlled Almint's body, parading him as the living prince. He told the Knights of Freia that the party had caused his disappearance and that Gallium had been his savior, turning Freia against the Hellbringers.

**The Battle for the Royal Ship.** Brought before **Captain** [[Layla Tenama]], the party exposed that Almint was controlled by Lord Gallium. Confronted, Almint's body unraveled into a mass of tentacles, and the abomination savaged [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] — tearing away his ear and eye — before the party killed it and cast it into the sea.

**Funeral and Release.** After Gallium's defeat, [[Oogloo]] and his father **Loo** bore Almint's monstrous body up from the sea floor. The party held a funeral, finally **freeing his soul to the afterlife**, and found the **second vault key** hidden within his body.

### Relationships

- **[[Gilles Freia]]** — Almint's father, the ailing King of Freia whose cure drove the entire quest.
- **[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]**, **[[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]** — Fellow founding Hellbringers who took him in and fought alongside (and against) him.
- **[[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]** — Failed the medicine checks that led to Almint's deaths, including the mind-controlled beating; a source of lasting guilt for Deyrale.
- **[[Lord Gallium]]** — The mind flayer who infected, killed, and ultimately puppeted Almint's body.
- **Cthulhu** — The eldritch being Almint unknowingly drew his cleric powers from after reaching the City of the Damned.
- **[[Layla Tenama]]** — Almint's childhood best friend. Once he was controlled, she was suspicious of his identity.
