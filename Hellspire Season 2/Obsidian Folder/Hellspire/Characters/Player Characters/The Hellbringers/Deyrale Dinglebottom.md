### Backstory

Deyrale Dinglebottom is a goliath barbarian whose ancestors came from the Giant island before splitting off into their own tribe — his great-grandfather was a giant. Stolen from his goliath tribe at a very young age, Deyrale has no memory of his people. He was raised by a dragonborn father figure named **Torrin Streats**, who taught and protected him until the age of 12. Deyrale spent much of his adolescence in a **gladiator pit**, and believes Torrin was killed there — a loss that fuels his rage.

*(How Deyrale left the pit, and the years between then and joining the Damned Guild, are left open for you to fill in.)*

### Season 1 Events

**The Crew of the Damned.** Deyrale and [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] began as crewmates aboard a pirate ship of the **Damned Guild** under **Captain** [[Camila Aleleaf]], recruited only days earlier. When the ship was destroyed by a **Dragon Turtle** summoned by a dying merfolk priest, the two ensured Aleleaf never reached the escape rowboat and fled with the lone other survivor, [[Kix]]. Stranded at sea, they were rescued by **Prince** [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s royal yacht, and the party was formed.

**Hunting the Lieutenants.** In the City of the Damned, [[Badger]] hired the party to kill the Pirate Lieutenants of the Damned Guild and seize the two keys to the vault at **Safekeeps**. When [[Almint Freia|Almint]] was kidnapped, Deyrale led [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] to the docks, and the two disguised themselves as Damned pirates. At the church they were exposed by the condemned [[Camila Aleleaf|Captain Aleleaf]] and forced to fight the towering lieutenant [[Griffin Bullethole]], whom they killed in the fleshy tunnels below.

**The Prophetic Dream.** Deyrale dreamed of a mermaid wielding the largest, most beautiful axe he had ever seen — pulled away just before he could claim it. This foreshadowed the lieutenant [[Fish]] and the **Greataxe of Certain Death**.

**The Death of Peace.** Ambushing the mute dwarf lieutenant [[Peace]], Deyrale's botched medicine check led to [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s first death (though the parasite revived him). Enraged, Deyrale killed [[Peace]] even as her sister [[Violence]] pleaded for peace, costing the party Violence's aid.

**Controlled Against His Friend.** Beneath the Damned Manor, a mind flayer seized control of Deyrale and turned him on [[Almint Freia|Almint]], beating him unconscious; Almint died once more when Deyrale, again controlled, failed to save him. Deyrale and [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] were then beaten unconscious by [[Fish]].

**The Sunken Arena.** Deyrale awoke in a cell of the **Sunken Arena**, despairing over slaying Almint while controlled and reliving the gladiator life of his youth. There he met [[Malm]], a plasmoid detective stuck to his back, and the two bonded. In the pit, Deyrale calmed a baby dragon turtle and helped it cough up his own lost **javelin**, now magically charged; with the creature — named [[Oogloo]] — the party destroyed the arena and escaped, rescuing [[Dama]].

**Grief and the Cove.** Suffering insomnia at the party's cove base, Deyrale saw a memory-visage of [[Almint Freia|Almint]] who forgave him and urged him onward — convincing him to follow Gabble's vision back to the City of the Damned.

**Amnesia and the Greataxe.** Aboard [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]]'s royal ship, the abomination wearing [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s body tore away Deyrale's ear and eye and savaged his brain, leaving him with **amnesia** — later undone by [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]'s **Wish**, which restored his eye, ear, and memories. When [[Fish]] split the ship in two, Deyrale finally killed him and claimed the **Greataxe of Certain Death**, the very axe from his dream. He also drew the **Gem** card from the **Deck of Many Things**, conjuring 50,000 gp in gems.

**Cast into Hell.** In the vault at Safekeeps, Deyrale, [[Malm]], and [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]] fought to stop the now Asmodeus-possessed [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] from opening the Gate to Hell. [[Badger]] arrived to ensure the ritual succeeded and hurled Deyrale into the **Nine Hells**, where the party watched him vanish.

### Relationships

- **Torrin Streats** — Deyrale's dragonborn father figure, who raised him until age 12 and is believed to have died in the gladiator pit.
- **[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]** — Fellow Damned Guild crewmate and oldest companion; their bond is strained when Gabble falls under Asmodeus's sway.
- **[[Almint Freia|Almint]]** — A founding companion whose repeated deaths at Deyrale's (often controlled) hands haunt him; Almint's forgiving visage helped him carry on.
- **[[Malm]]** — Met in the Sunken Arena; bonded over Deyrale's trauma and became close allies.
- **[[Fish]]** — The lieutenant who once bested him; Deyrale ultimately killed him and took his greataxe.
- **[[Badger]]** — The goblin who hurled Deyrale into the Nine Hells.
- **[[Oogloo]]** — The baby dragon turtle Deyrale calmed; a loyal ally to the party.
- **[[Layla Tenama]]** — Freian captain the party fought alongside.
