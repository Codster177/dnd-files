### Backstory

Gabble Skipplestump is a gnome from **the Shire**, who studied while traveling in order to become a wizard. He was **14 when his mother, [[Myla Skipplestump]], died** — a loss that has driven him ever since. His mother was freckled, with a kind face, green eyes, and a thin figure; his father, [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]], is very short, blue-eyed, remarkably strong and hairy, and plays the lute. Gabble's deepest wish is to **go back in time to learn how his mother died** and find clarity, the spark behind his pursuit of chronomancy. Broke, he joined a pirate crew of the Damned Guild.

What Gabble does not know at first is that his mother was herself a warlock who signed her soul away to a devil — and that her fate prefigures his own.

### Season 1 Events

**The Crew of the Damned.** Gabble and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] began as crewmates aboard a ship of the **Damned Guild** under **Captain** [[Camila Aleleaf]]. When a **Dragon Turtle** destroyed the ship, Gabble vortex-warped the fleeing Aleleaf deep into the ocean, and the two escaped with [[Kix]] before being rescued by **Prince** [[Almint Freia|Almint]].

**The Warlock Pact.** In the basement of the Broken-Bone Tavern, Gabble asked [[Badger]] about his powers. Badger used the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** to open a rift to Hell, through which stepped the amnizu devil [[Byrst]]. Gabble forfeited his soul to **Asmodeus**, sealing a warlock pact and beginning to multiclass as a wizard and warlock.

**The Prophetic Dream.** Gabble dreamed of his dead mother [[Myla Skipplestump]] knitting outside his childhood home. She asked, *"Was it worth it?"* before fire erupted behind her melting eyes — foreshadowing both Myla's own warlock pact and the melting of Gabble's left eye.

**The Infernal Calling.** As Badger set the party to hunting the Pirate Lieutenants, Gabble felt a mounting **hellish mental pressure** compelling him to obey. He helped fight through [[Griffin Bullethole]], [[Peace]], the dinner trap of [[Lord Gallium]], and the death of [[Barnacle]].

**Infection and Disfigurement.** Beaten unconscious by [[Fish]] beneath the Damned Manor, Gabble awoke in a flesh cage and was infested with a **tadpole**. He escaped and found [[Badger]], who summoned [[Byrst]] once more; together they **burned away the left side of his face** to kill the parasite, leaving half his skull exposed as the two smiled an evil grin.

**The Glowing Eye.** Gabble awoke in the Sunken Arena to find a **glowing eye** set in the melted side of his face — a "gift" from [[Byrst]]. It flashed him a memory of his mother's abuse and a vision of [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and [[Malm]] about to fight, sending him to their aid.

**Visions and the Cove.** Gabble's visions guided the party: he foresaw [[Almint Freia|Almint]]'s husk being reassembled (prompting the return to the City of the Damned) and [[Dama]]'s dissection by mind flayers. He also received the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** from Badger in Lootbridge, told he would "know when to use it."

**The Deal with Asmodeus.** Desperate after [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]'s amnesia, Gabble drank the **Tonic of God's Touch** — and **Asmodeus** himself emerged. Asmodeus offered power to defeat Lord Gallium in exchange for Gabble's **body and mind**, to be possessed at will. Dragged to **Stygia** and left to freeze, Gabble at last signed the deal. He awoke with a **mote of pure energy** — a contained **Wish** — and wished for [[Malm]], [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], and [[Kix]] to retain their sense of self and have eternal life, restoring Deyrale's eye, ear, and memories and Kix's hand and sight.

**Possession and the Gate.** In the vault at Safekeeps, dread overtook Gabble as **Asmodeus chose that moment to claim his vessel**. His consciousness returned to his soul in Stygia, where he met his mother [[Myla Skipplestump]] and learned the truth of her warlock pact and suicide. His possessed body seized the **Wand of Portals**, placed it in the **Brazier of Hellish Fire**, and opened a permanent **Gate to Hell** — buried in the collapsing vault as Season 1 ended.

### Relationships

- **[[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]]** — Gabble's father, a short, lute-playing gnome.
- **[[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]** — Fellow Damned Guild crewmate and oldest companion; saved by Gabble's Wish.
- **[[Almint Freia|Almint]]**, **[[Malm]]** — Fellow Hellbringers.
- **Asmodeus** — Archdevil who holds Gabble's soul and, by the end of Season 1, his body and mind as well.
- **[[Byrst]]** — The amnizu devil who brokered Gabble's pact and gifted him the glowing eye.
- **[[Badger]]** — The goblin warlock who initiated Gabble's pact and pressed him toward opening the Gate.
- **[[Dama]]** — A fellow warlock the party rescued and later lost to the mind flayers.
