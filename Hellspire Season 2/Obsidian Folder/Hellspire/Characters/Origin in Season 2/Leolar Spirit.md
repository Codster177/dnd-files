
Lord Leolar Spirit, also known as "Lord Jargle" or "The Blind Man," is a dark elf sorcerer of immense power and mystery. He resides in a solitary castle in the southern region of what was once the Evergreen Kingdom, a fallen realm now lost to history and hellish invasion. Once a loyal friend to royalty, Leolar's journey took him through arrogance, exile, enlightenment, and ultimately, a self-imposed mission to restore the world from the chaos that has consumed it. 

### Early Life and Banishment

 Leolar Spirit was born in 862 SK and grew up as the closest companion of Prince Lucan Galanor, the future ruler of the Evergreen Kingdom. Their friendship ran deep, but Leolar's admiration for Lucan slowly grew into something more. However, societal expectations and his own lack of understanding of his feelings kept him from ever expressing them. When King Galanor passed in 914 SK, Lucan ascended to the throne. Leolar, however, was pushed aside. His father, a rigid and traditional figure, saw him as a distraction to the young king and deemed his arcane abilities insufficient. Leolar was exiled from the kingdom with the directive to return only when he had attained true magical strength. For decades, Leolar wandered the world, gathering magical relics and deepening his knowledge of sorcery. His father monitored his progress until his death in 1004 SK. However, by this time, Leolar had inherited many of his father’s prejudices, believing in the superiority of elves over other races, particularly humans. 

### Return to Evergreen Kingdom and Downfall

 In 1088 SK, at the age of 226, Leolar returned to the Evergreen Kingdom—not by his own volition, but to attend the wedding of his old friend, now King Lucan Galanor, to Queen Juliana Aran, a human woman. The realization that he had "lost" Lucan to a human infuriated him, though he hid it well. Despite his personal turmoil, Lucan appointed Leolar as the Royal Sorcerer, entrusting him with the magical defenses of the kingdom. However, Leolar's obsession with magic deepened into dangerous territory. He sought knowledge forbidden even to the most learned sorcerers, and his research began to erode his sanity. In 1092 SK, during a moment of weakness, his hatred boiled over, and he made a public outcry against Queen Juliana, questioning the legitimacy of a human ruling alongside the elven king. This was the final straw. King Lucan stripped him of his title and banished him to his family's ancestral castle, cutting him off from the kingdom he once sought to serve. 

### The Ritual and the Watcher’s Influence

 Exiled, bitter, and desperate to prove himself right, Leolar delved into soul magic, seeking evidence that humans were inherently lesser than elves. Yet, after years of research, he came to an undeniable truth: all humanoid souls share the same cosmic energy. No race was superior to another. The revelation shattered his entire worldview. Determined to understand the universe at its core, Leolar performed a forbidden ritual that sacrificed his own eyes in exchange for absolute knowledge. This act did more than blind him—it erased his very soul. In its place, he became one with a celestial entity known as The Watcher, an omniscient being bound to the fabric of reality. Now a creature without a soul, Leolar exists outside the perception of gods and extraplanar entities. He can see all things, all possibilities, and all futures, but at the cost of his mortal understanding of the world. To retain some semblance of sanity, he crafted a magical blindfold that limits his perception, allowing him to focus on only one thing at a time. 

### The Fall of Evergreen and a New Purpose

 In 1107 SK, the portal to hell tore open, unleashing an infernal invasion that consumed the Evergreen Kingdom. Leolar fought alongside the defenders but was ultimately powerless to stop the kingdom’s destruction. In 1113 SK, during a final desperate battle, he discovered the lifeless bodies of King Lucan and Queen Juliana. The friend he had never apologized to was gone. With nothing left but regret, Leolar devoted himself to protecting their daughter, Princess Matilda Galanor, who was only eleven at the time. He vowed to fix the broken world, using his knowledge of fate and magic to shape events in ways that might lead to salvation. 

### Encounter with the Party

 By 1127 SK, Leolar had turned his attention to the movements of Zilton Zalzam, Na' Hal, and Fel, sensing their growing importance in the battle against the forces of hell. In Gemstone City, he approached them under the guise of a beggar, requesting coin—specifically, a soul coin from Na’ Hal. The party, suspicious of his motives, initially refused him. Later, during an amateur magic display, Leolar reappeared and convinced Na' Hal to relinquish a soul coin. Unbeknownst to them, he freed the trapped soul, allowing it to pass on to the afterlife. Leolar then gifted the party unicorn hair, a key component needed to track a unicorn, further guiding them on their journey. 

### The Vision and Asmodeus’ Blind Spot

 Shortly after, Fel discovered a mysterious book in the Gemstone City library titled Outside the Grain of Salt. The book bore no registration and was authored by "The Blind Man." Inside, Fel found a riddle: "An insight into an infinite mind,With only one riddle in the way.What is my name?" Fel correctly answered, "The Watcher," gaining access to a vision of the future. In this vision, the party fought against Gabble Skipplestump, now a puppet of Asmodeus, and met their demise. This vision is a crucial turning point. Because Leolar exists outside the perception of gods, Asmodeus—despite his omnipotence—cannot see or account for Leolar’s interference. This provides the party with a rare advantage: knowledge of a future that even the Lord of the Nine Hells cannot predict. Now, Leolar Spirit remains an enigmatic force in the battle against hell’s invasion, guiding the party from the shadows with his infinite sight, hoping to alter fate and atone for his past. 

### Conclusion

 Lord Leolar Spirit stands as one of the most powerful and enigmatic figures in the current age. Once an arrogant noble, he was humbled by exile, maddened by knowledge, and reborn as something beyond mortal comprehension. His story is one of loss, regret, and redemption—an endless quest to fix the world he once sought to rule. Though he has no soul, no place in the afterlife, and no way to escape the knowledge that burdens him, he continues to push forward, determined to guide the few who still have a chance to save what remains of existence.
