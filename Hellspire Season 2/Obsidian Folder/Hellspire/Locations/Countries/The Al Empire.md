## Overview

The Al Empire is the dominant imperial power in the world of Hellspire and the largest political entity, ruling from [[The Capital of the Land]]. Over the years between 1107 and 1127 it expanded aggressively, absorbing territory from neighboring realms such as [[Freia]].
