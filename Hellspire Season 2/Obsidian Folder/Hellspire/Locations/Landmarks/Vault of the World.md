## Overview

The Vault of the World is the tallest mountain in the world. It contains an ancient dwarven vault containing powerful technology and magic. This mountain is also home to the Silver Greatwyrm, who also guards this vault, only allowing those they seem fit to enter (or those that can defeat it).
