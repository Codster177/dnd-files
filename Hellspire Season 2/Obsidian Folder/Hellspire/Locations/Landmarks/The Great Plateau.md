## Overview

The Great Plateau is a large enough structure to be home to several caves and at least one temple: The Court of the Will of Copper. This temple was dedicated to the Copper Greatwyrm, a godlike dragon who blessed the dragonborn paladins with various boons for worship. This plateau is still home to the greatwyrm, yet it slumbers within the deepest caves of the structure, as it's days of involving itself in the world are long past. This is the location of the fight with Badger in season 2.
