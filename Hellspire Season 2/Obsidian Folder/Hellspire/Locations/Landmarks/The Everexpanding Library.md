## Overview

The Everexpanding Library is home to the Emerald Greatwyrm, who has the largest library In the world. Unlike most of the other greatwyrms, this demi-god does not slumber. Instead, it uses its mass psionic intellect to collect more information about the world to fill the books in its library. Once the library fills up, the greatwyrm expands deeper underground with its expertly crafted architecture.
