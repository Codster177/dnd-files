## Overview

Gaping Inferno is an Island formed by a volcano spanning many many miles, and that actively continues to govern the biome and slowly expand as it cools into more sediment. At the core fo the volcano is the Red Great-wrym's Nest. It hibernates within the magma core, and those know have heard myth of it living there have fortold that the world would be sent into a fiery apocalypse if it ever awoke. This is also the location of the final session of Season 2 Act 1, where the party encounters Gabble inside the Mech. By the time they awaken, the Mech is deep inside the volcano and is fighting the Greatwyrm while time is stopped. The Mech then fires a blow upon it, killing it instantly.
