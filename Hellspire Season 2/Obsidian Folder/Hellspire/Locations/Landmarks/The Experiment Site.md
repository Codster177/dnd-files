## Overview

In researching defenses against the infernal invasion, an arcane exeriment went wrong on the main island south of Orthscar, creating possibly the biggest magical explosion ever created. The explosion originated at the small town of Lakeleak and created a radius of over 30 miles, evicerating other towns and cities and corrupting the land permanatly.
