## Overview

**Twistgrowth** is a town in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Orthscar]]. As of 1127 it was held by [[Orthscar]].

Twistgrowth is likely the only town that has been physically affected by the arcane experiment but was not in range to get evicerated. It still stands and has adapted to a deformed environment.
