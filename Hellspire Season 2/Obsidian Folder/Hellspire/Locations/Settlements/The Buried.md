## Overview

**The Buried** is a town in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by Independent ([[The Wastes]]). As of 1127 it was held by Independent ([[The Wastes]]).

The only town to survive in the wastes, this is an old mining town that layed most of its infrastructure underground. They found the easiest way to defend from the invasion was to keep themselves underground, creating a labrinth entrance with different mining tunnels leading to traps and dead ends.
