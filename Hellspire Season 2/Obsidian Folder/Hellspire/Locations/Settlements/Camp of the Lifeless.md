## Overview

**Camp of the Lifeless** is a camp in the world of Hellspire. Its ownership in 1107 is unrecorded. As of 1127 it was held by Independent ([[The Sand Babies]]).

This is the camp of the sand babies, the group of imps who attempted to live differently than how normal imps live.
