## Overview

**Lootbridge City** is a city in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Bruibary Monarchy]]. By 1127 it lay in ruins, devastated by the infernal invasion.

Lootbridge City was initially destroyed by Lord Gallium in the siege of Lootbridge City. It then reformed itself into the Lootbridge Refuge, led by Layla Tenama during the early months of the invasion. This did not last forever, as the invasion eventually took back the city, with Layla Tenama evacuating the survivors to eventually adopt them into being citizens of New Freia.
