## Overview

**The Old Camp** is a camp in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[The Hellbringers]]. By 1127 it lay in ruins, devastated by the infernal invasion.

Despite being very close to the gate to hell, this camp was inhabited for many years by Ig the Emerald dragon after the opening of the gate to hell. However, Ig eventually abandoned it, leaving only his pet to guard the place, Gemstone.
