## Overview

**Slitcoast City** is a city in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by Independent ([[The Unkept Forest]]). As of 1127 it was held by [[The Al Empire]].

The city where Deyrale Dinglebottom originally grew up as a pit fighter. After escaping, he was able to quickly stow away on a pirate ship set to sail.
