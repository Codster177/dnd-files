## Overview

**Lord Jargle's Castle** is a keep in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Evergreen Kingdom]] (Lord [[Lord Jargle|Jargle]]). As of 1127 it was held by Lord [[Lord Jargle|Jargle]].

Lord Jargle's Castle (Or Leolar's Castle) is in apparent ruin from the outside. However, the castle itself is still in complete shape. The real castle exists in an extra dimensional space, allowing the real castle to stand while appearing demolished at the same time.
