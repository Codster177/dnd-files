## Overview

**The Landlocked Pass** is a town in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Territories of the Al Empire]]. By 1127 it lay in ruins, devastated by the infernal invasion.

This was territory the Al Empire took from the Kingdom of the Primitive to have a canal that allowed the Al Empire to colonize further to the Heartwood Tribe land.
