## Overview

**Coalmouth** is a capital in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Kingdom of Aflien]]. As of 1127 it was held by [[Kingdom of Aflien]].

The capital of The Kingdom of Aflien, Coalmouth, was the only city to survive the invasion. This city sprawled miles and was built inside of a mountain. The people of Coalmouth barricaded themselves inside the mountain to isolate themselves from the invasion. After the earthquake caused by the Silver Greatwyrm, it is unsure if Coalmouth survived the cave ins or if it was only partially damaged.
