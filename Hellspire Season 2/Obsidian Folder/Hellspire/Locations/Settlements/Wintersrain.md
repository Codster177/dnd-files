## Overview

**Wintersrain** is a district capital in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Freia]]. As of 1127 it was held by [[The Al Empire]].

Is a district capital in the Al Empire post Freia takeover. Oversees the Freia District. Is the size of a normal Capital. Home to Almint Freia prior to Season 1.
