## Overview

**City of the Damned** is a lawless port city on the [[Outcasts Coast]], historically the seat of the **Damned Guild** of pirates. Unknown to most of its inhabitants, the Guild was secretly ruled by the mind flayer pirate lord [[Lord Gallium]], who used the city as the staging ground for his schemes. It was here that the [[The Hellbringers|Hellbringers]] first came together, and the journey that began in its streets ultimately ended with the opening of the Gate to Hell. By 1127, two decades after the infernal invasion, the city lay in ruins.

## Notable Locations

- **The Broken-Bone Tavern** — A tavern run by the goblin barkeep [[Badger]], a secret worshipper of **Asmodeus**. Its basement held a hidden ritual room and the **Brazier of Hellish Fire**. Badger's check-in book secretly let him scry on anyone who signed it.
- **The Damned Manor** — [[Lord Gallium]]'s lair atop the hill above the city, containing chambers of flesh pods used to infest victims with mind flayer tadpoles.
- **The Sunken Arena** — A gladiator pit run by [[The Treasurer]], where [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] later met [[Malm]].
- **The Water-Blade Smithy** — The smithy run by the mute dwarf lieutenant [[Peace]].
- **The Marketplace** — Where the party met the artist and potion seller [[Frink]].
- **The Church and Docks** — Sites of Damned Guild activity, including executions and hidden passages into fleshy tunnels beneath the city.

## Season 1 Events

### Arrival of the Hellbringers

[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], crewmates aboard a Damned Guild pirate ship, were shipwrecked by a **Dragon Turtle** as the vessel approached the city. Rescued at sea by **Prince** [[Almint Freia|Almint]] — who was traveling to the city in search of a cure for his ailing father, **King** [[Gilles Freia]] — they arrived together and the [[The Hellbringers|Hellbringers]] were formed.

### Badger and the First Contact

At the **Broken-Bone Tavern**, [[Badger]] hired the party to clear a **Cranium Rat** infestation in the basement, where they discovered a shrine to **Asmodeus**. As the last rat died, [[Almint Freia|Almint]] heard the voice of [[Lord Gallium]] in his mind for the first time. In the basement, [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] forfeited his soul to Asmodeus, sealing a warlock pact via the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** and the devil [[Byrst]].

### The Abduction of Almint

Investigating a murdered pirate, [[Almint Freia|Almint]] was abducted from the tavern and infested with a mind flayer tadpole at the **Damned Manor**. He was secretly freed by the conflicted lieutenant [[Violence]] and recovered by Badger. Meanwhile [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] fought through the city's church, slew the Giff lieutenant [[Griffin Bullethole]], and witnessed the execution of their former **Captain** [[Camila Aleleaf]].

### The Lieutenants, the Manor, and the Burning

Tasked by Badger with killing Gallium's lieutenants to obtain the two keys to the vault at [[Safekeeps]], the party clashed with [[Peace]] at the **Water-Blade Smithy**, where Almint died and was revived blind by his parasite. They were lured to a dinner at the **Damned Manor** and met [[Lord Gallium]] in person, barely escaping as the **Broken-Bone Tavern** burned behind them. 

### The Sunken Arena and Return

After Almint's final death at the manor, [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] awoke imprisoned in the **Sunken Arena**, where he met the interdimensional detective [[Malm]]. With the dragon turtle [[Oogloo]], the party destroyed the arena and escaped to a coastal cove. Returning to find the city now controlled by the **Knights of Freia**, they ventured back through the arena's tunnels into Gallium's lair, where [[Malm]] freed the emerald dragon [[Ig]] from a tadpole-pumping transformation chamber. Learning of Gallium's plan to invade [[Lootbridge City]], the party departed the city for the Bruibary Monarchy.

## Season 2 Events

### Ruins of the Damned

Twenty years later (1127), [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] emerged from the Gate to Hell at the ruins of [[Safekeeps]] and followed a ruined path back to the **City of the Damned**, now a desolate wasteland watched over by a distant spire. Among the ruins, Fel played his spectral violin to attract survivors and drew the attention of the tiefling [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. As the two took each other's measure, a **Bone Devil** stalked the ruins, and a shapeshifting **Yochlol** disguised as a crying woman ambushed them. The arrival of the warforged [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] — dropped from the sky in a summoning pod — helped turn the fight, marking the first gathering of the [[The Fellowship of the Gate|Fellowship of the Gate]].

### The Manor Revisited

South of the city, the party explored a cove that had become [[Ig]]'s nest (the [[The Old Camp|Old Camp]] where the Hellbringers once stayed), uncovering a shrine to [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and research notes authored by [[Malm]]. Returning north to the city, they investigated the ruined **Damned Manor** atop the hill, where [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] fell partway through the floor and was revealed to be two imps — [[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]] — stacked into a tiefling illusion.
