## Overview

**Lightbane City** is a city in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[The Al Empire]]. As of 1127 it was held by [[The Arc Rebellion]].

Hidden beneath the city is the home of the Emerald Greatwyrm. This is also the temporary capital of the Arc Rebellion.
