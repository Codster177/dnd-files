## Overview

**Forest Guard Village** is a town in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[The Spire]]. As of 1127 it was held by [[New Freia]].

The original home of Ig, New Freia would eventually make it's central home base here, with it's nature being protected by the radiating effect of the Emerald dragon's lair.
