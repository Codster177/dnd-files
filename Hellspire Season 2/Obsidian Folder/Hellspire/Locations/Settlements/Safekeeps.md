## Overview

**Safekeeps** is a town in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Outcasts Coast]] (The Damned Guild). By 1127 it had become the site of the Gate to Hell.

Originally the location of Lord Gallium's Vault, Safekeeps is now the location of the Gate to Hell, after it formed when the Hellbringers opened the vault.
