## Overview

**Greenrim** is a capital in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[Evergreen Kingdom]]. By 1127 it lay in ruins, devastated by the infernal invasion.

This was a major target in the invasion of the nine hells. Greenrim was home to the College of the Rift, which housed the artifact the Demonomicon. This allowed the forces of hell to gain a significant advantage to the Blood war.
