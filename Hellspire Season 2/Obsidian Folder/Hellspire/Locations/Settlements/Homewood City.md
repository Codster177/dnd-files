## Overview

**Homewood City** is a city in the world of Hellspire. As of 1107 it was held by [[The Spire]]. By 1127 it lay in ruins, devastated by the infernal invasion.

The closest city to the rural home of Aji, Myla, and Gabble Skipplestump.
