# Season 1 Summary — The Hellbringers

The **Hellbringers** were a band of unlikely allies who came together aboard a pirate ship and, across a desperate journey through the City of the Damned, were dragged ever deeper into the schemes of the mind flayer pirate lord [[Lord Gallium]]. Their story ended with the opening of the Gate to Hell.

The founding members were [[Gabble Skipplestump]], a gnome wizard who would become a warlock; [[Deyrale Dinglebottom]], a goliath barbarian; and [[Almint Friea]], an aarakocra cleric and prince of Friea. Later, after Almint's death, the plasmoid rogue [[Malm]] joined the party.

---

### The Crew of the Damned

[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] began as crewmates aboard a pirate ship of the **Damned Guild**, having been recruited only days earlier under **Captain** [[Camila Aleleaf]]. As the ship approached the City of the Damned for the first time, it was ambushed by merfolk and a merfolk priest. The crew fought back, but as the priest fell, he called upon the ocean for aid — and a **Dragon Turtle** answered, tearing the ship apart.

As most of the crew perished and Captain Aleleaf fled toward the ship's escape rowboat, Gabble and Deyrale ensured she never reached it; Gabble vortex-warped her deep into the ocean. The two escaped the sinking ship with the only other survivor, a man named [[Kix]], and set out rowing toward the city.

Stranded at sea, the three were rescued by a royal yacht belonging to **Prince** [[Almint Friea|Almint]], who was traveling to the City of the Damned in search of a cure for his ailing father, **King** [[Gilles Freia|Gilles Friea]]. Hoping the survivors could guide him to a cure, Almint joined them against his advisor's judgment, and the party was formed.

### The City of the Damned

Arriving at the city, Kix split off from the group. In the marketplace, the party met an artist and potion seller named [[Frink]], who all but begged them to buy his unlabeled, mysterious wares. They relented, unknowingly acquiring a **Tonic of God's Touch** (allowing communion with one's deity), a **Philter of Cold Resistance**, and a **Draught of the Berserker**. The latter two mattered little; the Tonic of God's Touch would prove pivotal.

The party made their way to the **Broken-Bone Tavern**, run by a goblin barkeep named [[Badger]]. Badger hired them to clear an infestation in the basement and had them sign his check-in book — a book that secretly allowed him to scry on anyone whose name it held. Below, the party discovered a hidden ritual room dedicated to the worship of **Asmodeus**. Badger appeared behind them, insisting his religion was no concern of theirs, and the party went on to exterminate an infestation of **Cranium Rats**. As the last rat died, [[Almint Friea|Almint]] heard a deep voice in his mind: *"I see, royalty hmmm…"* — the first unknowing contact from [[Lord Gallium]], the secret mind flayer ruling the Damned Guild.

After being paid, the party took up Badger's offer of free rooms. In private, [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] asked Badger about his powers, and Badger offered him the same. He led Gabble to the basement and used the **Brazier of Hellish Fire** to open a small rift to Hell, through which stepped [[Byrst]], an amnizu devil and the representative of Badger's patron. After negotiation, Gabble forfeited his soul to Asmodeus, sealing a warlock pact and beginning to multiclass as a wizard and warlock.

### The Abduction of Almint

While Gabble was below, [[Almint Friea|Almint]] noticed a frantic pirate flee the tavern and followed him to a hut near the docks. Peering through the window, Almint watched the man barricade himself in fear — and in the brief moment Almint glanced away, the pirate was killed by an arrow between the eyes. The vanished assassins bore the marks of the Damned Guild. Almint reported his findings to [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], and his royal advisor agreed to keep watch through the night.

By morning the advisor was dead, Almint's room ransacked, and the prince gone. Following the clue of the murdered pirate, Gabble and Deyrale went to the docks, killed two Damned Guild pirates cleaning the crime scene, and disguised themselves in their clothes. The trail led to the city's church, where they witnessed the towering Giff lieutenant [[Griffin Bullethole]] commanding pirates. Pirates dragged in a prisoner for execution — [[Camila Aleleaf|Captain Aleleaf]], condemned by Lord Gallium for the loss of her ship and crew. Before Griffin executed her, she named Gabble and Deyrale as her former crew, turning the pirates against them.

Fleeing the ensuing battle, the two found a makeshift prison and a hidden passage beneath the church bathroom, descending into cavernous, fleshy tunnels. [[Griffin Bullethole|Griffin]] pursued them, and though gravely wounded, they killed him. Too injured to follow the tunnels further, they retreated to the tavern with a note written in Deep Speech.

Meanwhile, [[Almint Friea|Almint]] awoke trapped in a fleshy capsule. An alien hand reached in and pressed a tadpole into his eye, where it burrowed into his head before he fell unconscious. He awoke again in the Broken-Bone Tavern, tended by [[Badger]], who claimed to have found him outside the **Damned Manor** on the hill above the city. Unknown to all, a pirate lieutenant named [[Violence]] — seeking to undo Lord Gallium's corruption — had freed Almint from the flesh cage and left him where Badger could scry and recover him. Now able to read Deep Speech, Almint translated the note: *"Bring him to the manor. — Lord Gallium,"* the party's first knowledge of the pirate lord's name.

### The Prophetic Dreams

Exhausted, each party member dreamed a vision of things to come:

- **[[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]** dreamed of a mermaid wielding the largest, most beautiful axe he had ever seen — pulled away just before he could claim it. This foreshadowed the lieutenant [[Fish]] and the **Greataxe of Certain Death**.
- **[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]]** dreamed of his dead mother, [[Myla Skipplestump]], knitting outside his childhood home. She asked, *"Was it worth it?"* before fire erupted behind her melting eyes. This foreshadowed both Myla's own warlock pact and the melting of Gabble's left eye.
- **[[Almint Friea|Almint]]** dreamed of his dying father, [[Gilles Freia|Gilles Friea]], who asked *"Who are you?"* before sinking into an inky mass that birthed a dark silhouette of Almint himself — foreshadowing the tadpole's coming conquest of his body.

### The Lieutenants and the Keys

[[Badger]] offered the party a new job: kill the Pirate Lieutenants of the Damned Guild. The two most trusted lieutenants each carried a key, and together those keys could open the vault at **Safekeeps** — said to hold both a health cure and a powerful weapon. Gabble increasingly felt a hellish mental pressure compelling him to obey. Badger also detected the tadpole inside [[Almint Friea|Almint]], realized it gave Lord Gallium a window into the party, and cast them out of the tavern.

The party targeted the lieutenant [[Peace]], a mute dwarf who ran the **Water-Blade Smithy**. Their ambush failed when Peace got the drop on them. In the fight, Almint fell and died after a botched medicine check by [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] — but the parasite revived his brain, leaving him blind. The lieutenant [[Violence]], Peace's sister, arrived pleading for peace, but an enraged Deyrale killed [[Peace]] anyway, and Violence refused to help them further. Almint's blindness was offset by a new **creature sense** that let him see souls through walls, accompanied by tentacles sprouting from his eye sockets. The party brought [[Violence]] to Badger, who executed her before she could speak; Deyrale buried her on the coast.

Returning to the abandoned smithy, the party met [[Barnacle]], a sorcerer claiming to be Lord Gallium's second-in-command. He invited the party to a dinner hosted by the pirate lord and revealed he held one of the two keys as bait. At the **Damned Manor**, the party found jars of brains and gray, husk-like butlers, then met [[Lord Gallium]] in person — a mind flayer who devoured the cooked brain of [[Violence]] as the main course. He offered the party positions as lieutenants; when they declined, he ordered Barnacle to kill them. As [[Barnacle]] faltered, Gallium disintegrated him in a flash of green and rose from his chair, and the party barely escaped.

Outside, they found the Broken-Bone Tavern in flames. They fled to Almint's ship, only to discover it overrun by disguised pirates; the party broke the wheel and escaped by rowboat, leaving the ship stranded. [[Kix]] departed north toward the **Bruibary Monarchy**. Here [[Almint Friea|Almint]] received a vision from **Cthulhu** and realized the truth of his powers: never having truly worshipped a god, his cleric magic had begun drawing its strength from Cthulhu ever since he reached the City of the Damned.

### Almint's Final Death

[[Badger]] rejoined the party — he had survived the tavern's burning by turning into a raven — and insisted the job continue. On the way back, the party met [[Gus]], a dragonborn paladin searching for the dragon he served. Approaching the Damned Manor from its coastal dock, Gus split off, and the party found a secret chamber of flesh pods in the master bedroom — the very place Almint had been infected.

Below, a mind flayer seized control of [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and turned him on [[Almint Friea|Almint]], beating him unconscious; Almint died once more when Deyrale again failed to save him. The party preserved his body in a **Bag of Holding**. They then encountered [[Fish]], a mute plasmoid brute and Gallium's true second-in-command, a shining key embedded in his translucent chest. Holding [[Gus]]'s severed head, Fish beat both Gabble and Deyrale unconscious.

[[Almint Friea|Almint]]'s soul ascended into space, witnessing the ages of the world pass before coming face to face with **Cthulhu**, who cast his soul toward a pit of endless torment — only to retract it with the words *"Not yet."* [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] awoke in a flesh cage and was infested with a tadpole, but escaped the manor and found [[Badger]] fleeing pirates. In the forest, Badger summoned [[Byrst]] once more, and together they burned away the left side of Gabble's face to kill the tadpole — leaving half his skull exposed as the two smiled an evil grin.

### The Sunken Arena

[[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] awoke in a cell of the **Sunken Arena**, a gladiator pit, sunk in despair over slaying [[Almint Friea|Almint]] while controlled, falling to [[Fish]], and reliving the gladiator life of his youth. His grief was interrupted by a small plasmoid stuck to his back — [[Malm]], an interdimensional detective investigating [[Lord Gallium]]'s crimes, who had decided Gabble and Deyrale were his best path forward. The two bonded over Deyrale's trauma and prepared for the morning's battle.

[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] awoke to find a glowing eye set in the melted side of his face — a "gift" from [[Byrst]]. It flashed him a memory of his mother's abuse, then a vision of Deyrale and Malm about to fight in the arena. He rushed to aid them. In the pit, the cruel owner known as [[The Treasurer]] reveled in the slaughter and sold the audience cannon shots to fire upon the fighters. When the Treasurer loosed a baby dragon turtle into the arena, [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] calmed the creature and helped it cough up a lodged **javelin** — his own, thrown into the sea during the first dragon turtle attack, now magically charged. With the dragon turtle, named **Oogloo**, the party destroyed the arena and escaped, rescuing an enslaved gladiator named [[Dama]], a warlock like Gabble.

### The Cove

[[Oogloo]] carried the party to a cove, which they fortified into a base camp over the following days. That night, [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], unable to sleep, saw a memory-visage of [[Almint Friea|Almint]] who forgave him and urged him onward. [[Malm]] logged his evidence against the pirate lord in his journal — a journal that would prove important in Season 2. [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] dreamed of a husk of Almint being assembled and revived, and in the morning convinced the party to return to the City of the Damned to learn the truth.

### The Imposter Prince

The party found the city now controlled by the **Knights of Friea**. Guards revealed that **Prince** [[Almint Friea|Almint]] was somehow alive and had departed by ship, claiming the party had caused his disappearance and that [[Lord Gallium]] had been his savior. Unable to catch the ship, the party instead went through tunnels beneath the ruined Sunken Arena to investigate the manor again. They defeated [[The Treasurer]] in a fierce rematch, pressed deeper into Gallium's lair, and found a transformation chamber pumping tadpoles into an emerald dragon named [[Ig]]. [[Malm]] freed Ig and removed the tadpoles, and the dragon tunneled the party to freedom — but in the chaos, [[Dama]] was quietly taken by creatures of the lair. That night Gabble dreamed of Dama being dissected to her death by mind flayers, and the party reluctantly chose not to search for her.

Seeking information, the party infiltrated the Damned Guild's main encampment, now held by the Knights of Friea, where [[Malm]] learned of Gallium's plan to invade **Lootbridge City** in the Bruibary Monarchy. Spotted while leaving by a knight named [[Sir Albert]], the party kidnapped him and flew toward Lootbridge aboard [[Ig]], convincing him along the way that Almint's accusations were lies crafted by Lord Gallium.

### Lootbridge City

The party found Lootbridge City in ruins, mind flayers roaming its streets. After a clash, a mysterious man finished the last mind flayer and revealed himself as [[Kix]], who led them to a resistance holding out in a cathedral. Among the survivors, [[Kix]] revealed his prisoner: [[Badger]], who had willingly surrendered himself. Badger asked to speak with [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] alone and revealed that his shackles were mere illusion. His plan: use the resistance to assault [[Lord Gallium]] and seize the vault keys. When Gabble feigned agreement, Badger saw through him and warned that resisting his infernal calling would only bring worse upon him. He handed Gabble the **Brazier of Hellish Fire**, telling him he would know when to use it.

In the cathedral, [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] and [[Malm]] reunited with [[Frink]], who supplied healing potions, and met the artificer [[Harold Gobblefern|Harold Gobblefirn]], who sold magic items. [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] intimidated Harold into surrendering a **Helm of Teleportation**, a **Folding Boat**, and an **Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location**. The party rallied a small battalion to board the royal ship carrying the controlled prince. [[Sir Albert]] gave them his **Knight Medallion** as proof of friendship, and [[Ig]] flew them to the ship.

### The Battle for the Royal Ship

Apprehended by the knights and brought before **Captain** [[Layla Tenama]], the party deepened her existing suspicions of [[Almint Friea|Almint]] by revealing he was controlled by [[Lord Gallium]]. Confronted, Almint's body unraveled into a mass of tentacles, and a massive battle erupted across the ship as hidden pirates among the crew turned on the rest. The resistance arrived, and the knights defended Layla and the party, but the Almint-abomination was monstrously strong — its jaws tore away [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]]'s ear and eye and savaged his brain. The party killed the creature and cast it into the sea, but Deyrale awoke with **amnesia**, devastating [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] and [[Malm]].

In desperation, Gabble drank the **Tonic of God's Touch** in the captain's quarters, expecting [[Byrst]]. Instead, **Asmodeus** himself — the true holder of Gabble's soul — emerged from the portal. He offered a deal: power to defeat [[Lord Gallium]] in exchange for Gabble's **body and mind**, to be possessed indefinitely whenever Asmodeus chose. Gabble asked for time; Asmodeus instead dragged him to **Stygia**, the fifth layer of Hell, showed him his own soul drifting down the River Styx, and left him to freeze. Refusing at first, then dying in the snow, Gabble found a dotted line — his only escape — and signed.

He awoke unharmed in the captain's quarters, blinded by a brilliant light in his pocket: a **mote of pure energy**, a contained **Wish** spell. The light shone through the ship's cracks, catching [[Malm]]'s eye below. Gabble wished: *"I wish for Malm, Deyrale, and Kix to retain their original sense of self, and to have eternal life."* [[Malm]] felt rejuvenated, while [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] regained his eye, ear, and memories in an instant, and [[Kix]] regained his lost hand and sight.

As Malm confronted Gabble over what he had done, Deyrale spotted an axe head punching through the hull. [[Fish]] broke into the sinking ship, and battle resumed. Fish's critical blow split the ship in two, and amid a storm of his wild magic, many knights went down — though the party saved several, including [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]]. [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] finally killed [[Fish]] and claimed the **Greataxe of Certain Death**, the very axe from his dream.

### The Nautiloid

Reaching the surface, the party beheld [[Lord Gallium]]'s ship for the first time — a flying **nautiloid** locked in battle with [[Ig]] and raining destruction below. The party and [[Kix]] boarded the nautiloid, where Gallium sprang a trap, revealing the fallen Pirate Lieutenants he had remade into mind flayer abominations. When Gallium loosed a ray of disintegration at [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], [[Kix]] threw himself in its path, held the energy in his chest, rushed Gallium, and grabbed hold — the disintegration consuming them both. Gallium was reduced to ash, and Kix vanished without a trace. The party found one vault key aboard and left the ship.

On the beach, reunited with [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]], the party was approached by [[Oogloo]] and his father **Loo** — the dragon turtle they had fought at the very beginning — who bore the monstrous body of [[Almint Friea|Almint]] from the sea floor. The party held a funeral, finally freeing Almint's soul to the afterlife, and found the **second vault key** within his body. With both keys in hand, [[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] was gripped by mounting dread, knowing his infernal calling forced him to pursue the vault even as he sensed something terrible would come of it.

### The Vault and the Gate

The party traveled with [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]] to **Safekeeps**, the pirate lord's vault. They fought past the elite **Vault Pirates**, used both keys to open the vault door, and beheld its treasures. Among them was a **Deck of Many Things**: [[Malm]] drew **Rogue**, turning an ally against him, while [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] drew **Gem**, conjuring 50,000 gp in gems at his feet.

[[Gabble Skipplestump|Gabble]] did not draw. Consumed by dread, his consciousness was pulled back to his soul as **Asmodeus** chose that moment to claim his vessel. Gabble awoke on the cliffs of **Stygia** where he had signed the deal — but this time he felt no cold, only contentment, with the soul of his mother [[Myla Skipplestump]] beside him. Myla revealed that she too had been a warlock who signed away her soul; when her devil refused to release her and began possessing her, she took her own life rather than risk harming Gabble or his father, [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]]. When Gabble asked what they should do now, Myla answered, *"There is nothing to do now,"* and they returned to the River Styx.

The party realized Gabble's change too late. He seized the **Wand of Portals** from the vault, placed it in the **Brazier of Hellish Fire**, and began opening a permanent **Gate to Hell**. [[Malm]], [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]], and [[Layla Tenama|Captain Layla]] fought to stop him — but [[Badger]] arrived to ensure the ritual succeeded, and hurled [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Deyrale]] into the **Nine Hells**, where the party watched him vanish.

The ritual was completed. The massive portal tore open, collapsing the vault's supports and burying the structure in on itself. The Gate to Hell stood open — and there, Season 1 came to its end.
