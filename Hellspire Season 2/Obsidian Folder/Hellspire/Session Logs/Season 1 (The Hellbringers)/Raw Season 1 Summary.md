The hellbringers is comprised of Gabble Skipplestump, Deyrale Dinglebottom, and Almint Friea. Gabble is a gnome wizard/warlock, Deyrale is a goliath barbarian, and Almint is a aarokocra cleric. They are the characters that the players adopted during season 1. Later in season 1 after Almint dies, Malm joins the party. Malm is a plasmoid rouge. The hellbringers is first formed on a pirate ship, where Gabble and Deyrale are crewmates who have spent the last of days together. This crew is apart of the larger pirate guild, known as the Damned Guild. They are arriving at the City of the Damned for the first time after being recruited to the crew in the last couple of days. They serve under captain Camila Aleleaf. A few miles before the crew reaches the City of the Damned for the first time, the ship is ambushed by merfolk and a merfolk priest. The crew fights back, but as the merfolk priest is being defeated, he calls upon the ocean for help. The ship is then attacked by a Dragon Turtle. Most of the crew perishes, and captain Aleleaf begins to retreat to the escape rowboat the ship has stored. Gabble and Deyrale ensure she does not make it to the rowboat, as Gabble vortex warps her deeper into the ocean. They escape the sinking ship with the only other remaining crewmate, a man named Kix. Now stranded at sea, the three begin rowing in the direction they believe the City of the Damned is in. On the way, they are rescued by a Royal Yacht, belonging to Prince Almint Friea, who is traveling to the City of the Damned searching for a cure for his father. Almint brings the survivors aboard, and hoping that they can lead him in the direction of a cure, he joins with them, against the judgement of his advisor. They now form the party, consisting of Gabble, Deyrale, and Almint. Arriving at the City of the Damned, Kix splits off from the party. Gabble began feeling an overwhelming sense of stress and dread, knowing that he is forced pursue the vault due to his infernal calling, but also feeling doing so will result in something terrible. However, he could not put these feelings into words, and the party continued on. The party returned back to their camp with Captain Layla, unknowingly resting together for one final time. This rest is rough for Gabble, as this sense of dread is growing. After the rest, the party and Captain Layla continued and made their way towards Safekeeps, the location of the Pirate Lord's vault. Arriving at Safekeeps, the party began to infiltrate the vault, being attacked by the Vault Pirates, highly trained pirates recruited to protect the Vault. The party was able to defeat them, and made their way deeper into the vault. Approaching the vault door, the party used the keys to open the vault, and witnessed the various treasures lying inside. Exploring the treasures, the party experimented with a deck of cards that had a great impact on the world around them, known as the Deck of Many Things. Malm pulled the card Rogue, causing a npc to turn against him (Not decided yet), and Deyrale pulled the card Gem, causing 50,000 GP worth of gems to appear at his feet. However, Gabble was not engaging with the cards. He had been consumed by stress and dread, having an out of body experience. No, this was worse than an out of body experience, as this was actually his consciousness returning to soul, with Asmodeus choosing now to take over Gabble as a vessel. Gabble awakened on the cliffside of Stygia where he signed the deal. However, this time, he could not feel the cold and felt content, and the soul of his mother Myla Skipplestump was next to him. Here, Myla revealed to Gabble that during life, Myla was a warlock who had signed her soul away to a devil, similar to Gabble. Myla had left her adventuring life and tried to remove herself from her pact, but the devil refused, possessing her from time to time. Fearing that she would eventually hurt Aji, Gabbles father, or Gabble in one of these fits, she killed herself. Their bonding did not last long however, as when Gabble asked "What do we do now?", Myla answered "There is nothing to do now." and they returned to the river styx to continue their afterlife. The party was alerted to Gabble's change in his mind too late, as they witnessed Gabble grab one of the treasures in the vault, the Wand of Portals, and place it into the Brazier of Hellish fire. He began opening a permanent gate to hell, and Malm, Deyrale, and Captain Layla were forced to stop him, entering a fight. Gabble had help however, as Badger emerged into the Vault to ensure the gate opened smoothly. During the fight, Deyrale was thrown into the Nine Hells by Badger, to which the party saw him disappear. Eventually, the ritual to open this portal was completed, and the party failed. The massive portal opened, breaking the supports of the vault and having the structure crumble in on itself. The portal to hell was now open, and that is where Season 1 of the story ended.
