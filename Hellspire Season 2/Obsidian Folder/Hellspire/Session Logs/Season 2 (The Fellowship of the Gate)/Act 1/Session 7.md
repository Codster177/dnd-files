## Saga20:
The party soared through the overcast sky aboard the emerald dragon [[Ig]], leaving behind the smoldering ruins of Fort Lake Gap and the distant menace of The Engine of Nessus. Their destination was New Friea, a mountain sanctuary where Queen [[Layla Tenama]] hoped to regroup and plan a counterstrike against the hellish incursion. During the eight-hour flight, the companions rested and reflected, each in their own way. [[Zilton Zalzalm]], the warforged monk, stood sentinel, observing his allies with mechanical precision. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], the disguised twin-imps, studied a mysterious amulet and later indulged in the tavern's games. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], the soul displaced into a gnomish body, explored his physical form and later performed music that drew both coin and curious glances. Upon landing in the cavernous hangar of Emerald Mountain, the heroes were swept into the bustling bureaucracy of the last free kingdom, where they accepted a royal commission, recruited an unlikely engineer, bartered for magical gear, gambled into the night, and faced a haunting dream that revealed the true owner of [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s stolen body.

### Key Events

1. **The Long Flight and Midnight Vigil**

The dragon's back was wide enough for the entire company, with Queen [[Layla Tenama]] and Captain [[Sabrina Liaro]] near the neck and the three heroes clustered further aft. As the wind howled past, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] remained motionless, his sentry mode cataloguing every detail. He watched [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] trance, noting the subtle imperfections in the tiefling's illusion: hair that moved unnaturally in the wind, a faint shimmer at the edges. When [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] rummaged through his pack, a few stray papers fluttered into the void, but the vital notes of [[Malm]] were safe. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] also took a moment to examine his own body with a soldier's practicality, discovering the maul, spirit spears, and dungeoneer's pack that had belonged to the warrior [[Deyrale Dinglebottom]]. Meanwhile, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] focused on the amulet retrieved from [[Gabble Skipplestump]]'s lair, his arcane senses revealing it to be a potent spell focus that could enhance his magic if properly attuned, though it would require a future ritual to integrate with his sword.

2. **Arrival at** Emerald Mountain

Below the clouds, the landscape shifted from red desert to a muddy, deforested expanse dotted with dead trees. Then, a walled village appeared, nestled against a mountain, and beyond it a patch of green. [[Ig]] banked toward a massive opening in the mountainside, a hangar-like cavern where a cluster of officials awaited the queen. As the dragon touched down, the delegation swarmed [[Layla Tenama|Layla]] with urgent reports: shipments from Gemstone City, protests from a Manifestor group, a missing ambassador. The queen addressed the party over the clamor, instructing them to meet her in the Emerald Palace below after she dealt with the immediate crises. The three heroes descended a grand staircase into the mountain, entering a gathering hall that was modestly furnished save for an emerald chandelier. Citizens lined up to petition advisors, and the air hummed with the orderly chaos of a fledgling government.

3. **The Queen's Commission**

[[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], overcome by the sight of civilization, climbed onto a table and began to play his spectral violin. The melody was pleasant enough to earn a few annoyed glances from busy clerks but also a gold piece from a passing dwarf, who mentioned a tavern called Cats. When Queen [[Layla Tenama|Layla]] entered and took her throne, the room's murmur rose. She summoned the three forward, gently chiding [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] for the distraction but praising his skill. Then, with the gravity of a ruler who had seen too much loss, she offered them a place as a separate garrison of the Royal Knights of New Friea. In return for their service, they would receive resources, lodging, and support in their quest to close the Gate to Hell. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] accepted immediately, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] agreed with enthusiasm, and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] gave a cautious affirmation. The queen handed [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] a token bearing her crown, which would grant them access to the magical wares of [[Harold Gobblefern]] and the services of the mountain's blacksmith. She also suggested they recruit additional members, hinting that Captain [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]] might be willing, and advised them to visit Gemstone City's library for lore on unicorns and stags. A sending stone was promised so they could stay in contact.

4. **The Lift and the Engineer**

Descending the mechanical escalators, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] felt a rare flicker of approval at the organized transit system, so reminiscent of Mechanus. On a lift deeper into the mountain, they encountered a half-elf named [[Skip Leafing]], who managed automatons in the industrial district. When [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] mentioned their mission to close the hell portal, [[Skip Leafing]] was awestruck and immediately offered to join their garrison. The warforged, ever direct, accepted on the spot, but [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] intervened, concerned for the man's safety. After persuasion and intimidation that made [[Skip Leafing|Skip]]'s eyes darken with thaumaturgy, and a compromise, they convinced him to keep his job for one week and attempt to build a combat-ready automaton. If he succeeded, he could join them. [[Skip Leafing]] agreed, though his enthusiasm had already cost him his position; he was last seen shamefully returning to his workstation as the party continued toward [[Harold Gobblefern|Harold]]'s office.

5. **Harold's Workshop and New Allies**

The office of [[Harold Gobblefern]] was a cluttered workshop filled with ticking constructs and the smell of oil. The elderly gnome, bald with a long gray beard, was tinkering with a disabled robot and barely looked up until the queen's token was presented. He provided the sending stone and listened as they inquired about unicorn horns and stag blood. He had no such rarities, explaining that unicorns were creatures of legend even before the invasion, and the nearest untouched forest lay hundreds of miles east or north. While [[Harold Gobblefern|Harold]] rummaged for magic items, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s gaze fell on a powered-down mechanical dog in the corner. The gnome activated the canine construct and gifted it to the warforged, who named it Dot. From a selection of rare and uncommon items, the party chose a cloak of displacement for [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] and a helm of comprehend languages for [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. [[Harold Gobblefern|Harold]] also agreed to research a way to counter The Engine of Nessus, intrigued by the challenge. As they left, they spotted [[Skip Leafing]] through a window, miserably sawing at air, and called out encouragement.

6. **Steel, Stone, and Memory**

The trail down the mountainside led to a cobblestone path and, at last, grass. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] knelt to touch it, a simple joy after so much desolation. The tunnel smithy was manned by a no-nonsense human woman who, upon seeing the token, agreed to forge [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] a suit of steel plate armor. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] attempted to barter with a performance, but his nervous solo fell flat, and the blacksmith dryly suggested he stick to crowds. While they waited, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] visited the Freya Monument, a hilltop park with a fountain, statues, and walls of slate inscribed with the names of the fallen. He studied the silver effigies of King [[Gilles Freia]] and Prince [[Almint Friea|Almond]], and the marble memorials to [[Deyrale Dinglebottom]] and [[Malm]]. A newspaper article from the era revealed that King [[Gilles Freia|Gilles]] had adopted the infant [[Almint Friea|Almond]], a rare bird-like humanoid, sparking tensions with the human-supremacist Empire of Al. The prince had later found a new homeland for his people, now called New Friea. The monument's walls served as a graveyard, with a stand where citizens could record their lost loved ones.

7. Cats **Tavern: Cards and Coin**

The tavern called Cats was a warm, raucous establishment run by [[Catherine]], a young human woman, and her half-tabaxi stepbrother [[Ninsk]], who managed the gambling tables. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] took up a spot near the bar and began to play, his music this time flowing effortlessly, earning him a steady stream of tips. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] and [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] approached the blackjack table, where [[Ninsk]] dealt cards with a practiced flick. The warforged, unfamiliar with the game, received a quick lesson and promptly won his first hand, his mechanical mind grasping the probabilities. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] bet copper pieces and soon lost them all, but the real drama came when [[Skip Leafing]] stumbled in, having indeed lost his job. He threw three gold pieces onto the table, his life savings, and promptly lost everything. The engineer passed out at the bar, and [[Catherine]] enlisted her sons to carry him upstairs.

8. **The Queen's Visit and the Night's Revels**

Around four in the afternoon, Queen [[Layla Tenama|Layla]] arrived with two guards. She bobbed her head to [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s music, then sat at the blackjack table, betting modestly and chatting with the heroes. She was pleased they had acquired the sending stone and reminded them to check in daily. The queen proved a lucky gambler, winning four gold pieces, which she promptly used to reimburse anyone who had lost, including the unconscious [[Skip Leafing]]. As the night deepened, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] drank heavily, his constitution pushed to the limit. When the queen departed, the tiefling's disguise began to falter, but the imp [[Andhal]] worked frantically to maintain the illusion while [[Nampha]] kept the body upright. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] and [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] helped the staggering warlock to a room, where he collapsed into two separate imps before passing out. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] retired to his own chamber to read the dragon tome, while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] settled into a fitful sleep.

9. **The Dream and the Accusation**

In his dreams, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] found himself back in the dungeon, but this time he wore his original half-elven body. A rat scurried past, and he followed it through the dark corridors until it clawed at a cell door. When he opened the food hatch, the rat was snatched by a man on the other side. The door swung open to reveal a face [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] knew from mirrors: the gnomish body he now inhabited, but with a trimmed beard, a scar over one eye, and a wild desperation. The man accused him of stealing his body, of dragging him back from the brink of his goal in the Nine Hells. He demanded his body back, his voice cracking with fury and despair. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] apologized but refused, unable to undo what had happened. The man cursed him and slammed the door, leaving [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] to wander the dream until dawn.
