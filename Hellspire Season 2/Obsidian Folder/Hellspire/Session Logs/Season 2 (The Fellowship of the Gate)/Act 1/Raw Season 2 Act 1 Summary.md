### Session 1
Season 2 begins 20 years after the Gate to Hell opened (1/10/1127). We follow Fel, the over 6000 year old half-elven soul that has found its way into a man who fell into the river styx. The first scene opens as Fel journeys out of the gate and into the barren wasteland that Al has become. He is in what was Safekeeps, the site where Lord Gallium stored his vault. However, that location is virtually lost to time it seems, as here Fel witnesses a looming portal and a spire that watches over it. As Fel exits up the stairs that descend into the portal, he hears a faint squish and finds two pages. One paper has a logical rune on it, with words at the bottom saying "BURN THIS PAGE". The other page states the following: "The statues in these vaults resemble the Pirate Lord well. One of these statue's eyes has a faint glow, while the other does not. While the case is closed, this should be noted as important evidence for future cases." (Hinting that these pages are authored by Malm just before and after the gate to hell opened). Fel looked upon the spire, spotting some very large ravens and a couple hundred imps sleeping throughout the bottom floor. (Approximately 934 imps). The spire has a large crystal at the top swirling with energy. (Unknown to the players, this spire allows for souls collected in AL to be transferred back to the Nine Hells for easy transfer). Fel then discovered a ruined path leading back to the City of the Damned, and he decided to follow it. Just getting the hang of his new perspective, Fel puts it together in his head that his new body is much shorter than his old one, hinting that he has possessed the body of a smaller race. Arriving at the ruins of the City of the Damned, Fel decided to engage with the spectral energy he possesses and plays his spectral violin to attempt to attract any survivors. This attracts the attention of the Tiefling Na'Hal, who approaches. Attempting to figure out any information about each other, they hear the approach of a Bone Devil and swiftly hide. The bone devil searches the ruins for about an hour, as the two hide, and eventually a dark elf woman begins crying out for help, attracting the attention of the two and of the Bone Devil. Fel and Na'Hal approach the woman, and she reveals herself to actually be a shapeshifting Yochlol, who attacks. While in combat, Fel burns the runic paper, summoning a digital beam from the sky, and landing the summoning pod of warforged Zilton Zalzam upon the bone devil. The fight ensues, and encountering deja vu, Fel witnesses a memory of the body he possesses: a grewsome battle with a different devil within the sixth layer of hell: Malbolge, giving a small bit of insight into his body's past. Towards the end of the battle, the bone devil saw through Na'Hal's disguise, calling him out in Infernal: "Why do you betray your own kind?". When the devil saw Na'Hal produce a spell with a radiant effect, the devil proclaimed: "Thats impossible". The demon perished, and Na'Hal banished the bone devil back to the nine hells. The party comes to a realization: where did the demon come from, when devils come from the nine hells? The first session ends with a after credit cutscene for the players (characters have no knowledge of this): The camera panned in to see a ruffled Deyrale Dinglebottom in the depths of hell waking up for the first time (4/20/1107). Approaching before him, in a gust of smoke, Charon: God of the Dead approaches. As Deyrale looks up, Charon speaks to him saying "That is mine." as he effortlessly plucks the Greataxe of Certain Death from Deyrales hands.

### Session 2 + 3
Session 2 begins with a cutscene as well: The scene is set at the Mechanus Agency. Modron units named 1000000000000000001000 and 1000000000000000001001 receive an emergency distress signal. It describes that it is from an investigator that has been following a dangerous mindflayer that has traveled the universe and found its way to Al. However, the current threat was of a level 9 invasion from the nine hells happening on Al. These Modrons make their way to the office of Unit 100000000000000, their overseeing warforge unit. The warforge then reads the distress, and makes its way over to the stasis chamber of 011110100110100101101100, otherwise known as Zilton Zalzam. The warforge deployed Zilton. We cut back to the party making proper introductions with each other, and deciding where to go. Na'Hal had the info that there was a dangerous spot south of the city of the damned. He thought it would have loot, but told the party it had information about the invasion. They decided to go. Arriving at the spot, the party found a cove appearing to be an old camping spot that has turned into somethings nest. 
(Unknown to the party, this location is the camping spot that The Hellbringers camped in during season 1. Since the portal has opened, Ig created this cave system and nested here for several years collecting information about his missing friends (the hellbringers) before being found by Queen Layla and moving back to his hometown Forest Guard Village within the new borders of New Freia.)

Eventually, the party reached the main nest within the cave, which contained a central sleeping spot of a large bed a grass (Where Ig would sleep). Upon seeing grass for the first time in over 6000 years, Fel rans to it. In running towards the grass, Fel was surprised and trampled by a Gem Stalker. The party fought this Gem Stalker, and during the fight Fel falls into unconsciousness, and displays his ability to have his soul leave his body and possess another. He possesses the fallen metallic peacekeeper, and continues to fight. However, the party then made a successful animal handling check, and deduced that the Gem Stalker was just acting in order to protect his master's nest. The fight ended, Fel returned to his original body, and the party befriended the Gem Stalker and took a long rest. The party asked the Gem Stalker it's name, and it said it was not given a name (However, it did have a name, it just did not remember it. It's name is Gemstone). This Gem Stalker was prim, proper, and shy, but acted like a big dog. Essentially, it was Ig's pet when he lived here in the nest. Exploring the rest of the nest, the party found:

Following the rest, the party said their goodbyes to the Gem Stalker and began returning North back to the City of the Damned. When returning to the City of the Damned, the party ventured to investigate the destroyed Manor sitting at the top of the hill (Lord Gallium's Manor). While inside, Na'Hal partially fell through the floor, revealing the illusion that he was actually two imps stacked and forming an illusion of a tiefling. They introduced themselves to the party as Nampha and Andhal, and apologized for lying. Interrupting the party's mixed feelings about the two lying imps, the spire that was next to the portal miles away released a burst of energy that even the party felt. Going outside, they saw the spire off in the distance revealing a bright glowing light, then subsiding. The party began traveling to investigate. Before reaching the portal grounds, Na'Hal halted about a mile out, with worries of being captured and found out. Fel and Zilton investigated the gate, and while they were there, a colossal hellfire mech (The Engine of Nessus) began exiting the portal, heading south. The two hid, and were not found, but were in complete shock of the powerful machine leaving the nine hells. After regrouping with Na'Hal, the party decided to head further north towards Fort Lakegap, a fortress they spotted while looking at a map of the area. Arriving at the fortress, they were greeted by the forces of New Freia, specifically Queen Layla, Ig the emerald dragon, and various knights that were investigating the fort. Queen Layla called out, asking if they were survivors, to which the party answered yes.

### Session 4 + 5 + 6
We started session 4 with a our first proper gleam into Fel's mindscape. Fel wakes in a cold shiver inside the dungeon that he died in. However, contrarily to how he spent his life, this time he is on the outside of his cell. He is in the long hallway that is his cell block, and is quite a long ways away from his cell. As he gets closer to his cell door, he'll begin to hear a faint voice. Even as he gets up next to the door, the voice is still heavily muffled. It slowly gets louder and louder, a scream of "CAN YOU HEAR ME?" (coming from Aji). As he begins to hear the voice more clearly, he hears a "Hey, can you hear me?" from Layla, and the party's scene is set in front of the fort that the last session ended on. Layla greets the party and informs that they do not find much survivors out here, especially so close to the portal. She then informs the captain of her nights, Sabrina Liaro, to use an Amulet of Good and Evil to locate any fiends nearby, testing the party's identity. All 3 party members will appear as neutral to the amulet, as Na'Hal realizes that his sword has shielded him from the spell. Once they succeeded in the test, the party asked if they could join the queen's men in exploring the fort, to which the queen assigned the captain to accompany them, ensuring they would stay out of trouble. Upon entering the fort, they found a room with a collapsed hole in the ceiling, to which a Barlgura emerged, fighting the party and the captain. The party impressively defeats the Barlgura, causing Sabrina to report to the queen their skill and granting the party permission to explore without her. Zilton and Na'Hal began exploring the upper layer of the fort, spotting the Acid lake that lays just beyond it. Fel joined the queen and began discussing Ig the dragon. She mentions to Fel that her and Ig lost an old friend, a plasmoid named Malm, which is the reason the knights still explore this close to the portal. Fel mentions the pages that he found by the gate and in the cave (Ig's lair). As Zilton and Na'Hal continued to explore the fort, they found an office belonging to the Captain who ruled over the fort before the invasion, a Leo Soria. Inside they found the following journal entries:
- 4/13/07: I fear Lootbridge is a lost cause. Out of the 18 men I have sent, only 2 have returned, and they give stories of a truly disturbing sight. Abominations and flying ships wrecking the city, I can only pray that the destruction ends soon.
- 4/20/07: A massive earthquake has occurred. My men are stable, and the fort had little to no damage, but my men are in fear. It has given me fear too. An earthquake that left little impact on our day to day should not have this much of an effect on us.  After the attacks on Lootbridge, I fear there is something more sinister at foot.
- 4/22/07: A scout has confirmed the worst: the hells have been unleashed on Al and our fort is at the foot of the terror. My men have been readied for a complete lockdown. Our rations are on critical control going forward, as we not know the next time we will be delivered food.
- 6/13/07: The prisoners have been getting more outraged since we have stopped giving them rations. As much as we don't want to starve them, what choice do we have.
- 6/20/07: The dungeon has been locked. A prisoner has murdered one of my men and all of the other prisoners. After surviving this long from the facing hells, this will not be how my fort falls.
- Holy Marduk has been slaughtered. (This was implied to be the loss of faith and hope from the captain, as he had witnessed violent and abhorrent things from both the hell invasion and from the prisoners downstairs. AKA: the proclamation that "God is dead")
	While in the office, Na'Hal also studied the regional map pinned to the wall, and committed all of it's details to memory. Suddenly, the entire party was alerted to a knights shout, and upon arrival, the knight claimed to find something behind a barrel. However, Zilton discovered an oozy strand connecting to the man, revealing that this knight was created by an Oblex. The party engaged and fought the Oblex with the rest of the knights, and swiftly defeated it, only to the loss of the knights that discovered it. Once defeating the Oblex, the queen thanked the party for their aid and the party left her to acknowledge the weight of her losses. The party looted the armory in the fort, and began heading downstairs with Captain Sabrina. Arriving in the dungeon of the fort, the party noticed the various bloody scenes that awaited by the prison cells, and Na'Hal discovered a hidden compartment in a chest that had a key to the solitary confinement. The party then broke down a door leading to the war room, revealing a Babau demon that ambushed them. The party fought this demon and defeated it. Once they defeated it and analyzed the war room, they fonud a large map of the surrounding region marked with a black X over Lootbridge city and red X's over locations Fortitude (The capital of the Bruibary Monarchy), Palm Cross (A town), and various others upwards the peninsula of the Bruibary Monarchy (The country in which Fort Lakegap, Fortitude, Lootbridge City, and Palmcross reside in), with a red star over Safekeeps, where the gate to hell is located.
