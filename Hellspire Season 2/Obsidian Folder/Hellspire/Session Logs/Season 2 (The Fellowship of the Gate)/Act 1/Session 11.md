## Saga20:
The party traveled through the snow-laden wilderness north of Gemstone City and made strange discoveries while facing internal conflict and a brutal confrontation with infernal forces. After securing a mysterious potion from the eccentric shopkeeper [[Frink]], the group followed the mechanical hound Dot on the trail of a unicorn and encountered a celestial white stag that offered its aid. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s sentient greatsword seized control and impaled the creature, which created distrust within the party. The night brought uneasy rest and a tense confrontation between [[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] over the sword's influence. The next day, a brief stop at Forest Guard Village allowed Captain [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]] to join the expedition, and the trail led into a cave system that revealed an infernal workshop hidden within the mountain. There, an accidental activation of an ancient alarm summoned lava elementals, resulting in a desperate battle that claimed the life of [[Skip Leafing]] before [[Solace Forger]]'s divine magic and [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]]'s radiant smite turned the tide. With [[Skip Leafing|Skip]] revived, the party stood in the workshop.

### Key Events

1. **A Potion and a Complaint**

The morning began in Gemstone City at the shop Potions Oh My, where the party discovered that [[Frink]] had not received their earlier order. Despite low stock, the eccentric potion maker recognized the gravity of their mission and, after a moment of consideration, produced a single, mysterious vial: a metallic, mercury-like liquid that smelled faintly of beer. [[Frink]] warned that the last time he sold such a concoction, the Gate to Hell had opened a week later. The gift was accepted with a mixture of gratitude and unease. Outside the shop, [[Zilton Zalzalm]] noticed workers hauling tarp-covered banana tree saplings into the city, an idea he had proposed to the council but for which he had received no credit. After lodging a formal complaint, the group moved on to a general store to purchase heavy winter gear, waterskins for collecting blood, and a pocket watch before departing the city.

2. **The Trail and the Vanishing Book**

Traveling north at a slow pace through deep snow, the party followed Dot's tracking of the unicorn's scent. As they walked, each member studied the lore they had gathered. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] opened the enigmatic tome _Outside the Grain of Salt_ and was presented with a final riddle: "What is my name?" He guessed "the Watcher," the deity he had learned of in the Church of the Watcher. The book shuddered, then warped in on itself and vanished from his hands, leaving behind only confusion. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] read _Anatomy of Celestial Creatures_, learning that white stags were celestial beings, not ordinary animals. Meanwhile, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] struggled with a book that demanded he speak in a native tongue; with [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s help, he uttered the Celestial word for "stag," and the ink on the pages swirled into a living painting that showed the party's own figures moving through the forest.

3. **The White Stag and the Sword's Betrayal**

The painting's vision became reality as a white stag bolted across their path. The party gave chase into a snowy clearing where the creature stood on a bare patch of earth and spoke, declaring itself an ally that shared their goal of closing the Gate to Hell. It offered its blood willingly and even directed [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] to the trail. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]], sensing the tension, turned to [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] and warned him that if he wished to prove he was different from other fiends, he should not attack the unicorn when they found it. Before the words had fully settled, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s greatsword moved of its own accord, plunging through the stag's heart with surgical precision. The celestial creature collapsed, dead. Shock and fury erupted among the party. [[Scarlet]] and [[Skip Leafing]] performed a solemn funeral while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] played a mournful tune, and the group struggled to reconcile the act with their mission. The stag's blood was collected, and Dot was given the scent of the unicorn once more.

4. **Camp in the Snowstorm**

As night fell and the temperature plummeted, the party decided to make camp rather than press on to Forest Guard Village in the dark. [[Skip Leafing|Skip]] set up a large circular tent and attempted to start a fire, but the frozen wood and heavy snow made it impossible. [[Solace Forger]] managed to keep herself warm through sheer Fortitude, but both [[Skip Leafing|Skip]] and [[Scarlet]] suffered from the cold, gaining a level of exhaustion. Inside the tent, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] used his song of rest to soothe the group, while [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] examined his amulet but found no new insights in the bitter chill. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]], needing no sleep, stood watch outside, his thoughts troubled by the day's violence.

5. **A Midnight Confrontation**

After the others had fallen asleep, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] crept back into the tent and approached the sleeping [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. His goal was to examine the greatsword that had acted so autonomously. With a successful arcana check, he sensed that the sword was a source of much of [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s power and that it was aware of his presence. As he reached to draw the blade, a voice thundered in his mind: "Wake up." [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] found himself paralyzed, held by a hold person spell emanating from the sword. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] jolted awake, saw the frozen warforged, and without a word walked out into the snowy woods. When the paralysis faded, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] followed his footprints and found [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] standing alone. A tense conversation followed. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] confessed that he had been denied a promotion in the Hells and now sought revenge against the devils who had wronged him. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] pressed him about the sword's role, and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] admitted he did not fully understand why it helped him. The warforged made it clear that closing the Gate was his only priority, and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], after a long silence, conveyed his commitment to the same goal. They returned to camp, and [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] spent the rest of the night watching the quiet snowfall.

6. **Reinforcements at Forest Guard**

The next morning, the party broke camp and followed the trail northeast. It led them close to Forest Guard Village, and they decided to make a brief stop. The village was now blanketed in snow, and the guards at the checkpoint waved them through to the guard house, where Queen [[Layla Tenama]] and Captain [[Sabrina Liaro]] were waiting. The queen reported that [[Ig]] the emerald dragon was away scouting the colossal construct known as the **Engine of Nessus**, which had been spotted moving into the ocean. [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]], fully equipped and eager, officially joined the party. The queen also mentioned that the Gemstone City council had praised [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s banana tree proposal, though [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] remained bitter about the lack of credit. With [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]] in tow and the unicorn's trail still fresh, the group departed once more, heading north toward the mountains.

7. **Into the Mountain Cave**

The trail led the party to a cliff face where a narrow path wound into a cave. Inside, the snow gave way to a stone floor, clearly crafted by intelligent hands. The group advanced stealthily, with Dot's tail fibers stiffening into a metallic ridge as he entered a combat-ready state. They discovered a portcullis that [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] simply lifted off its damaged hinges, revealing a larger chamber beyond. The air grew uncomfortably hot, and the party shed their winter gear as they entered what appeared to be an infernal workshop. Desks cluttered with papers, a massive mechanical structure, and a pool of bubbling lava dominated the space. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] recognized the machine as a prototype hellfire engine, while a dissected spider on a table revealed traces of soul energy, teaching him that souls consisted of both a pointer and a memory address. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] found a cauldron swirling with purple energy that smelled of roasted meat; when he dipped his finger in, the entire contents surged into his body, granting him a temporary boost to his attacks. In a side room, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] discovered piles of soul coins, fifty-seven in total, though some were defunct. He pocketed them all.

8. **The Alarm and the Lava Elementals**

Curiosity got the better of [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], who inserted a broken soul coin into the hellfire engine's deposit slot. The machine ground to life briefly, and a loud clang echoed through the chamber. The lava pool began to churn violently, and from its depths rose armored figures of molten rock and fire: lava elementals. Two were hulking warriors wielding greatswords, while two others were draconic spellcasters wreathed in flame. The party scrambled into position as the elementals attacked.

9. **A Desperate Battle**

The fight was brutal from the start. [[Skip Leafing]], ever eager, was the first to fall, cut nearly in half by a lava warrior's blade. [[Scarlet]] fired arrows from the shadows, while [[Solace Forger|Solace]] called forth spirit guardians and a spiritual weapon, their radiant energy searing the elementals. [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]] stood firm, her shield blocking blow after blow, though she and many others were soon ignited by the creatures' touch. A wall of fire erupted across the chamber, splitting the party and forcing them to endure searing heat. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] rallied the group with a motivational speech and mass cure wounds, while [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] darted across the lava, using his crossbow and surugi to harry the enemy. Dot bit and tore at the elementals, his metallic body enduring immense punishment. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] unleashed eldritch blasts and a devastating psychic lance, cracking the helmet of one spellcaster. The tide turned when [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]], channeling divine power, drove her longsword through a warrior's back and released a radiant smite that exploded the creature from within. [[Solace Forger|Solace]], with a final swing of her warhammer, extinguished the last armored foe, and the chamber fell silent save for the bubbling lava.

10. **Revival and Aftermath**

As the party caught their breath and doused the flames clinging to their clothes, [[Solace Forger|Solace]] rushed to [[Skip Leafing|Skip]]'s broken body. She produced a diamond from her pouch, pressed it to his chest, and cast revivify. The gem shattered, and [[Skip Leafing|Skip]]'s wounds knitted together as he gasped back to life, bewildered but whole. The infernal workshop was littered with the remains of the elementals and the scattered soul coins. The party, exhausted and wounded, stood in the sweltering heat, knowing that the unicorn's trail led deeper into the mountain and that greater dangers surely awaited.
