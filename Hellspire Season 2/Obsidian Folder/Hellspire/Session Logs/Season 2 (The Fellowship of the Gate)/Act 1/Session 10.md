## Saga20:
The party awoke in Gemstone City to the aftermath of a night fraught with supernatural chaos. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], having been violently ejected from his own body during a psychic struggle, spent the dark hours as a disembodied spirit, desperately seeking a vessel to survive until dawn. His frantic search led him to possess a hapless innkeeper, then to a failed attempt to inhabit [[Skip Leafing]] and the mechanical hound Dot, before finally finding refuge within [[Nampha]], the upper imp of [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]. The morning brought a fragile peace, but the tension lingered as [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] was confronted by his sentient sword for trading a soul coin to a mysterious [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]]. With new recruits in tow and supplies gathered, the group departed the city into a sudden, unnatural snowstorm, following a lead on a unicorn. Their journey culminated in a haunting encounter with a wounded celestial stag, an entity that offered its blood freely but warned [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] against his infernal nature, a warning that was ignored when his sword compelled him to strike the creature down.

### Key Events

1. **The Spirit's Desperate Flight**

[[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] found himself trapped in a crumbling mental dungeon, a reflection of the body he had stolen from [[Aji Skipplestump]]. As he investigated the decaying hallway, the floor gave way, sending him plummeting through a void and out of his physical form entirely. He emerged as a ghostly, incorporeal spirit in the Home Away Inn, with only three turns to find a new body or face oblivion. His spectral form drifted through the halls, and with time running out, he forced his way into the mouth of the sleepy innkeeper, possessing the man to stabilize his own fading essence.

2. **A Frantic Search for Sanctuary**

Inside the innkeeper's body, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] struggled with unfamiliar motor control, clumsily making his way upstairs to seek help. He knocked on [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s door, and after a tense exchange to prove his identity, was let inside. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] grappled with the bizarre situation, even pondering the metaphysical implications of making a deal with a soul inhabiting another's body. They decided to try placing [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s spirit into the construct Dot, but the dog's immutable form repelled him. A desperate plan was hatched to use [[Skip Leafing]] as a vessel, but [[Skip Leafing|Skip]]'s surprisingly strong constitution resisted the possession.

3. **A Temporary Home in an Imp's Flesh**

With options dwindling, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] made a difficult choice, offering up the body of [[Nampha]], his own upper imp half. He struck the innkeeper to jettison [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s spirit, and after a tense moment, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] successfully entered [[Nampha]]. The sensation was bizarre, a smaller body with a tail, but [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] managed to control it with surprising grace. The two then retreated to [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s room, hiding under the covers together to maintain their secret, while [[Zilton Zalzalm]] was tasked with disposing of the unconscious innkeeper's body, staging it at the bottom of the stairs to look like a simple fall.

4. **A Meeting of Minds in a Cave of Chains**

As [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] slept within [[Nampha]], his consciousness was pulled back into the mindscape, this time appearing in a cave with a campfire. There, he finally met [[Aji Skipplestump]], the gnome whose body he inhabited, chained to the cave wall. The conversation was surprisingly civil, a stark contrast to their previous violent encounter. [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]] expressed his frustration at being trapped, while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] apologized and explained his predicament. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], observing the scene via their shared link, used a mage hand to offer a s'more, a small gesture of peace. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] realized his very presence had reshaped [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]]'s mindscape, building a dungeon over the gnome's original mental home, an unsettling revelation.

5. **The Sword's Cold Rebuke**

The fragile peace was shattered when [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] was violently expelled from [[Nampha]]'s body by a psychic lance from [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s sentient sword. He was flung back into his own body, waking with a hangover-like headache and fragmented memories. Alone in the room, the sword addressed [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] directly, its voice cold and ancient. It did not chastise him for the possession, but for the loss of the soul coin, a transaction the blade had not witnessed and could not remember. The sword expressed genuine worry, revealing that the [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]]'s power was significant enough to cloud even its perception, and it warned [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] to be far more careful in the future.

6. **New Allies and Old Tensions**

The morning brought an unexpected development as the party descended to the inn's lobby. [[Skip Leafing]], eager to prove his worth, had recruited two potential members for the garrison: [[Percy]], the enthusiastic desk clerk from the keep, and [[Scarlet]], a sharp-eyed tiefling woman who had tracked the party down after hearing rumors of their mission. An impromptu job interview ensued, with [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] grilling the candidates. [[Percy]] was earnest but non-combatant, while [[Scarlet]] proved confident and capable, claiming skills in stealth and tracking. Her flirtatious banter with [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] created a moment of levity, though [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] watched the exchange with quiet, robotic unease.

7. **Potions and Peculiar Gifts**

The party visited [[Frink]] at Potions Oh My, only to realize they had never actually given him the written list of requested potions. A moment of collective embarrassment was salvaged by a stroke of incredible luck, as [[Frink]] revealed he had, against all odds, brewed exactly what they needed. Moved by the sight of a proper adventuring party, [[Frink]] unscrewed his peg leg to retrieve a hidden key, unlocking a glass case to present them with a mysterious, square potion of swirling metallic liquid that smelled faintly of beer. He admitted the last time he sold such a concoction, the Gate to Hell opened a week later, but he wished them better fortune with this one.

8. **The Chill of Change and a Councilman's Ire**

Stepping out of the potion shop, the party noticed a sharp drop in temperature and a caravan transporting banana trees into the city. The sight ignited a cold fury in [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]], who had proposed this very idea to the city council only to be dismissed. He marched to the keep and confronted [[Percy]] at the front desk, his voice tight with restrained anger as he dictated a message for the council, a terse reminder of whose idea the banana trees had been. The encounter highlighted [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s growing frustration with the city's bureaucracy and his own unheeded wisdom.

9. **Provisions for the Road**

Before departing, the group returned to [[Samantha]]'s general store to purchase waterskins for collecting the stag's blood, a crucial detail they had almost forgotten. They also acquired winter clothing to combat the sudden cold. When [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] finally remembered to present the Queen's seal, [[Samantha]]'s demeanor shifted entirely, and she offered the gear for free, along with a magical calendar. The party left the store equipped for the harsh weather, their resources bolstered by their official status.

10. **Into the White Wilderness**

The party exited the mountain city through a snow-choked tunnel, emerging into a transformed landscape of white powder and skeletal trees. As they traveled, they divided their attention between tracking and research. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] donned the **Helm of Comprehending Languages** to read _Creatures of the Outer Planes_, while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] opened _Beyond the Grain of Salt_, a book that posed a single, reality-warping riddle: "What is my name?" When [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] answered "The Watcher," the book collapsed in on itself and vanished.* [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] studied the _Anatomy of Celestial Creatures_, finding detailed diagrams of a stag, its organs, and its migration patterns, knowledge that proved useful.

*Huge DM Secret right here: From this point on, the players are unaware that, until further notice, everything is a vision that [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] is having of the future. This will last several sessions, and will be a big reveal at a later point.

11. **A Celestial's Plea and a Blade's Betrayal**

[[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] studied the stag's image in _Anatomy of Celestial Creatures_. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] read _Creatures of the Outer Planes,_ to which it prompted him to say a creature. Once he said a Stag, the ink of the page began to move, showing the creature running through a forest. The party looked up to see the very same stag, a magnificent but grievously wounded beast with a broken antler and claw marks, standing on a snowy hill. It spoke into their minds, a gentle and knowing voice that acknowledged their quest for its blood and offered it freely. The stag warned [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] that if he was truly different from other imps, he would not attack the unicorn. At that moment, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s sword acted of its own accord, forcing his hand to summon a greatsword and drive it through the stag's heart. The celestial creature fell dead, and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] could only stagger back, horrified and unable to explain his actions to his stunned companions.

12. **Mourning and Moving Forward**

In the somber aftermath, [[Solace Forger]] performed purification rites over the stag's body while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] played a mournful tune on his instrument. [[Skip Leafing|Skip]] wept openly, and [[Scarlet]] stood in respectful silence. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] pulled [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] aside, his voice a low, serious hum as he questioned the nature of the sword that could override its wielder's will. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], bound by his pact, could only offer cryptic assurances that his goals remained aligned with the party's, even if the blade's did not. With the blood collected and a heavy heart, the group turned their attention to the fresh unicorn tracks Dot had discovered in the snow, and they pressed deeper into the frozen forest.
