## Saga20:
The shadows of Fort Lake Gap deepened as the party descended into the forgotten dungeon beneath the crumbling fortress. [[Sabrina Liaro]], the stoic knight assigned by **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]], followed the group with a mixture of caution and curiosity, her earlier question about origins hanging in the musty air. The basement beyond the heavy door was a realm of absolute darkness, where even the faint light seeping through the upper floors could not reach. [[Zilton Zalzalm]], the warforged monk from the plane of Mechanus, found himself reliant on his companions' torches, his own vision useless in the blackness. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], the bardic soul trapped in a dwarf's body, rummaged through his belongings and produced a flame, handing a torch to [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] while [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], the tiefling warlock who was secretly a pair of imps, summoned a spectral mage hand to carry an additional light ahead. Thus arrayed, they pressed into the oppressive gloom, the floor beneath them slick with ancient grime and the distant echo of dripping water.

### Key Events

1. **The Descent into Darkness**

The dungeon's arrival hall was a scene of neglect. A splintered desk lurked in one corner beside a broken chair that still bore the traces of dried blood, human and old. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s investigation confirmed that the room had not seen use in a long age, though the crimson stain hinted at a violent past. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] busied himself with a supply closet, discovering rusted short swords and empty barrels, but his eye caught a hidden compartment in a chest that yielded a solitary key. Meanwhile, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] examined the blood more closely, finding it so desiccated and fused to the stone that it was impossible to clean, a permanent scar on the floor. The hallway beyond opened to a row of prison cells, most sealed tight, their portcullises weathered but intact. One cell door stood ajar, and from it, a dark trail of blood led deeper into the dungeon, as if something had crawled out long ago.

2. **The Rusted Door and the Ceiling Horror**

Drawn by the bloodstains, the party gathered before a heavy metal door that was rusted shut. Efforts to pry it open with brute force proved futile until [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], with a powerful swing of his maul, burst through. In that instant, a grotesque creature that had been clinging to the ceiling above the doorway lunged downward, its claws raking into the warlock and grappling him in a surprise attack. The thing (a Babau demon) was pale and gaunt, like an alien horror, and it held [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] fast while the others scrambled to react. [[Sabrina Liaro|Sabrina]] moved swiftly, casting a protective ward over [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] before stepping in to free [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] with her longsword, though the creature's counterattack left a deep gash across her back. The battle raged in the narrow corridor, and despite a series of unfortunate mishaps—[[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] accidentally braining himself with his own maul and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] momentarily losing his pact weapon—the tide turned when [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s precise surugi strikes and flurry of blows drew the monster's blood. With a final series of slashes and a deep thrust through the abdomen, the creature collapsed, its shriek dying in its throat.

3. **The War Room and the Queen's Arrival**

Beyond the fallen beast, the party entered a chamber that had once served as a command post. A large map of the surrounding region stretched across a table, marked with black and red notations that spoke of a long-ago conflict. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] recognized the Evergreen Kingdom, his former homeland, and felt a pang of distant memory. The map displayed arrows from the City of the Damned toward Loot Bridge City, and red X's over fallen settlements like Fortitude and Palm Cross, while a star marked the location of the Gate to Hell. As they studied these notations, the sound of armored footsteps echoed from behind. **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] appeared at the chamber's entrance, her knights at her back, their swords drawn but not raised. Her gaze swept over the three strangers, and her question was direct: who were they, and from where had they come?

4. **The Interrogation and Revelations**

The queen's inquiry was sharp, her attention lingering on [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s pristine mechanical form and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s unsettling aura. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] declared his origin from Mechanus and his purpose to close the Gate to Hell, a statement that drew a raised brow. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], his voice heavy with sorrow, confessed that he had escaped from Hell itself, a lost soul trapped in a body not his own. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], ever guarded, claimed to be a wanderer without a homeland, a half-truth that the queen seemed to accept with a note of sympathy. When [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] revealed the existence of the The Engine of Nessus near the cave where they had found journal pages, the queen's composure cracked. Those pages were writings of [[Malm]], a plasmoid and an old friend of the dragon [[Ig]], who had been missing for years. The queen shared the dark history: the gate had been opened by a warlock named [[Gabble Skipplestump]], using a goblet of fire and a wand from a magical vault, while she, [[Malm]], and a man named [[Deyrale Dinglebottom]] had tried to stop him. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s maul, she noted with a start, had once belonged to [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Daryl]]—a revelation that sent a shiver through the bard, connecting the voice he had heard in his visions to that lost warrior.

5. **The Journal's Secrets and the Solitary Cells**

With the queen's permission to rest, the party turned back to the dungeon's remaining mysteries. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]], his eidetic memory absorbing every detail, examined a recruitment journal found in the war room. Its entries painted a timeline: a massive earthquake, a week of hellish incursions, and a prisoner uprising that culminated in the murder of a guard and the deaths of all remaining captives. The final entry, dated June 24th, spoke of the dungeon being locked forever. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s calculations led him to a conclusion: the prisoner who had caused so much death was likely still somewhere in that dungeon. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] then used the key to open the cell doors one by one, revealing only skeletal remains and rusted beds. One solitary chamber, however, held a meticulously arranged skeleton on a bed and a man-made tunnel that had been clawed through the stone, leading away from the fort.

6. **The Abyssal Sigil and the Moss Demon**

Crawling through the narrow tunnel, the party emerged into a chamber dominated by a sigil of dried blood arranged in a pentagram pattern, with green fungus growing along its lines. The air hummed with residual energy, and at the center, a shimmering portal to the Abyss pulsed faintly. As they stepped closer, the fungus stirred, coalescing into a shambling mass of moss and chitinous limbs studded with humanoid eyeballs. The demon creature, a horror of the Abyss, lashed out with tentacles, nearly crushing [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]. The warforged monk responded with a whirlwind of blade and fist, but the monster's resilience was unnerving. Then [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] moved into position, his blade sheening with black, otherworldly power as he channeled a banishing smite. With a single, devastating strike that struck the creature's core, he sent it hurtling back through the portal in a cascade of shimmering particles, sealing the rift for a few precious moments.

7. **The Dragon's Intervention and a Lingering Threat**

As the party caught their breath, the ceiling above them cracked and crumbled, and the massive emerald dragon [[Ig]] descended, his horns vibrating with arcane energy. The great wyrm's presence was palpable, his mind reaching out to touch theirs. He spoke not with words but with a resonant understanding that bypassed language. With a sweep of his claw and a low, rumbling incantation, [[Ig]] collapsed the remaining portal, but the moment was short. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s arcane senses and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s investigation revealed a truth: the sigil on the floor remained active, its hellish origin still thrumming with a malevolent life of its own. The blood that formed it was ancient and irreversibly fused to the stone, and the pentagram seemed attuned to a powerful archdevil. The battle was not over—the portal had been closed, but the doorway itself was only temporarily stilled.

8. **A Fleeting Respite and a Whispered Counsel**

The group retreated to the fort's second floor for a short rest, the knights and the queen settling in while [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] kept watch over the sigil. The warforged's sensors never left the dark hole, his thoughts processing the day's revelations. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] sank into a meditative state near the queen, the weight of his fractured identity pressing upon him. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] slipped away to a secluded corner, his pact weapon in hand. In the privacy of the shadows, he sought counsel from his patron, the sentience within the blade, questioning whether he should reveal his true fiendish nature to the queen. The sword's answer was a warning wrapped in a threat: to keep the secret at all costs, for now. The warlock returned, his expression unreadable, his course set. They healed their wounds and replenished their strength, but even the brief rest was shadowed by the knowledge that the dungeon's horror was not yet ended.

9. **The Stomach Devil Rises**

As the hour of rest drew to a close, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s keen auditory sensors caught a new sound—a thick, viscous dripping that was not water but the faint sizzle of acid. Peering through a gap in the broken wall, he saw the acid lake outside the fort stir. From its depths, a nightmare was emerging. A creature of bloated, folded flesh and glistening skin climbed slowly onto the shore, its form a twisted mockery of life. The stomach devil, an abomination of the Abyss with a ravenous maw and countless writhing limbs, fixed its gaze upon the fortress. The sight was enough to freeze the oil in [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]]'s joints, and he calmly alerted his companions. The session ended with the party staring down at the horror, initiative soon to be rolled.
