## Saga20:
The party's search for the means to close the Gate to Hell led them deeper into the intrigues of Gemstone City, where political tensions, strange bargains, and personal demons threatened to overshadow their mission. [[Zilton Zalzalm]], the warforged monk, nearly shattered the group's standing with the city council through a brash outburst, while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], the bard trapped in a gnome's body, endured a harrowing psychic confrontation with the body's original owner. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], the dual-imp warlock, was forced into a clandestine deal with the enigmatic [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]], trading a soul coin for a tuft of unicorn hair that may prove vital. By the day's end, the party had secured precious resources and knowledge, but the shadows of their own secrets grew longer, and the road ahead remained fraught with peril.

### Key Events

1. **The Council Chamber**

The party approached the keep of Gemstone City with the intention of recruiting allies, but the visit quickly soured. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] strode into a meeting of the city council unannounced and demanded their assistance, his tone sharp and his manner abrasive. When a gnome councilwoman questioned his authority, he retorted with an insult that stunned the chamber. Guards were summoned, and the situation teetered on disaster until [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] produced the Queen's token and used every ounce of his charm to apologize for his companion's behavior. The council, though offended, agreed to let the matter rest, but the political damage was done, and the party left without the recruits they had hoped for. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], who had waited outside, sensed the tension and later learned of the near-disaster, adding to his frustration with the day's events.

2. **The Armorer's Bargain**

At **Smelter's Heaven**, a weapon shop, the party browsed enchanted items displayed behind glass. The proprietor, a tall wood elf named [[Lulin]], showed them a dagger that inflicted lingering sickness and a mace that sowed fear. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], ever the performer, attempted to seduce the armorer into lowering the price of the dagger. His flirtations were so effective that [[Lulin]] closed the shop and led him to a back room, but the encounter took a dark turn when [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] suddenly collapsed, unconscious before anything could happen. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], who had been waiting outside, remained unaware of the specifics, but when [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] awoke, he was shaken and purchased the dagger at a reduced price, leaving the shop in a daze.

3. **The Dungeon of the Mind**

As [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] lost consciousness in the armory, his mind was pulled into a vivid vision. He found himself back in the cold stone cell where his body's original owner, [[Aji Skipplestump]], was imprisoned. The furious gnome burst through the cell door and attacked him with relentless punches, screaming accusations of theft. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], stripped of his weapons and magic, tried to reason with the man, but the blows continued. Desperate, he cast a sleeping spell upon himself, forcing his own mind into slumber to escape the beating. The vision faded, and he awoke on the floor of the armory with [[Lulin]] standing over him in concern, the dagger now his only prize from the strange ordeal.

4. **The General Store's Curiosities**

While [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] was otherwise occupied, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] visited a general store run by a human woman named [[Samantha]]. The shop offered luxurious goods, from crystal necklaces to pocket watches, and a magical calendar that could display any date in history. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] was fascinated by the calendar but could not afford its price, even when he offered a shard of emerald. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] remained quiet, his thoughts clearly elsewhere. The two left empty-handed, but the brief respite was a welcome contrast to the tensions of the keep.

5. **The** Amethyst Library

The group reconvened and made their way to the grand library, a towering structure of crystal and stone. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], fearing a magical sensor that might reveal his fiendish nature, elected to wait outside while [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] entered. Inside, they met the goblin librarian [[Ariel Grimwill]], who led them to the restricted section after they invoked the Queen's authority. There, [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] discovered a tome titled _Creatures of the Outer Planes_, written in Celestial, while [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] found a book called _Outside of the Grain of Salt_, authored by someone called the [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]]. They also uncovered _Anatomy of Celestial Creatures_, a volume on the apocalypse, and a scroll of speak with animals. [[Ariel Grimwill|Ariel]] agreed to hold the books until council approval could be secured, leaving the party hopeful but anxious.

6. **The** Fisted Dwarf **Tavern**

[[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] ventured alone into the rowdy Fisted Dwarf, a tavern where dwarves wrestled shirtless and patrons were handed tankards of ale by a manic gnome. He was handed a flyer for a fortune teller named [[Tip Trinket]] by a halfling who seemed more promoter than prophet. Later, when [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] arrived, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] joined the band on fiddle and quickly became intoxicated, a single shot leaving him hammered. The chaotic energy of the tavern offered a brief escape, but it also drew the party toward a fateful encounter.

7. **The Charlatan's Tent**

Following the flyer, the party entered a tent where the supposed wizard [[Tip Trinket]] held court. The halfling wore a fake beard and a starry robe, and his magic tricks were obvious illusions. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] paid a silver for a reading, and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] received one as well, the predictions vague and theatrical. When [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] challenged the charlatan, the man fled into the night. In the sudden silence, a familiar voice echoed from the shadows: the [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]] had returned, seated at the table as if he had always been there.

8. **The Blind Man's Bargain**

The [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]], who had accosted the party earlier in the day, now offered a trade. He produced a tuft of radiant white unicorn hair, the very thing the party sought, but demanded a soul coin from [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] in return. The warlock was hostile, distrustful of the figure who knew too much, but after a tense negotiation, he secretly handed over the coin while [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] were distracted. The [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]] crushed the coin in his hand, the energy within dissipating rather than flowing into him, and then he vanished, leaving the unicorn hair behind. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] concealed the transaction from his companions, adding a new layer of secrecy to the group's dynamic.

9. **The Library's Return**

The party hurried back to the library to retrieve the books that had been approved by the council. [[Ariel Grimwill|Ariel]] met them on the steps, distressed that one of the restricted books was missing. She handed over the remaining volumes, including the Celestial tome and the book by the [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]], and promised to search for the lost one. The party now had the means to research unicorns, but the missing book hinted at darker forces at play within the city.

10. **The Sending Stone**

At the Home Away Inn, the party secured rooms and gathered to send their nightly report to **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]]. They composed a brief message: "Today good. Found tuft of unicorn hair. Dot is on the trail. No stag yet. One new recruit: Solice. Thank you so much for everything". The reply came swiftly: "Remarkable. Sent [[Ig]] out scouting the big construct. Earthquake from two days ago has me busy. Keep up the good work. You're welcome." The words were encouraging, but the mention of the construct reminded them of the larger threats looming beyond the city.

11. **Fel's Recurring Nightmare**

As [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] fell asleep, he was dragged once more into the dungeon of his mind. The cell door stood pristine, the food tray burst open, and [[Aji Skipplestump]] faced him with bitter recognition. "Back again I see," the gnome said, and the vision faded to black. The session ended with the unresolved conflict between [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] and the original owner of his body, a psychological wound that refused to heal.
