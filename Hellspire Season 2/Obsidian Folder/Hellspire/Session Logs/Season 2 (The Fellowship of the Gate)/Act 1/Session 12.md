## Saga20:
The chamber had barely recovered from the spider swarm when Zilton’s investigation triggered an even greater threat. From a pool of molten rock, a dragon fused with a hellfire engine rose and filled the hall with searing lava, sending Dot sinking into the floor. Desperate tactics and Skip Leafing's hidden reservoir of water turned the battle, culminating in Na'Hal banishing the engine-warped beast. But the victory was short-lived as Na'Hal's own spectral servant turned against him, forcing the party to battle errant spirits amid steam and mud. When the last wraith dissolved and the lava hardened, a wall collapsed to reveal a distant glimmer, promising a path toward their goal.

### Key Events

1. **Awakening the Engine-Bound Dragon**

While the group gathered their strength after the spider attack, Zilton Zalzalm probed deeper into the ruined chamber. A disturbance rippled through the air, and the far wall crumbled as a massive shape emerged from a pool of lava. The dragon’s black scales were marred by crimson veins that radiated from the infernal engine bolted into its chest, and with a roar it flooded the floor with fresh molten stone. Dot, the mechanical hound, was caught in the deluge and clattered motionless into the lava, his metal frame beginning to sink. Panic spread through the party as the heat warped the air and the beast snaked toward them like an enraged crocodile.

2. **Frantic Rescue and Urgent Discovery**

Zilton Zalzalm dashed across the surface of the lava, his monk’s discipline letting him run over the searing liquid even as it scorched his legs. Amid the chaos, he shouted in draconic, a desperate plea to lure the dragon toward easier prey in Hell itself, though the creature could not understand. As he scooped up Dot’s limp body and retreated behind a shattered wall, he barked a more urgent realization in common: the engine in the dragon’s chest could be sabotaged with water. Meanwhile, Na'Hal voiced incredulous disbelief that anyone would try to negotiate, but Zilton Zalzalm's stubborn determination held firm, even when Na'Hal accused him of killing his own dog. Cradling the inert construct, Zilton Zalzalm worked swiftly to stabilize Dot, preventing the dog’s complete destruction.

3. **Fright and the Waterborne Gamble**

Fear gripped many of them; the dragon’s presence rooted Zilton Zalzalm, Fel, and Sabrina Liaro in place. As the creature crashed through stone walls, the group weighed flight against fight. Zilton Zalzalm focused his perception and realized that this was no true red dragon but a black dragon corrupted by the engine, and water would be devastating to its machinery. Scarlet bolstered Zilton Zalzalm's courage from afar, and Na'Hal readied a banishing strike while the others provided cover. In the hallway, Fel cast a grey fear cantrip toward the beast, but the dragon shook off the compulsion with sheer will, draining its legendary Fortitude.

4. **Skip’s Torrential Gambit**

Skip Leafing, the unassuming automaton manager, stepped forward with his bag of holding. He had filled it to the brim with fresh water long ago, and now, hearing the call to waterlog the engine, he inverted the bag. A colossal wave erupted, a tsunami that barreled down the passage and collided with the dragon and the blazing floor. Steam billowed in a violent hiss as lava turned to obsidian, and the water crashed against the engine, wracking the dragon with triple-seared damage. Two fiery elementals that had risen from the lava were snuffed out to slag and twisted armor. Skip Leafing himself was nearly swept away, but Sabrina Liaro's steadying presence helped him keep his footing. The waterfall’s wake left the chamber transformed, the molten field quenched and the dragon struggling amid the flood.

5. **Nahal’s Vanishing Smite**

As the wave receded, the battered dragon crawled out of the steam, its engine glowing an angry red. Na'Hal saw his moment. With Scarlet's encouraging help, he charged forward, maul crackling with black lightning. The first swing missed, but the second connected squarely with the engine housing. The banishment smite flared, bending the metal inward until the engine choked on its own fire. A geyser of smoke and ash erupted as the dragon was ripped from the world, vanishing in a puff of black haze. Na'Hal, boasted that he was the literal goat of the party, a champion of vanishing and banishing one foe after another. The hallway fell silent save for the hiss of cooling stone.

6. **The Spirit of Death Turns**

The quiet shattered when Fel's own summoned spirit of death, a spectral reaper he had conjured earlier, twisted against its master. It intoned that Charon wanted to reclaim its souls, then glided through Na'Hal with a scythe that cut at his very soul. Stunned, Fel felt a resigned despair that the gods were angry with him. He attempted to frighten both the spirit and the dragon with grey fear, but the spirit resisted and the dragon spent its last legendary resistance to shrug it off. In desperation, Fel cast another spirit of death, hoping they would fight each other, but the new wraith likewise turned its gaze on him. Now two grim specters stood between the party and the exit, their forms wavering with malevolent intent.

7. **Fell’s Rally and Dot’s Revival**

Trapped and frightened, Fel dug deep. He abandoned his earlier bravado of being death in its purest form and instead wove a motivational speech that infused Zilton Zalzalm, Na'Hal, Solace Forger, and the fallen Dot with a rush of temporary vitality. Dot’s optics flickered to life, the mechanical dog rising on shaky legs with a burst of borrowed health. The speech also granted clarity against the lingering fear, though both Fel and Zilton Zalzalm still struggled to shake the dragon’s awe. With Dot mobile again, the party gained an ally, but the reaper continued to press forward, slicing into Fel with necrotic precision while the second spirit menaced the others.

8. **Zilton Fights Free**

Zilton Zalzalm centered himself, using the stillness of his mind to purge the frightened condition. No longer paralyzed by dread, he launched an assault against the spirit nearest him. A punch and a slash of his tsurugi weakened the wraith, but maneuvering through the tight space proved difficult; he accidentally clipped Solace Forger with a flying fist as he dodged between allies. Despite the mishap, he retreated to a safer position, ready to continue the brawl. Sabrina Liaro added crossbow bolts into the fray, while Scarlet picked up a fallen rapier and switched to ranged attacks, each shot chipping away at the spectral forms.

9. **The Spirits Broken**

Solace Forger swung her warhammer with a cleric’s conviction, and Dot clamped his metal jaws onto a spirit, grappling its vaporous body so it could not flee. Scarlet peppered the creatures, and Sabrina Liaro's paladin healing hands soothed Fel's injuries, though the tiefling managed only a single crossbow shot before turning to aid. Finally, Fel raised the maul high and declared he was going to kill death itself. The weapon descended, and the reaper imploded, its essence sucked into the maul’s head with a soft hum. An unexpected charge thrummed within the weapon, a new power Fel did not yet understand. The second spirit soon followed, blasted apart by combined attacks and dispersing into mist.

10. **A Light Beyond the Ruins**

As the last echo of combat faded, the party took stock. The tsunami and the dragon’s banishment had extinguished every pool of lava in the cave, hardening the floor into a jagged plain of obsidian. The wall behind the former lava pit had crumbled, loose stones washed aside by the flood, revealing a narrow passage. At its far end, a soft light glimmered, a beacon that promised a way forward. Dot stood repaired and alert, Skip Leafing tucked his empty bag away with a weary grin, and the group, battered but alive, looked toward that distant glow as their next step on the road to Hell’s gate.
