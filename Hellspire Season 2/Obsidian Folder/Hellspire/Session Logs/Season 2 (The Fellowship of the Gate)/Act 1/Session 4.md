## Saga20:
The air around Fort Lake Gap was thick with the acrid tang of the acid lake, its green fumes drifting lazily over the crumbling battlements. Three survivors stood before **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] and her retinue of knights, having been plucked from the desolate wasteland that stretched south toward the Gate to Hell. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] , the dwarf whose soul had been thrust into a new body, [[Zilton Zalzalm]] , the robotic warrior from another realm, and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] , the tiefling whose form concealed the twin imps [[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]] , had barely a moment to catch their breath before the queen's scrutiny fell upon them. What began as a routine screening with a divine amulet quickly spiraled into a delve through a fort overrun by demonic taint, where the party proved their mettle against a vicious Barlgura and a sinister ooze that wore the faces of the dead. By the time the dust settled, they had uncovered a captain's desperate journal, a cache of firearms, and the grim knowledge that even the gods were not safe from the hells' corruption. The session closed with an invitation to the distant haven of New Friea, a promise of refuge that carried the weight of all they had lost and all they still hoped to save.

### Key Events

1. **The Queen's Test**

The party stood in the shadow of the fort's outer wall, the massive emerald dragon [[Ig]] coiled nearby like a living hillock. **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] approached with a guarded demeanor, her knights fanning out in a protective semicircle. She held aloft an amulet that pulsed with a pale light, explaining it was a standard precaution to root out hidden devils. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] recognized the spell as a detection of good and evil, and a cold certainty settled in his gut: the magic should have exposed [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] for the fiend he truly was. Yet as the light washed over them, the amulet glowed a serene green, signaling all was well. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] felt a subtle, manipulative presence emanating from the sword on his back, a dark intelligence that had twisted the spell's result to protect its wielder. The queen, satisfied, granted the trio permission to join the exploration of the fort, assigning her captain [[Sabrina Liaro]] to accompany them and ensure they stayed out of trouble.

2. **The Barlgura's Ambush**

The fort's interior was a maze of decay: a former stable, a training hall littered with broken dummies, and a gaping hole in the ceiling where rubble had collapsed from the floor above. As [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] crept toward a portcullis that led to an armory, a hulking shape dropped from the darkness overhead. The creature was a Barlgura, a simian demon with knotted muscles and a feral hunger, its claws raking across [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s shoulder and its fist slamming into [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] with bone-jarring force. The attack was sudden and brutal, but the party's response was immediate. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], reeling from the blow, raised his violin and began a mournful dirge that coalesced into a spectral reaper, a hooded embodiment of death that rose from the floor in a swirl of black smoke. The spirit's scythe bit deep into the demon's flesh, necrotic energy seeping into the wounds.

3. **Zoltan's Flurry and the Demon's Flight**

[[Zilton Zalzalm]] surged forward, his surugi blade flashing in the dim light. With the precision of a master duelist, he drove the rapier into the Barlgura's abdomen, then followed with a pair of iron-hard punches that cracked against its hide. The demon, bloodied and overwhelmed, shimmered and vanished from sight, its invisibility a desperate gambit. The reaper, attuned to the creature's presence, sensed it still on the same plane, roughly forty feet away on the floor above. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], his keen ears catching the thud of a landing on wooden planks, sprinted up the rubble pile and, with a surge of athletic prowess, launched himself through the hole to the upper level. Blinded by the creature's concealment, he channeled his warlock patron's power and loosed a pair of eldritch blasts. One beam struck true, punching a hole through the demon's chest and breaking its concentration. The Barlgura flickered back into view, collapsed, and lay still.

4. **A Divided Search**

With the immediate threat ended, [[Sabrina Liaro]] gathered the other knights, who filtered into the room with a mixture of awe and relief. The party split to cover more ground: [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] remained below, attempting to lift the heavy portcullis to the armory but finding it stubbornly locked, while [[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] explored the upper reaches. [[Zilton Zalzalm]], his warforged body allowing him to run vertically along walls, scaled the fort's inner face and discovered a hatch on the rooftop. He smashed it open with a single, powerful stomp and descended into a long gallery lined with cannons, their muzzles aimed through archer slits at the acid lake beyond. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], unwilling to be left behind, took a more destructive route: he summoned his maul and battered a hole through the stone wall, stepping through the dust to rejoin his companion. Together they noted the harpoon cannon and the eerie green glow of the lake, then found a staircase spiraling downward.

5. **The Queen's Revelation**

While the others were above, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] lingered in the training hall as **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] approached. She spoke of her kingdom's routine patrols for survivors, rarely venturing so close to the Gate of Hell, but explained that [[Ig]], her dragon companion, had been plagued by a persistent feeling about an old friend: a plasmoid named [[Malm]]. The name stirred [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s memory; he recalled the journal pages he had found in the cave, which mentioned a plasmoid matching that description. He kept the knowledge close, simply assuring the queen he would alert her if he saw any sign of a blue, gooey humanoid. The queen's gratitude was tempered by a warrior's stoicism, and she instructed him to continue searching while she checked on her other knights.

6. **The Captain's Journal**

[[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] entered a room that had once served as an office and living quarters, now thick with dust and the smell of old parchment. On a desk, half-buried under scattered papers, they found a journal belonging to Captain [[Leo Soraia]]. The entries painted a grim picture: scouts reporting flying ships and abominations destroying Loot Bridge City , a massive earthquake that felt unnaturally ominous, the confirmation that the hells had been unleashed, and the desperate decision to lock the dungeon after a prisoner murdered a guard and slaughtered his fellow inmates. The final, blood-spattered page bore a single, frantic line: "Holy Marduk has been slaughtered." [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] recognized the implication immediately: a god had been killed. The journal also contained a key, which they pocketed before [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] also studied a regional map pinned to the wall, committing its details to memory.

7. **The Ooze Revealed**

A knight's shout drew the party and the queen to a cramped chamber near the armory. The man claimed to have found something behind a large keg, but [[Zilton Zalzalm]]'s sharp eyes caught a far more sinister detail: thin, glistening strands of blue goo connected the knight to the wall and to the shadows beyond. The strands were almost invisible, like the remnants of a spider's web, but they pulsed with a faint, wet life. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] attempted to cut the tether with his double-bladed sword, and the moment the strand was severed, the knight collapsed into a puddle of azure slime. The puddle began to slither backward, drawn toward the keg, and from behind it rose a churning mass of ooze, its surface bubbling with half-formed faces and grasping limbs. This was a **Oblex**, a creature of stolen identities and psychic hunger.

8. **The Battle Against the Oblex**

**Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] was the first to react, charging around the barrel and plunging her blade into the ooze's shifting bulk. The creature retaliated with a lashing pseudopod that slammed into [[Zilton Zalzalm]], dealing a heavy blow and a spike of psychic agony, but the warforged's constructed mind shrugged off the mental assault. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] answered with his surugi, stabbing deep into the gelatinous flesh, and then, with a flourish, he unleashed a critical strike that carved through the creature's core. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], standing at the back, played a rousing countercharm on his violin, bolstering his allies against the ooze's frightening presence, and granted [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] a mote of bardic inspiration. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] fired eldritch blasts from the doorway, but the shots went wide. The ooze, desperate, tried to dominate their minds with a psychic wave, but [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s music held firm, and every ally resisted. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] then delivered the final sequence: a flurry of slashes that scattered the ooze across the walls, the faces within it screaming silently before dissolving into inert goo. A second knight, also tethered, collapsed as the connection broke.

9. **A Queen's Grief**

In the aftermath, **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] stared at the puddles that had been her men, her expression a mask of weary sorrow. She had lost knights before, and the weight of those losses was etched into the lines of her face. She thanked the party for their aid, acknowledging that their skills had proven invaluable, and announced that she would withdraw with [[Ig]] to wait outside. [[Sabrina Liaro]] would remain with them if they wished to continue exploring, and when they were ready, they would be escorted to New Friea, the kingdom that now sheltered the survivors of the hells' incursion. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] attempted to offer comfort, his words clumsy but sincere, but the queen responded with a cold, distant look before striding away. The dwarf was left to wonder if his charm had failed or if the queen simply had no room left for softness.

10. **The Armory's Bounty**

The key from the journal did not fit the portcullis to the armory, but it did unlock a side door that led to a staircase descending into the fort's lower levels. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], ever resourceful, waited until [[Sabrina Liaro]] was distracted by [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s conversation, then split into his two imp forms. [[Nampha]] cast a spell while [[Andhal]] transformed into a rat, scurrying through the bars of the portcullis and reverting to his true shape on the other side. Together, the imps managed to open the gate from within, granting the party access to a trove of weaponry: two flintlock pistols, a musket, and a pair of small cannons that could be carried by one person at a slow pace or two at full speed. The discovery was a welcome boon, a tangible reward amid the fort's tragedies.

11. **Whispers of Home**

As the group regrouped and prepared to leave, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] continued his conversation with [[Sabrina Liaro]], asking where she hailed from in this hell-scarred world. She spoke of New Friea, a name that carried a flicker of hope, a place where the remnants of civilization had gathered. The session drew to a close with that promise hanging in the air, the party standing at the threshold of a new destination, their packs heavier with weapons.
