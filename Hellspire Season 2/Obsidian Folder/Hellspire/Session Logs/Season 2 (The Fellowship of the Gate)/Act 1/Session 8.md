## Saga20:
The morning in New Freia began with fractured dreams and a divine tremor that passed through the waking world. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] stirred from a nightmare where his original body, still imprisoned in a hellish dungeon, accused him of thievery and demanded he leave. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] had spent the night reading the ancient tome of dragon history, unlocking new revelations about the draconic gods and a cryptic map of chromatic territories. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], composed of the two imps [[Nampha]] and [[Andhal]], had dreamt not of Al but of a spire in Avernus, where a sentient sword in a temple of flame demanded their loyalty and severed their wings, binding their souls to darkness. Upon waking, a surge of holy energy rippled through the party, a message from a celestial source that [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] alone managed to perceive and record: a series of cryptic phrases including references to six souls and a warning signed by something called The Good One . The day ahead tested their fledgling garrison, their tolerance for the hapless inventor [[Skip Leafing]], and their ability to navigate the delicate politics of faith and fiendish blood.

### Key Events

1. **The Awakening and the Divine Message**

The party rose from their beds at Cats **Tavern** with the weight of the previous night still clinging to them. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] had not slept at all, instead devouring the ancient book of draconic lore and committing every restored page to his eidetic memory. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] shook off the lingering dread of his dream, the voice of [[Aji Skipplestump|Aji]] still echoing accusations from a dungeon cell. The three companions converged in the tavern below, but before they could plan their day, a wave of energy washed over them, a presence that felt both foreign and oddly comforting. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] alone caught the full force of it, a burning message etched behind his eyes that he hastily transcribed onto paper. Though the others felt only a fleeting rush they mistook for waking jitters, the imp duo now carried a decoded missive from The Good One, hinting at six souls and a purpose they did not yet understand.

2. **The Lore of the Draconic Gods**

[[Zilton Zalzalm]] shared his night's discoveries with the group, his voice resonating with the enthusiasm of a scholar who had pieced together a fractured history. The book described how the five great dragon worms had merged their essences into three draconic gods: Bahamut of the metallic dragons, Sardior of the gemstone dragons, and Tiamat of the chromatic dragons. A thousand-year war raged between them, with the gemstone faction retreating into hiding while the conflict reshaped the world. The chromatic god had vanished after a climactic battle, and the metallic god ascended to the Seven Heavens of Mount Celestia, leaving behind ambient magic that accelerated the development of sentient civilization. Most critically, [[Zilton Zalzalm]] studied a restored page that had once been blacked out, revealing a map of chromatic territories: a desert of ice, a swamp of acid, an island of hell, an overgrown jungle, and hills of sand, all encircling a mysterious central symbol of three overlapping necklaces.

3. **The Persistence of** [[Skip Leafing]]

Descending into the tavern, the party was met by the inevitable sight of [[Skip Leafing]] standing at the foot of the stairs, his oversized backpack hunching his frame as he saluted with desperate enthusiasm. The half-elf inventor had lost his job the previous day after gambling with the party and was now fully committed to joining their garrison. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] attempted to gaslight him, to persuade him they were not the people he had met before, but the effort only deepened [[Skip Leafing]]'s insecurity rather than dissuading him. He presented a homemade weapon, a socket wrench lashed to two kitchen knives that spun like a crude fan, and when [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] barked at him to put it away, he folded it into a towel with such sorrow that even the sternest heart might crack. The party debated his fate in hushed tones, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] suggesting they confine him to a workshop to build inventions for them, while [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] floated the idea that perhaps he should try to reclaim his estranged wife instead.

4. **The Forge and the Memorial**

The party split temporarily, with [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] visiting the Tunnel Hall Smithy to retrieve the plate armor he had commissioned the previous day. The blacksmith, a dwarven woman drinking a dark morning brew, presented the gleaming steel and sent him on his way. Meanwhile, [[Zilton Zalzalm]] visited the Freia Memorial, studying the birth and death dates of fallen heroes inscribed upon the stone, tracing the scars of the kingdom's history and finding names he recognized from tales told in passing. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], left without access to the Queen's token, wandered the market district in frustration, finding only luxury goods and trinkets meant for the upper class, a bitter reminder of his dependence on the token held by [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]].

5. **The** Church of the Watcher **and** [[Solace Forger]]

[[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] entered the Church of the Watcher just as the morning sermon began, the sanctuary filled with stained glass depictions of disembodied hands lifting toward a celestial mass of eyes and wings. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], following behind, found himself physically paralyzed at the threshold, his fiendish nature rebelling against the hallowed ground, and was forced to retreat to the market instead. As the sermon concluded and the congregation drank blessed water with lemon, the two party members approached the clergy. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] asked the pastor about unicorns and was directed toward the library in Gemstone City. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] was recognized by a dwarven woman named [[Solace Forger]], who claimed to have seen his face in the church over thirty years ago, before the invasion and the fall of the old world. When [[Solace Forger]] demonstrated her divine gifts by casting a mending spell upon [[Zilton Zalzalm]]'s ancient book, she accomplished what no one else could, restoring the scratched-out names of the draconic gods and unblacking the crucial page. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] offered her a place in the royal garrison, and she accepted, donning full plate armor and preparing to meet them within the hour.

6. **The Armor and the Modular Solution**

Returning to Cats **Tavern**, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] attempted to don his new plate armor and immediately discovered a critical flaw, his physical strength was insufficient to wear such heavy protection without a crippling reduction in speed. Frustration mounted until a closer inspection revealed a modular design built into the armor by the skilled blacksmith. By removing certain components, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] was able to reconfigure the armor into half plate, sacrificing some protection for mobility but preserving his agility in combat. He emerged wearing the adjusted gear, his discomfort palpable but his survivability improved, just in time to reunite with [[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]].

7. **The Guard House and** Captain [[Sabrina Liaro]]

The three companions, now joined by the enthusiastic [[Skip Leafing]] and the newly recruited [[Solace Forger]], made their way to the guard house to seek additional recruits. [[Zilton Zalzalm]]'s blunt introduction, announcing himself as a being from another dimension here to close the Gate to Hell, earned bewildered stares from the desk guards. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] intervened with the Queen's token, and they were directed upstairs to **Captain** [[Sabrina Liaro]], who greeted them warmly. She could not spare any of her current garrison, still recovering from losses at Fort Lake Gap, but offered herself as a potential recruit if given three days to train replacements. She confirmed that Gemstone City lay beyond a waypoint and a long tunnel through the mountain, a journey of several hours on foot. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] attempted humor, requesting a tall glass of water despite his inability to drink, and his mechanical laughter sent the party into such a fit of secondhand embarrassment that they retreated from the guard house entirely.

8. **The Road to** Gemstone City

The party set out southward, leaving the safety of New Freia and crossing into the white and yellow sands that marked the wastes. [[Solace Forger]] served as guide, having traveled this faint path before, and the group settled into the rhythm of a four-hour march through untamed terrain dotted with red rock and dead trees. During the journey, [[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] shared what they had learned at the church, while [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] revealed the decoded message from The Good One, its mention of six souls casting an uncomfortable shadow over their party of six companions. They reached the waypoint at the mountain's side, a fortified gate with massive stone doors and a queue of travelers awaiting inspection, and bypassed the line by invoking the Queen's authority and the urgency of their mission.

9. **The Potion Shop of** [[Frink]]

Entering Gemstone City through a tunnel illuminated by glowing amethyst crystals, the party emerged onto streets paved with the same purple stone, the city cascading up a hillside in a display of luxury and order. Their first stop was a shop called Potions Oh My, where they met an eccentric proprietor named [[Frink]], a man with two painted purple peg legs who moved with a theatrical zest. [[Frink]] recounted the loss of his legs during a mind flayer invasion years ago and spoke of a lingering fear that his early potion sales might have inadvertently supplied the warlocks who opened the Gate to Hell. The party placed a substantial order for healing potions, invisibility potions, potions of heroism, and even a request for a concoction that might allow [[Zilton Zalzalm]] to peer into other dimensions. [[Frink]] agreed to craft the items over the coming days, warmed by their royal credentials and their shared goal.

10. **The Blind Beggar**

As the party made their way toward the city's keep, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s sharp eyes caught a peculiar figure seated at the end of the amethyst street. A [[Leolar Spirit|blind man]] knelt in the thoroughfare, his empty sockets weeping blood down his cheeks as he begged not for food or shelter, but for coin, insisting he had special uses for it in a city where currency bore little meaning. [[Skip Leafing]] offered a standard gold piece, but the beggar refused it, turning his head with uncanny precision toward [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] and requesting a different kind of currency entirely. The demand for a soul coin, a cursed currency of the Nine Hells, struck the imp duo like a blade. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] gave the man ten seconds to flee, his voice cold with recognition of the danger, and the beggar vanished the moment the party turned to walk away. As they climbed the hill toward the keep, the question of who and what that creature was lingered like a stain on the otherwise gleaming city.
