## Saga20
The fellowship, battered but resolute after uncovering the horrors beneath Fort Lake Gap, took a brief respite in the courtyard. Their rest was shattered by the emergence of a large stomach devil from the acid lake, forcing a desperate battle atop the crumbling walls. Through a combination of spectral intervention and a mighty banishment, the creature was vanquished, but the victory revealed deeper mysteries: a vein of hellish stone weeping blue blood, and a vision that promised a path forward. They needed the horn of a unicorn and the blood of a stag. With The Engine of Nessus rampaging in the distance, the party took flight on the emerald dragon [[Ig]], salvaging what they could from his doomed lair before setting course for the sanctuary of New Freia.

### Key Events

1. **A Queen's Questions and a Troubled Rest**

The courtyard of Fort Lake Gap offered little comfort, but the party settled among the rubble to catch their breath. **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] approached [[Zilton Zalzalm]] and [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], her tone a mixture of royal authority and genuine curiosity. She probed [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] about his construct nature, marveling at how human he seemed despite his mechanical origins, and asked if he possessed feelings. [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] replied that he felt a powerful drive to close the Gate to Hell, which she took as a sufficient answer. The conversation shifted to [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]], whose fragmented memories of an elvish city in the Evergreen Kingdom and a lost soul in the Nine Hells only deepened the queen's wariness. As the exchange grew tense, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] suddenly reeled, hearing in his mind the distant clang of a cell door, an echo of his original body's imprisonment, before the sound was replaced by a rhythmic, earth-shaking stomp.

2. **The Stomach Devil Rises**

Separated from the group, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] had slipped away to commune with his patron, seeking guidance on whether to reveal his fiendish nature. The answer was stern: remain hidden, for revealing himself to his companions had already been a clumsy mistake. As the message faded, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] spotted movement in the acid lake. A massive, grotesque devil hauled itself from the corrosive depths, its many-layered stomach pulsating as it locked eyes with him and snarled about worms in its nest. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] darted away, scrambling up through a hatch to the top of the wall, shouting a warning to the others. The creature scaled the fortifications with terrifying speed, its bulk looming over the battlements as the party scrambled to meet it.

3. **Battle on the Battlements**

[[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] acted first, weaving a motivational speech that bolstered his allies with temporary vigor and sharpened their resolve. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] dashed up the wall with supernatural agility, firing a crossbow bolt into the devil's shoulder before the creature retaliated with a spew of acidic mucus that seared flesh and corroded armor. Queen [[Layla Tenama]] leaped onto the devil's back, stabbing with her blade, while the knights below fired crossbows in vain. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], his hexblade's curse empowering his strikes, slammed his maul into the devil's knee with devastating force, but the creature's ribcage opened like a saw-toothed maw, grappling him and dragging him into its stomach. The situation grew dire as the devil bit down, inflicting necrotic wounds that threatened to consume [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s very soul.

4. **A Spirit's Gamble and a Banishment**

Sensing that [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]]'s essence would be trapped forever if he perished inside the devil, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] made a desperate choice. He summoned a reaper spirit directly into the creature's gullet, hoping to force [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] out. The arcane gamble succeeded beyond expectation: the spirit materialized inside the stomach, shoving [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] free and grappling the devil from within. With the tiefling safe, the dragon [[Ig]] swooped in, tearing at the devil with claws and teeth, while the queen continued her assault. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], now free and channeling all his remaining power, wound up his maul as crackling black energy enveloped it. With a tremendous upward swing, he struck the devil beneath the sternum, and the creature was hurled backward, fading into the void as the banishment took hold. The stomach devil was gone, but the cost was steep: [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] collapsed, unconscious and bleeding, until [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]]'s mass healing magic brought him back from the brink.

5. **A Dragon's Urgent Flight**

In the aftermath, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] slipped away to investigate the lingering arcane energy he had sensed in the dungeon. He found the abyssal sigil now dormant but still faintly active, and a vein of deep red obsidian-like stone protruding from the wall, weeping a thin stream of blue goop. When [[Ig]] descended to examine it, the dragon touched the stone and recoiled as if struck, his horns vibrating. He announced telepathically that he had seen the great machine, the **Engine of Nessus**, approaching his old lair, and that he must leave immediately. Without waiting for debate, [[Ig]] gathered the party onto his back and launched into the sky, teleporting in bursts to cover the impossible distance in under an hour. The flight was a blur of wind and crackling energy as the landscape streaked by below.

6. **Salvage in the Lair**

Landing in the familiar grassy alcove, the party was greeted by [[Ig]]'s draconic creature, which wagged its tail and yapped excitedly at its master's return. With The Engine of Nessus only minutes away, the group had three brief turns to grab whatever they could. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] made for the main chamber, searching for a dragon lore book and also snatching a newspaper clipping with the headline "King's Son - Turmoil" from a wall of clippings. [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] attempted to recall what [[Deyrale Dinglebottom|Daryl]]'s belongings were, but his memory failed him. Instead, he focused on retrieving more of [[Malm]]'s notes, securing whatever documents he could find. Meanwhile, [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], keeping his actions discreet, touched his sentient sword and asked his patron whether he should take the amulet belonging to the warlock [[Gabble Skipplestump]]. The patron warned that it could prove useful, but cautioned him to be more private. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]] dashed to grab the amulet, though his haste made the retrieval clumsy.

7. The Engine of Nessus **Unleashed**

As the party scrambled, [[Ig]] grabbed his creature and burrowed through the wall, pulling everyone out just as the **Engine of Nessus** strode into view. The colossal war machine, a six-legged walking fortress bristling with cannons, waded into the blood-red water and opened fire. Rows of cannons unleashed a relentless barrage, and the alcove collapsed in a cascade of rubble and dust. From the safety of [[Ig]]'s back, the party watched the destruction, the Engine's exhaust ports puffing icy clouds as it obliterated the lair. The dragon flew fast and deep, putting distance between them and the mechanical horror, while the little draconic creature panted happily, thrilled to be with its master again.

8. **The Stone and the Blue Goop**

Back at Fort Lake Gap, the party descended once more into the basement to examine the strange stone. [[Zilton Zalzalm]] studied the vein and noted that it resembled a root more than a simple ore deposit. [[Na' Hal|Na'Hal]], drawing on his infernal knowledge, realized this was a manifestation of the Hells' dominion, a root system that spread wherever the Nine Hells claimed territory. It connected directly to the portal and likely extended into any corrupted land. The blue goop, [[Ig]] confirmed telepathically, was blood, the blood of [[Malm]]. The plasmoid was somehow trapped within or behind this hellish stone, and the dragon's earlier attempt to break through had inflicted considerable pain. The party debated the implications, with [[Zilton Zalzalm|Zilton]] bluntly suggesting that [[Malm]] might be the gate itself, a theory that hung in the air unanswered.

9. **Fel's Vision of the Path**

Determined to find a way through, [[Fel Sinderheim|Fel]] placed his hand on the stone despite the risk. A searing migraine erupted behind his eyes, but within the pain came a vision. He saw the root coil back, retreating from a radiant light. In his hands, he held a long, spiraled unicorn horn, its coils slick with blood that flowed into a bladder pouch. As he stepped forward, the root engulfed him safely, parting before the combined power of the horn and the stag's blood. When he withdrew his hand, the stone was as solid as before, but the message was clear: to enter the root and perhaps rescue [[Malm]], they would need to find a unicorn's horn and the blood of a stag. The party shared this revelation, though none knew where such rare components might be found in the blighted land.

10. **Departure for** New Freia

With the mystery of the stone lingering and the **Engine of Nessus** still roaming, **Queen** [[Layla Tenama]] announced that the time had come to leave the fort. She intended to fly to New Freia, a sanctuary far more peaceful than the hell-scarred peninsula. The party agreed, eager for a true rest and a chance to research their next steps. [[Ig]] extended his tail, and the three companions grabbed hold, hoisting themselves onto his broad back. As the dragon took wing, the fort dwindled below, and the fellowship set their sights on a new destination, carrying with them the dragon book, the newspaper clipping, the amulet of [[Gabble Skipplestump]], and the urgent knowledge that a unicorn and a stag held the key to the roots of Hell.
