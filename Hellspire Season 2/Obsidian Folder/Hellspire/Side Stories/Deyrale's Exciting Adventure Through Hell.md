Heres the overview for DEATH:

Session 1:
- Deyrale starts in Malbolge, the sixth layer of hell. This is two years after the gate to hell opens. (7/13/1109) Specifically, he is in Ossiea, which in this campaign, is a colosseum of punishment, humiliation, and torture typical for devils who have disappointed their station, but can also be for lost souls, especially powerful ones. Deyrale has been here for some period between a few week to a few months, its hard to tell. Ossiea sits about 400-500 feet above the river styx. 
- He will start his gladiator battle, and at some period, he will notice a small ship passing underneath the mountain. He will have his opportunity to jump onto the ship then, where he will witness Aji being attacked as a stowaway.
- (DM worldbuilding: The ship is full of various magical and cursed books. The ship is headed to Nessus with a shipment of possible Demonomicons, as the forces of hell are )
- They may either take over the ship, or abandon it. Either way, he will meet Aji.

Session 2:
- This is set in 1120. Session 2 will be the end of Aji and Deyrale's adventure to find a way to locate the souls of Gabble and Myla Skipplestump.

Session 3:
- This is set in 1125. Session 3 will be the end of Deyrale's adventure of hunting down and killing Myla's patron, Archdevil Angmar.
