A creatures entirety is consistent of Mind, Body, and Soul. Each play a vital role in what make up the creature.

- Mind: The mind of a creature determine their sane thoughts and voice.

- A creature without complete and permanent access to their mind, may go insane and have their personality permanently altered.
- A creature without temporary access to their mind may spout nonsense or have another creature communicate through them.
- In both cases, if the soul is not removed, the original self will be forced to watch helplessly as another entity controls what they say, but will still have access to their body and will retain the planar destination that they would have had pre-mind takeover.

- Body: The body of a creature determine their actions.

- A creature without complete and permanent access to their body, may perform actions that go against their thoughts and dialogue.
- A creature that regains access from a temporary takeover will regain feeling of the body, potentially causing a sharp influx of massive pain.
- In both cases, if the mind is not removed, the creature can still communicate as itself while the body is performing actions. If the soul is not removed, the creature will be forced to watch helplessly as another entity controls what the body does, but will still retain the planar destination that they would have had pre-mind takeover.

- Soul: The core sense of self that the creature has.

- A creature without access to their soul may have difficulty noticing a difference of before and after loss of soul.
- A creature without access to their soul may experience voices and strong pushes for choices and actions. The creature will very rarely act against the controlling party of the soul.
- In both cases, if the mind and body is not removed, the creature will have most of the access to their life that they had before the loss of soul. However, their domain of death is no longer up for judgement. Whichever entity controls their soul, controls where the creature will go upon death and has complete control of whatever the soul does post mortem.
