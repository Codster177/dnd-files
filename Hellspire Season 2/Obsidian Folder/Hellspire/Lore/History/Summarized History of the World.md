Start of the Universe?: -1.5 million 

Prior to -50,000: On the celestial body of Al, there was no life besides nature (animals and plants) and the primordial greatwyrms. There was a greatwyrm for each of the 5 chromatic dragons, metallic dragons, and gem dragons, with each having dominion over a certain part of the world.

-50,000 <-> -49,000 (1,000 year Dragon War): Certain animals were becoming more suited for their environment, and were becoming smarter and more developed. There was conflicting opinions over this new life with the greatwyrms.

- The Chromatic dragons believed the intelligent life should be destroyed in order to protect - the planet.
- The Metallic dragons believed that the intelligent life would bring the planet further and that they should be protected from the Chromatic dragons.
- The Gem dragons straight up didn’t care, as they didn’t view the new developing life as either dangerous or good for the planet. They mostly viewed the new life either as cute, or as inhabiting land that they were about to put their gargantuan sized foot.

This dilemma caused for the Chromatic dragons to become outraged, invoking them to combine large parts of their souls into a five headed dragon god (later named Tiamat). This was foresaw by the rest of the dragons, as seeing into the future was not a difficult ability for these god-like dragons. This prompted the Metallic dragons to combine their souls as well, into the Platinum Dragon God (later named Bahamut). In fear that these god dragons would overpower the rest of the greatwyrms, the gem greatwyrms combined their souls as well into the Ruby Dragon God (later named Sardior).

Very shortly after Tiamats creation, she set out to destroy the developing civilizations only to be engaged by Bahamut in an everlasting battle. This battle would last 1000 years, and as Sardior did not have a stake in the fight, he set out to watch it in a Ruby Palace he created that would orbit the planet. When the battle finished, Sardior stayed in the Palace but eventually grew board and looked outwards to the rest of the multiverse, going on to travel it in his palace.

The Chromatic, Metallic, and Gem greatwyrms still remained, but drastically less powerful as most of their soul had been drained into the god dragon that they created. However, while these greatwyrms were less powerful, they still were godlike compared to any normal dragon that was later to come. The greatwyrms took refuge during the 1000 year battle that occurred between the god dragons, and in order to build up some of the power they had lost, each greatwyrms went on to create dragons which they hoped would grow to become as strong as themselves. Although there were other powerful dragons that did emerge throughout history, none ever became as powerful as the original, and never nearly as powerful as the original with their entire soul intact.

The battle ended with Bahamut victorious, as the developing life would eventually grow to recognize that Bahamut was fighting for them, and would eventually join his side. The defeated Tiamat was then banished to Avernus, the first layer of hell. After being chronically exhausted due to the battle, Bahamut also decided to rest as he believed not only would the saved creatures keep the newly liberated planet safe, but also that they would cause it to thrive. Bahamut then made his home in Lunia, the first layer of the Seven Heavens of Mount Celestia.

Watching Bahamut banish and imprison Tiamat in the first layer of hell was the first real exposure that many of these creatures had to magic, which would cause wizards to eventually seek out magic and try to learn, as well as other obtain magic through different sources.

-40,000: The creatures eventually become races of Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Halflings, and Gnomes, as well as a few other intelligent creatures. This is when early civilizations finally became stable, with farming and permanent settlements being fairly new.

-37,500: Creatures such as Dragonborn, Half-Elves, Half-Orcs, and others emerge as creatures reproduce with each other, as well as enough dragons reproducing with people that it became a stable race.

-35,000: Dragons form an large kingdom spanning a large part of the planet, ruled by a long line of golden dragons. This line of dragons is so few that it has been almost exclusively limited to this bloodline that rules over this kingdom.

-10,000: Written history becomes a thing, as well as many aspects of modern civilization. Religion becomes much more popular as stories, myths, legends, and teachings can now be written down and spread.

-5,000: The first cult of Tiamat emerges, and they begin to look for ways to summon her. The order of Bahamut also emerges, to combat them but also to worship Bahamut.

-4,900: The order of Sardior emerges under their own agenda, but also to worship Sardior.

-4,700: The cult of Tiamat is extremely close to being successful at summoning Tiamat, prompting a call for both paladins from both sides and dragons from both sides to aid in the destruction or protection of this ritual. The dragon kingdom breaks into a civil war that lays destruction over most of the dragon kingdom. The metallic dragons and paladins are almost not successful at stopping the summoning of Tiamat, until paladins that follow Sardior join them in order to fight for their planet and for their lives. The cultists are successfully stopped, but forces on all sides are drastically reduced.
