The brazier of hellish fire is a powerful artifact used in rituals.

It can be a catalyst to perform immensely powerful magic. It can be used casually to perform consistent feats of magic, or can be expedited to perform legendary magic. However, this item usually limits its magic to be geared towards magic regarding the nine hells, its realm of origin.

For instance, this can be used to summon a fire elemental or fiend once per day. As well as this, it can create a small gate to the nine hells once per month, which lasts 10 minutes.

When expedited to perform legendary magic, it could be used in a ritual to build a new being, or hold open a large portal to hell. One is only left to try - It happened, they opened a portal to hell. After the Portal to Hell was created with the Brazier, the Brazier was destroyed.

The Brazier of Hellish Fire held the soul of Baator inside of it, giving it it's immense strength. Baator's soul has been separated into several artifacts, with the destruction of each artifact giving him more power, and more authority over the artifacts his soul is inside.

**THE BRAZIER OF HELLISH FIRE**  
_Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement)_

The Brazier of Hellish Fire has the following properties:

_**Attuning to the Brazier.**_ When attuning, the creature must spend a full long rest burning their most prized possession in the fire. This brazier is capable of destroying anything that is not a magic item, as well as magic items of the rarity Rare or lower. If the creature's most prized possession cannot burn in the fire, it magically teleports to another place in the universe.

_**Determined Properties.**_ The Brazier of Hellish Fire has the following properties determined by the will of the universe:

- 3 minor beneficial properties
- 3 minor detrimental properties

Gained from expending the flame:

- 1 major beneficial property
- 2 major detrimental properties

These properties are as agreed with the DM, as I will help choose fitting properties to fit the use of this item. These properties are only active when the brazier is in sight and not blocked by full cover.

_**Mark of Hell.**_ After you expend the flame, you acquire a physical disfigurement as a hideous sign of your devotion to vile darkness. An evil rune might appear on your face, your eyes might become glossy black, or horns might sprout from your forehead. Or you might become wizened and hideous, lose all facial features, gain a forked tongue, or some other feature the DM chooses. The mark of darkness grants you advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to interact with evil creatures and Charisma (Intimidation) checks made to interact with non-evil creatures.

_**Command Devil.**_ While you are attuned to the brazier and within sight, you can use an action to cast the Dominate Monster spell on a devil target (save DC 18). You can't use this property again until the next dawn.

_**Expending the Flame.**_ While attuned to the brazier and within sight, you can use a full minute to expend the flame. This will cause the Mark of Hell to inflict the creature that expends it, and the creature will gain the major properties from the item. These beneficial properties are properties that will be instant or permanent, as after the flame is expended, the brazier cracks in half forming into a neatly decorated broken non-magical brazier.
