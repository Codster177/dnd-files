This season will be primarily focused on the conclusion event of last season: The Hell Invasion of Al

Asmodeus's Scheme:

Asmodeus has produced a plan to end the Blood War and enable the takeover of the natural multiverse.

- During the first year of the invasion onto Al, the fighting on Avernus suffered heavy losses and casualties as Asmodeus had diverted the forces of hell away from the Blood War. This has some consequences against hell, especially in the first layer. Perhaps now there are demons littered all throughout the first 5 or so layers of hell, perhaps something else.
- As the invasion reached the Evergreen Kingdom at Greenrim, strong devils sieged the University of the Rift. After hard fighting for the city and the university, the forces of hell take over the city and the University, including the Demonomicon, an artifact inside of the university.
- By the end of the second year of the invasion, the Demonomicon is taken by the forces of hell and is used to stabilize their front in the Blood War. The Blood War is now back to being "even" and the invasion of Al can continue. Still, Avernus and the layer of Hell will have consequences for its sacrifice over the two years of invasions from the Abyss.
- The forces of hell now begin to spread out evenly instead of directly towards their artifact goal, as they adopt a new goal: They now wish to steal the hearts of all chromatic greatwyrms in order to perform a strong ritual to have full control of the dragon god: Tiamat. With control of Tiamat, the nine hells will have the extra power to win the Blood war and take over the rest of the multiverse.
